Skyhaven is a floating town above the clouds: the village at the centre of the **Hub**, where you shop, bank, reforge and start slayer quests. You reach it through the portal on your Private Island. The village is a safe place. This page walks round it by area, with coordinates so you can find things on the map. The islands around it are on [The Hub](the-hub). North is the negative Z direction, and the Skyheart plaza is at 0, 0.

## The rules of the hub

- The Hub is its own dimension. Your Private Island and the public islands are separate from it.
- Everyone walks **60% faster** in the Hub, so getting from one merchant to the next is quick. The boost ends the moment you leave the Hub.
- In the village nobody can fight, break blocks, place blocks or take damage, and nobody gets hungry. The seven areas around it let you break what they are for (Wheat, Oak, Coal and so on, which grow back) and fight the mobs that live there. See [The Hub](the-hub).
- You can use doors, trapdoors, fence gates, buttons, levers and bells. Other blocks, such as chests, cannot be used.
- Fields cannot be trampled.
- If you fall off the island you land back at spawn.
- Block items, buckets and spawn eggs cannot be used here.

`/hub` brings you here from anywhere, after a few seconds of standing still. `/is` takes you to your Private Island.

## The Skyheart plaza

The plaza is the middle of the town. A golden beacon fountain rises under the floating **Skyheart**, a spinning amethyst crystal ringed with purple shards. Eight runestones with banners stand round the edge, between four avenues, with cherry planters and benches facing the fountain. Hidden lights keep it bright at night.

The plaza is where you arrive when you use `/hub`, a little south of the fountain. Hypixel's other Hub spots (the Museum, the Fashion Shop and so on) are not built yet.

## The north: the Crown Terrace

The avenue north of the plaza climbs to the raised **Crown Terrace**.

- **Skyhaven Bank**, with a copper dome. The Banker keeps your bank balance and pays you interest. See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).
- **The Emberforge**, where Rosetta sells armour sets and the Blacksmith reforges your weapons, armour and tools. See [Reforging](reforging).
- **The Mason's Guild**, where the Wool Weaver sells wool, glass and clay in every colour.
- **The windmill**, with turning sails, on the north-west edge. The Mine Merchant stands near it, selling ores, stone and pickaxes.

## The west: Greenhollow and Mistfall

Greenhollow is a working farm with fields, and the Farm Merchant sells crops and seeds there. Further west the **Mistfall** waterfall pours from the island onto a small pond islet below.

## The south-west: Timberline

**Timberline Lodge** sits among great trees. The Lumber Merchant buys logs and sells wood and axes.

## The south-east: the tavern

Just off the south avenue, on the lawn in front of the gazebo, stands the **brainrot podium**: statues of the three best brainrot collectors on gold, silver and copper pedestals, with their scores. See [Brainrots](brainrots#the-leaderboard).

The tavern, with a beer garden and a gazebo, is where the Bartender sells drinks. The build calls the building the Gilded Gull. A bridge from the beer garden leads to the Arcane Library.

## The east: the Bazaar Promenade and the Grand Bazaar

The east avenue is a tree-lined promenade with benches, flower planters, banner posts and a koi garden with a brook. It ends at the **Bazaar Gate**, a great arch with a hanging "GRAND BAZAAR" sign, and a wide dark-oak bridge leads from it to the **Grand Bazaar** islet.

The Grand Bazaar is a market. A 19 by 19 plaza with a bell tower fountain sits in the middle, and six merchant pavilions stand round it:

| Pavilion | Keeper | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Armoury | Weaponsmith | Swords and bows |
| Curiosities | Librarian | Experience Bottles and Books |
| Petals & Blooms | Alchemist | Brewing ingredients |
| The Pantry | Combat Merchant | What an adventurer needs |
| The Angler | Fishing Merchant | Rods, bait and fish |
| Slayer Lodge | Maddox the Slayer | Slayer quests. See [Slayers](slayers) |

There is a pond and dock east of the Angler.

## The Arcane Library

Seraphine Vael's enchanting hall stands on an islet to the south-east. Inside, a giant open book floats under a crystal. Talk to her to open the enchanting table. See [Enchanting](enchanting).

## The Hub gate

On its own islet to the south stands a great stone ring with a shimmering purple veil. A sign on it sends you home: walk through and you are on your Private Island. The portal on your island brings you back.

## Starwatch

Starwatch is a small observatory islet in the north-east, reached from the terrace. Right-click the telescope and you get a short stargazing show: fireworks burst into stars over the observatory, a field of sparkles fills the sky, and you get Night Vision for 15 seconds.

The first time you look through it you earn the **Stargazer** advancement and **500 coins**. Only one person can use the telescope at a time.

## Getting around

- The minimap in the top right shows the town and its NPCs. The map opens zoomed in when you are in the hub.
- Press **M** for the world map. Hover an icon to see who it is.
- Drop a waypoint on a spot you want to return to. See [Waypoints](waypoints).
- `/warp` takes you to the areas and islands you have already visited. See [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu#fast-travel).

> [!TIP]
> Not sure where an NPC is? Open the world map and look for their face. Hover to see their role.
