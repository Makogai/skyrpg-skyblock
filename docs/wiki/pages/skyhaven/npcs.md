There are **65** NPCs in the Hub and on the public islands. Between them they run every shop, the Bank, enchanting, reforging, slayer quests, the Fairy Soul exchange and the Deep Caverns' lifts, and a good number only stand around and talk. This page groups them by where you find them. Prices are on [NPC Shops](npc-shops).

## How NPCs work

- **Right-click** an NPC. Most open a screen: a shop, the Bank, the Blacksmith's anvil, the slayer screen. The ones that only talk greet you with a line.
- A small label under the name says what they do: **Shop**, **Bank**, **Reforge**, **Slayer Quests**, **Enchanting** or **Talk**.
- NPCs turn to look at you when you are within 8 blocks. They cannot be hurt.
- Every shopkeeper buys anything that has an NPC price: click it in the sell panel. See [NPC Shops](npc-shops).
- NPCs show up on the minimap and the world map with their face, name and role. Hover to read them.
