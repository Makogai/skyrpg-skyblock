**Pets** float at your shoulder and give stats. Every one of Hypixel SkyBlock's **87 pets** is in the game (Bee, Ender Dragon, Rock, Golden Dragon...), at the rarities Hypixel has them in, with their stats and abilities. A pet levels from **1 to 100** (the Golden Dragon to 200) as you gain skill XP.

## Getting a pet out

A pet is an item: a head with the pet's face. **Right-click it** to add it to your pets; if you have none out, it's summoned straight away.

**25 pets can be crafted** (Bat, Blaze, Chicken, Endermite, Horse, Ocelot, Pigman, Rabbit, Sheep, Silverfish, Skeleton, Spider, Wither Skeleton, Wolf, Zombie and more): find them in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book) by name. A crafted pet starts at level 1. Pets can also be traded on the [Auction House](auction-house).

Open the **Pets** menu from the [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu) or with `/pets`:

- The pet you have out is on the card at the top. **Put away** sends it back.
- Every other pet is in the grid below. **Click** one to summon it (the one out swaps places with it). **Right-click** one to take it back out as an item, to trade or sell.
- Hover a pet for everything about it.

The pet you have out floats beside your shoulder, for everyone to see.

## Levels and stats

A pet's name shows its level: **[Lvl 48] Bee**. Its stats and its abilities' numbers grow in a straight line from level 1 to level 100, and a rarer pet has more of them. Its tooltip shows them at its level, and the progress to the next level.

A rarer pet needs more XP for each level: a Legendary pet starts further up Hypixel's pet XP table than a Common one.

## Pet XP

Your summoned pet gets a share of all the skill XP you gain:

| XP you gain | What the pet gets |
| --- | --- |
| Its own skill (a Farming pet and Farming XP) | all of it |
| Its own skill, for a Mining or Fishing pet | one and a half times it |
| Alchemy or Enchanting | a twelfth |
| Any other skill | a quarter |

**Taming:** whatever your pet gets is also your Taming XP, and every Taming level gives your pets 1% more XP (Zoologist). See [Skills](skills-overview).

## Pet items

**Right-click a pet item** while a pet is out to give it to that pet. A pet holds one item; the one it held before comes back to you. Its tooltip shows **Held Item** and what it does. Hypixel's pet items work the way their text says:

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Stats | Big Teeth (+5 Crit Chance), Hardened Scales (+25 Defense), Sharpened Claws (+15 Crit Damage), Lucky Clover (+7 Magic Find), Saddle (+30 Speed), Spooky Cupcake (+30 Strength, +20 Speed) |
| More of the pet's own stats | Textbook (+100% of its Intelligence), Iron Claws (+40% of its Crit Damage and Crit Chance) |
| More pet XP | Skill Exp Boosts (+20% to +50% for one skill), All Skills Exp Boosts (+10% to +30% for all) |
| Rarity | Tier Boost: the pet's stats count as one rarity higher |

A few (Flying Pig, Quick Claw, Exp Share...) show their text but don't work yet.

## Kat

**Kat**, the pet sitter at a stall in the Hub's market, takes a pet **up one rarity**. Her menu lists your pets with what their next rarity costs: coins, sometimes items (Honey Dippers for a Bee, Summoning Eyes for an Ender Dragon), and a wait (from a minute to weeks, in real time, even while you're offline). Click a pet to give it to her; she looks after **one pet at a time**. When it's ready you get a message; open her menu and click it to take it back at its new rarity (it keeps its XP; its level follows the new rarity's table). All 216 of Hypixel's upgrades are there.

> [!NOTE]
> Not yet: pet abilities (they show with their numbers but don't work yet), Kat Flowers and Bouquets (to skip the wait), and the pets that come from drops, shops and events rather than recipes.
