The **Museum** collects Hypixel's **973 donations**: weapons, tools, accessories and **armor sets**, in eight categories (Combat, Farming, Mining, Fishing, Foraging, Dungeoneering, Hunting, Special). Open it with `/museum`.

## Donating

- The tabs show each category and how many you've donated. In a category, an item you **have with you** is outlined in gold and comes first: **click** it to donate it. Donated ones are green; ones you haven't found show **?**.
- An **armor set** is donated whole: bring every piece.
- A donation goes into the Museum, and the Museum keeps **that very item** (its reforge, enchantments and stars). You can **borrow it back** any time (see below).
- Each donation pays Hypixel's **SkyBlock XP** for it (1 to 20+; Special items pay nothing, and the Special tab holds 48 items).
- A better item also counts the ones it replaces: a **Refined Mithril Pickaxe** counts the Mithril, Bandaged and Fractured Mithril Pickaxes too, with their XP.

Hover a donation for its SkyBlock XP and its game stage (Starter to Master).

## Borrowing items back

Donated items aren't gone: click a **green** donation to **borrow it back** (you need a free inventory slot per piece; a set comes back piece by piece). It shows a blue arrow while it's on loan, and it **still counts** for your milestones and progress: only its value (see the Appraisal Service) drops while it's out. Bring it back and click it again to **return** it: the Museum takes the very items you hold then, so reforging or enchanting it while it's out is fine. A donation that only counted because you donated a better item has nothing to lend.

## Milestones

Every **100 SkyBlock XP** the Museum has paid is a **Museum Milestone**, up to 40 (4,000 XP). The bar under the tabs shows how far you are to the next one: hover it for what it gives, click it (or talk to the **Curator**, `/curator`) for the whole list.

Each milestone gives **+1% Bits Multiplier** and **+2% Bank interest** (your daily interest gets that much bigger: 24 milestones is +48%). Some give more:

| 3 | Deluxe Bank Upgrade, Museum Appraisal Service |
| 5 | Travel Scroll to Museum (an item) |
| 6 | Super Deluxe Bank Upgrade |
| 8 | The Hex |
| 10 | Dust the Fish (an item), Premier Bank Upgrades, Shen's Auction, the Flower Minion XII recipe |
| 12 | Collecting Top Hat Emblem |
| 14 | Luxurious Bank Upgrade |
| 18 | Palatial Bank Upgrade |
| 20 | Eon the Fish (an item), Shen's Special |

The Curator hands over the items (**Claim**, once per milestone). The Bank interest works now; the Bank upgrades, the Hex, Shen's Auction, the emblems, the recipe and the Bits Multiplier are listed as unlocked, for the day those systems are in.

## The Curator and Madame Goldsworth

Both stand in the Hub's market, next to Kat. The **Curator** shows your Museum XP, the 40 milestones with their rewards and a **Claim** button. **Madame Eleanor Q. Goldsworth III** opens your Museum and sells the **Museum Appraisal Service**: from Milestone 3, pay **10,000,000 coins once** and she tells you your Museum's value. Here that's what an NPC would pay for the items on display (Hypixel uses market prices); borrowed items don't count while they're out. She also shows how many of the 48 **Special** items you've put on display.
