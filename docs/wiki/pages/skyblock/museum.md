The **Museum** collects Hypixel's **973 donations**: weapons, tools, accessories and **armor sets**, in eight categories (Combat, Farming, Mining, Fishing, Foraging, Dungeoneering, Hunting, Special). Open it with `/museum`.

## Donating

- The tabs show each category and how many you've donated. In a category, an item you **have with you** is outlined in gold and comes first: **click** it to donate it. Donated ones are green; ones you haven't found show **?**.
- An **armor set** is donated whole: bring every piece.
- A donation goes to the Museum **for good** (it's taken from your inventory).
- Each donation pays Hypixel's **SkyBlock XP** for it (1 to 20+; Special items pay nothing but count).
- A better item also counts the ones it replaces: a **Refined Mithril Pickaxe** counts the Mithril, Bandaged and Fractured Mithril Pickaxes too, with their XP.

Hover a donation for its SkyBlock XP and its game stage (Starter to Master).
