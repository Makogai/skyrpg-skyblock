Your **SkyBlock Level** is the number in brackets next to your name in chat and in the tab list. It goes up as you play: every **100 SkyBlock XP** is a level, as on Hypixel. Open the SkyBlock Menu and choose **SkyBlock Leveling** (or type `/sblevel`) to see your level, the bar to the next one, what your levels give you, the features they open up, the prefix colours and where SkyBlock XP comes from.

## Earning SkyBlock XP: the tasks

SkyBlock XP comes from **tasks**, as on Hypixel. Click **Earning SkyBlock XP** on the Leveling page (or type `/sblevel tasks`) for every task in its category, with how much each has given you against the most it can give:

| Category | Its tasks (the most XP) |
| --- | --- |
| Core | skill levels (8,710), Museum progression (3,646), Fairy Souls (570), the Accessory Bag's Magical Power (2,184), Pet Score (1,554), collections (3,172), unique minions (3,164), warps unlocked (360), bank upgrades |
| Event | Mining Fiesta ores, Fishing Festival sharks, Spooky Festival brackets, Chocolate Factory prestiges, Mythic and Divine rabbits, gold medals in Jacob's contests, the festivals' perk shops |
| Dungeon | Catacombs levels (1,220), class levels (+4 each, 1,000), the first clear of each floor (190) and Master Mode floor (350) |
| Essence Shop | every perk tier bought (+2 to +10), shop by shop |
| Slaying | slayer levels (3,825), the Bestiary (5,635), the first kill of each slayer tier and dragon, Kuudra and Arachne tiers, Mythological Creatures |
| Skill Related | Heart of the Mountain tiers and the Peak, powder earned, Commission milestones, Crystal Nucleus runs, fossils, the Garden (its level, visitors, plots, crop upgrades and milestones), Trophy Fish |
| Miscellaneous | Timecharms, Accessory Powers and bag upgrades, the Dojo, faction reputation, and more |
| Story, Consumables | objectives, the Rift Guide; items that give XP once used |

Some tasks belong to features that aren't in yet; they're listed anyway, at 0. Every gain shows above your hotbar ("+4 SkyBlock XP (54/100)").

## Emblems

An **emblem** is a symbol after your name in chat (hover it to see what it is). Pick one from the **Emblems** card in the tasks menu:

- **SkyBlock Levels**: Diamond ♦ at 10, Spade ♠ at 20, Heart ❤ at 30 ... Star ⚝ at 80, Jerry ꈔ at 100 ... the Golden Fleur De Lis ⚜ at 500.
- **Skills**: Mining Pickaxe ⸕, Farming Flower ✿, Combat Explosion ❁, Foraging Leaf ⸙, Fishing Fish α, Enchanting Pencil ✎, Alchemy Brew ☕, Carpentry House ☖, Taming Clover ♣ at level 50, and a gold "Master" version at 60 for some; Social ℻ at 15 and 25.
- **The Catacombs**: Catacombs Swords ⚔ at Catacombs XL (gold at L), each class's emblem at XL and L, and Dungeon Runner ☠ for a Floor VII clear.

## What levels give

- **+5 Health** every level.
- **+1 Strength** every 5 levels.
- A new **prefix colour** every 40 levels: grey, then white at 40, yellow at 80, green at 120, dark green at 160, aqua at 200, cyan at 240, blue at 280, pink at 320, purple at 360, gold at 400, red at 440 and dark red at 480.

A level up shows as one panel in chat with everything it gives.

## Features

| Level | Opens |

| 3 | The Community Shop |
| 5 | The Garden, and the [Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe) |
| 6 | Auto-pickup of block and mob drops |
| 7 | The Bazaar |

| 50 | A daily coins trading limit of 1B |

The Wardrobe is the first of these that's locked here; the others come with their features.
