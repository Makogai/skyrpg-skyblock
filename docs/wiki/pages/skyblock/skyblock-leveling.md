Your **SkyBlock Level** is the number in brackets next to your name in chat and in the tab list. It goes up as you play: every **100 SkyBlock XP** is a level, as on Hypixel. Open the SkyBlock Menu and choose **SkyBlock Leveling** (or type `/sblevel`) to see your level, the bar to the next one, what your levels give you, the features they open up, the prefix colours and where SkyBlock XP comes from.

## Earning SkyBlock XP

| What | SkyBlock XP |
| --- | --- |
| A [skill](skills-overview) level | 5 (levels 1 to 10), 10 (11 to 25), 20 (26 to 50), 30 (51 to 60) |
| A [collection](collections) tier | 4 |
| A new unique [minion](minions) | 1 (tiers I to VI), 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, 24 (tier XII) |

Fairy Souls, slayers, the bestiary and more come later. Every gain shows above your hotbar ("+4 SkyBlock XP (54/100)").

## What levels give

- **+5 Health** every level.
- **+1 Strength** every 5 levels.
- A new **prefix colour** every 40 levels: grey, then white at 40, yellow at 80, green at 120, dark green at 160, aqua at 200, cyan at 240, blue at 280, pink at 320, purple at 360, gold at 400, red at 440 and dark red at 480.

A level up shows as one panel in chat with everything it gives.

## Features

| Level | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | The Community Shop |
| 5 | The Garden, and the [Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe) |
| 6 | Auto-pickup of block and mob drops |
| 7 | The Bazaar |
| 12 | The Wizard Portal |
| 50 | A daily coins trading limit of 1B |
| 100 | A daily coins trading limit of 10B |

The Wardrobe is the first of these that's locked here; the others come with their features.
