A **minion** is a little helper you place on your island. It works the ground around it and fills its storage with what it gathers, even while you're offline. There are **61** kinds, from the Cobblestone Minion to the Inferno Minion, each with up to 12 tiers. Every number comes from Hypixel: time between actions, storage, what each one collects, and what each upgrade costs.

## Placing a minion

Hold a minion and right-click the **top of a block on your own island**. It stands on that block and works the **5 by 5** around it. You can place **5 minions** per profile to start with; on Hypixel, crafting more unique minions unlocks more places, up to 26, and that's coming here too.

Each minion works in its own way:

| Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Mining (Cobblestone, Coal, Iron, Diamond...) | Places its block on the empty spots around it, at the height of the block it stands on, then mines them. |
| Farming (Wheat, Carrot, Melon...) | Plants on the ground around it, then harvests. |
| Foraging (Oak, Birch...) | Grows logs on the outer ring around it, then chops them. |
| Combat and animals (Zombie, Cow, Chicken...) | Spawns its mob next to it, then kills it. |
| Fishing | Fishes. |

Give a mining minion room: leave the 5 by 5 around it **empty** (air) at the level of the block it stands on. It fills every empty spot first, then mines one each time and refills the gap, so it harvests on every other action.

## Working while you're away

A minion keeps time with the real clock. When you come back, it has done everything it would have done while you were gone, up to what its storage holds. When its storage is full it stops, and **/!\ My storage is full! :(** floats above it.

## The minion screen

Right-click your minion to open it:

- **The minion** turning on its stand, with a bar that fills up to its next action and a countdown.
- Its **speed** (time between actions) and how much it has **generated** in total.
- Its **storage**. Higher tiers hold more, and the locked slots show how much room the next tiers add. Click an item to take it, or use **Collect All**.
- **Upgrade**: what the next tier costs (the items from its Hypixel recipe), in green when you have enough. A higher tier works faster and holds more.
- **Pick Up**: puts the minion back in your inventory, with everything it had collected.
- **Fuel, shipping and upgrades**: four slots under the storage, explained below. Shift-click an item from your inventory and it goes into the slot it belongs in.

Only the profile's members can open a minion.

## Fuel

Fuel makes a minion work faster while it burns. Put a stack in and it burns one item after another, in real time, even while you're offline. The screen shows what's burning and how long it has left. Burning fuel can't be taken back out, except fuel that lasts forever. You can only add more of the same fuel.

| Fuel | Speed | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coal, Charcoal | +5% | 30 minutes |
| Block of Coal | +5% | 5 hours |
| Enchanted Bread | +5% | 12 hours |
| Enchanted Coal | +10% | 24 hours |
| Enchanted Charcoal | +20% | 36 hours |
| Hamster Wheel | +50% | 24 hours |
| Foul Flesh | +90% | 5 hours |
| Enchanted Lava Bucket | +25% | forever |
| Magma Bucket | +30% | forever |
| Plasma Bucket, Everburning Flame | +35% | forever |
| Catalyst | x3 output | 3 hours |
| Hyper Catalyst | x4 output | 6 hours |

## Automated shipping

A **Budget Hopper** or **Enchanted Hopper** in the shipping slot sells whatever no longer fits into storage, for 50% or 70% of its NPC price, so the minion never stops. It holds the coins until you press **Collect All** (or pick the minion up), and they go into your purse.

## Upgrades

A minion has two upgrade slots. Speed upgrades add up with each other and with the fuel.

| Upgrade | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Auto Smelter | Smelts what it makes: Cobblestone into Stone, ores into ingots, logs into Charcoal, Sand into Glass... |
| Compactor | Turns what it makes into block form: 9 Coal into a Block of Coal, Snowballs into Snow Blocks. |
| Super Compactor 3000 | Turns what it makes into its enchanted form once there's enough in storage: 160 Cobblestone into an Enchanted Cobblestone, then on into enchanted blocks. A minion needs room for 160 items for this to work. |
| Dwarven Super Compactor | Smelts first, then does what the Super Compactor does. |
| Diamond Spreading | 1 Diamond for every 10 items the minion makes, on average. |
| Potato Spreading | 1 Potato for every 20 items. |
| Krampus Helmet | A tiny chance at a Red Gift for every item. |
| Minion Expander | Reaches 1 block further (a 7 by 7) and works 5% faster. |
| Flycatcher | Works 20% faster. |
| Berberis Fuel Injector | Works 15% faster (farming minions only). |
| Solar Panel | Works 25% faster during the SkyBlock day. |
| Enchanted Egg | Chicken Minions only: an extra Egg from every chicken. |
| Flint Shovel | Gravel Minions only: always Flint, never Gravel. |
| Enchanted Shears | Sheep Minions only: shears every sheep for extra Wool. |
| Lesser Soulflow Engine / Soulflow Engine | Halves what the minion makes, but adds a Raw Soulflow every 3 minutes / 90 seconds. |

Two of the same upgrade only count twice for the speed ones (Minion Expander, Flycatcher). An upgrade that doesn't fit the minion does nothing. Picking a minion up gives back its fuel (minus the one burning), its hopper and its upgrades.

## Getting minions

For now admins hand them out: `/sbitem cobblestone_generator_1` gives a Cobblestone Minion I (`<type>_generator_<tier>`). Crafting minions from their collections comes with the Recipe Book.
