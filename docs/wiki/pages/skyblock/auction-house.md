The **Auction House** is where players sell items to each other: weapons, armour, accessories, anything. Open it from the SkyBlock Menu's **Auction House** tile, or type `/ah`.

## Browsing

Auctions are sorted into **Weapons**, **Armor**, **Accessories**, **Consumables**, **Blocks** and **Tools & Misc** (or **All**). Search by name, sort by **Ending soon**, **Lowest price** or **Highest price**, and switch on **BIN only** to see only Buy It Now items. Each card shows the item, its price and how long is left; hover it for the full tooltip and the seller. Click it to buy or bid.

## Buying and bidding

- **Buy It Now (BIN)**: click **Buy** and it's yours, straight into your inventory (or to collect, if your inventory is full).
- **Auction**: type a bid (at least the shown amount: the starting bid, or 15% over the top bid) and click **Bid**. If someone outbids you, your coins come back: to your purse if you're online on that profile, or to collect in **Manage**. When the auction ends, the top bid wins.

You can't bid on or buy your own items.

## Selling

Click **Create**, pick an item from your inventory, choose **Buy It Now** or **Auction**, type the price (or starting bid; `25k`, `2.5m` work), and pick how long it runs: 1, 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours. The listing fee is shown before you create it:

| | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Listing | 1% of the price (2% from 10M, 2.5% from 100M) |
| 1 / 6 / 12 / 24 / 48 hours | 20 / 45 / 100 / 350 / 1,200 coins |
| Collecting what you sold | 1% of it, when it's over 1M |

You can have up to 14 auctions at once. An auction with no bids can be cancelled from its page; the fee isn't paid back.

## Manage

**Manage** lists your auctions and the auctions you bid on: what's still running, what sold (coins to collect), what ended unsold (item to collect) and what you won (item to collect). **Collect** one at a time or **Collect all**, which also collects coins from bids you lost while away. You get a message when your item sells or you win an auction.
