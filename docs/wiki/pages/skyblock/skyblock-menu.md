## The SkyBlock Menu

Press the menu key (**K** unless you changed it in Controls) and the menu opens on the **SkyBlock Menu**.

On the left is **you**: your character (it looks at your mouse), your name, the profile you're playing, where you are, the SkyBlock date and time, and your purse.

On the right is everything you can open, in four colour-coded groups. Each tile shows something live about it:

| Group | Tiles |
| --- | --- |
| You (gold) | Your Profile, Skills, Collections, SkyBlock Leveling |
| Gear (purple) | [Storage](storage-and-wardrobe) (how many items in how many pages), [Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe#wardrobe) (the set you wear), [Accessory Bag](accessories) (your accessories, Magical Power), [Pets](pets) (your pet out), [SkyBlock Items](skyblock-items#browsing-every-item) |
| World (aqua) | Fast Travel (warps unlocked), [Calendar](calendar-and-events) (today's date), Recipe Book |
| Account (green) | [Profiles](profiles-and-islands) (how many of 5), Personal Bank, Settings |

The grey Personal Bank tile is still being built (the Banker in the Hub runs the bank for now). Every page is also in the menu's sidebar on the left.

## Fast Travel

Choose **Fast Travel** in the SkyBlock Menu, or type `/warp <place>`. Each warp has a short warm-up: stand still, and you can't warp while you're in combat.

| Warp | Command | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private Island | `/warp home` (or `/is`) | Your island |
| Hub | `/warp hub` (or `/hub`) | The Hub |

More islands get a warp as they're built. Like on Hypixel, you have to **visit an island once** before you can warp to it. The first time you arrive, chat says **Fast Travel unlocked**, and the warp lights up on the Fast Travel page.

## Working areas on public islands

Public islands are protected, so you can't break or build on them. The exception is the parts meant to be worked: a farm's crops, a forest's trees, a mine's ore.

- In those areas you can break the blocks that belong there. They **grow back** after a few seconds. Mined ore turns to bedrock until it does.
- Monsters keep spawning in fighting areas (like a graveyard), and only there can you fight on a public island.
- The sidebar shows which area you're in (for example **⏣ Coal Mine**).
