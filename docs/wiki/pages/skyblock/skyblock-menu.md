## The SkyBlock Menu

Press **K** and the menu opens on the **SkyBlock Menu**, a board of everything you can open. The tiles follow Hypixel's order:

| Tile | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| Your SkyBlock Profile | Your stats, level and purse |
| Skills | Your skills and their levels |
| Collections | Everything you've gathered, and what it unlocks |
| Quest Log | Your quests |
| Calendar and Events | The [SkyBlock calendar](calendar-and-events) |
| Storage | Your [Ender Chest pages](storage-and-wardrobe) |
| Pets | Your pets |
| Wardrobe | Your saved [armour sets](storage-and-wardrobe#wardrobe) |
| Fast Travel | Warps (below) |
| Profile Management | Your [profiles](profiles-and-islands) |
| Settings | HUD, sounds, keys and more |
| SkyBlock Items | [Every item](skyblock-items#browsing-every-item) |

Tiles in grey (Recipe Book, SkyBlock Leveling, Personal Bank) are still being built. Every page is also in the menu's sidebar on the left.

## Fast Travel

Choose **Fast Travel** in the SkyBlock Menu, or type `/warp <place>`. Each warp has a short warm-up: stand still, and you can't warp while you're in combat.

| Warp | Command | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private Island | `/warp home` (or `/is`) | Your island |
| Hub | `/warp hub` (or `/hub`) | The Hub |

More islands get a warp as they're built. Like on Hypixel, you have to **visit an island once** before you can warp to it. The first time you arrive, chat says **Fast Travel unlocked**, and the warp lights up on the Fast Travel page.

## Working areas on public islands

Public islands are protected, so you can't break or build on them. The exception is the parts meant to be worked: a farm's crops, a forest's trees, a mine's ore.

- In those areas you can break the blocks that belong there. They **grow back** after a few seconds. Mined ore turns to bedrock until it does.
- Monsters keep spawning in fighting areas (like a graveyard), and only there can you fight on a public island.
- The sidebar shows which area you're in (for example **⏣ Coal Mine**).
