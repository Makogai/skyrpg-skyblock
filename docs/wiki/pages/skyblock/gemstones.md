**Gemstones** come from the [Crystal Hollows](crystal-hollows): every gemstone block (stained glass) you mine drops a **Rough** gem of its kind. Many items (499 of them, Hypixel's) have **gemstone slots**; a gem set into one gives the item its stat.

## The gemstones

| Gemstone | Stat | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ruby | Health | Everywhere in the Crystal Hollows (red glass) |
| Amethyst | Defense | The Jungle (purple glass) |
| Jade | Mining Fortune | The Mithril Deposits (lime glass) |
| Amber | Mining Speed | The Goblin Holdout (orange glass) |
| Sapphire | Intelligence | The Precursor Remnants (light blue glass) |
| Topaz | Pristine | The Magma Fields (yellow glass) |
| Jasper | Strength | Small rare veins anywhere above the Magma Fields (magenta glass) |
| Opal | True Defense | not mined here yet |
| Onyx, Aquamarine, Citrine, Peridot | Crit Damage, Fishing Speed, Foraging Fortune, Farming Fortune | not mined here yet |

## Tiers

**Rough → Flawed → Fine → Flawless → Perfect.** 80 of a tier craft one of the next ([Recipe Book](recipe-book)). A better gem gives more, and so does a rarer item: a Fine Ruby is +4 Health on a Common item and +14 on a Mythic one. Hypixel's whole table is used (from NEU).

## Gemstone slots

An item's slots show under its stats as a row like ` [❤] [⸕] [✦]`: the symbol is the slot's gem (or kind: ⚔ Combat, ☤ Defensive, ✦ Mining, ❂ Universal), the brackets the gem's tier (gray empty, white Rough, green Flawed, blue Fine, purple Flawless, gold Perfect), dark when the slot is locked. A gem's bonus shows on the stat line in pink: **Mining Speed: +300 (+40)**.

- A slot for one gemstone takes only that gem; a **Combat** slot takes Ruby, Amethyst, Sapphire, Jasper or Onyx, **Defensive** Ruby, Amethyst or Opal, **Mining** Jade, Amber or Topaz, **Universal** any.
- Some slots are open from the start; the others cost coins and gemstones to unlock (Hypixel's costs: 50,000 coins and 20 Fine Jade for a drill's first Jade slot, up to 250,000 coins and Flawless gems for Combat slots).

## The Gemstone Grinder

**Gemma** in the [Crystal Nucleus](crystal-hollows#the-crystal-nucleus) runs the Gemstone Grinder. Put an item in:

- **Click** a locked slot to unlock it (its coins and gems are taken).
- **Click** an open, empty slot to set your best gem from your inventory that fits it.
- **Right-click** a full slot to take its gem back out, for Hypixel's price: 1 coin for Rough, 100 Flawed, 10,000 Fine, 100,000 Flawless, 500,000 Perfect.

Admins: `/gemstones` opens the Grinder anywhere.
