**The Rift** is a strange dimension you can only visit for a while. It opens at **SkyBlock Level 12**.

## Getting in

Go to the **Wizard Tower** (`/warp wizard`, the island south of the Wilderness) and climb to the top floor. Walk into the portal next to the Wizard. You need a **Dimensional Infusion**:

- You get one free every **4 hours**, up to 3 stored.
- The Wizard sells one for **32 Grand Experience Bottles**.
- It's used when you leave the Rift's own Wizard Tower, so a look around the tower costs nothing.

## Rift Time

Inside, your health bar becomes **Rift Time** (ф). You start with **8:00**, plus the Rift Time your gear gives (Wyld armour, the Enigma Cloak). It runs down a second at a time everywhere except inside the Rift's Wizard Tower.

- At **10 seconds** the Rift starts collapsing; at **0** you're sent back to the Hub's Wizard Tower.
- To leave early, walk into the portal on the Rift tower's ground floor.
- Nothing hurts you in the Rift. A monster's hit takes Rift Time instead.
- Falling off the edge sends you back to the tower and costs 10 seconds.
- **Mote orbs** (pink, floating near the ground) give **25 Motes and 2 seconds** each.

Your weapon doesn't matter here: every hit deals your **Rift Damage**, 20 plus your gear's.

## Motes

**Motes** are the Rift's money. The sidebar shows them instead of your purse. You get them from:

- Rift monsters.
- Mote orbs.
- The **Motes Grubber**, who eats Rift items: Shy Crux, Deadgehog Spines, Lil' Pads, Muted Bark and more.

## Enigma Souls

Violet **Enigma Souls** are hidden all over the Rift: on branches, in a grotto, behind a cage, at the end of a trail of flower pots. Right-click one to take it. A couple come from people:

- **Tel Kar** gives one if you hear his whole story.
- **Roy** sells one for 4,000 Motes and 8 Deadgehog Spines.

**Enigma**, outside the Rift's Wizard Tower, sells the **Enigma Cloak** for 100 Motes. With it, every soul you've found gives **+20 Rift Time**. Every 4 souls he sells a cloak upgrade: +2 Speed and +4% Mana Regen in the Rift.

## The Black Lagoon

West of the woods is a swamp of channels, giant mushrooms and the **Lagoon Hut**. Kay sells **Lil' Pads** for 10 Motes, and Roy sells his Enigma Soul.

| Monster | Motes | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deadgehog | 150 | 3 Deadgehog Spines (sometimes 4) |
| Lagoon Leech | 40 | Lil' Pads |

The channels are rough: right-click the water with a **Lil' Pad** to put down a lily pad you can stand on for 10 seconds.

### The Leech Supreme

The **Leeches Lair** lies under the lagoon, reached by the tunnel past Dr. Edwin. Its boss, the **Leech Supreme**, has 800 health and four phases:

- **Slime Pound:** a shockwave around it (-8 seconds; get off the ground).
- **Wicked Bombs:** red circles that blow up after 2 seconds (-10 seconds).
- **Leech Swarm:** small leeches.
- **Mortiferous Lazer:** a beam; **sneak** to duck under it.

It drops **8 Leech Supreme Fragments and 3,000 Motes** to everyone who fought it, and comes back 40 seconds later.

## Timecharms

**Timecharms** open the rest of the Rift. Craft them or earn them, then bring them to **Elise** in the Rift's Wizard Tower to secure them: +100 SkyBlock XP each, and your first earns the **Rift Necklace**.

The first is the **Supreme Timecharm**: 4 Bottled Odonata, 32 Lil' Pads, 24 Deadgehog Spines and 4 Leech Supreme Fragments. To bottle an **Odonata** (the dragonflies over the woods' ponds), hold an Empty Odonata Bottle (the Bugshopper sells them) and look at one for 3 seconds.

## West Village, the Dreadfarm and the Village Plaza

- **West Village:** north of the lagoon, across a rope bridge. Crooked houses, walkable rooftops, the Cake House, the Infested House and the Mirrorverse's door. **Shadows** drop 150 Motes and Shadow Crux.
- **The Dreadfarm:** a wilted mirror of the Hub's farm, with the **Great Beanstalk** to climb. **Volts** drop 155 Motes and Volt Crux.
- **The Village Plaza:** the town square, Barry and his protesters, Barrier Street and the floating islets of Deja Vu Alley. **Scribes** drop 100 Motes and Scribe Crux.
  - Cryptosis sells an Enigma Soul for 15,000 Motes and 10 Scribe Crux.
  - Seymour sells the Exceedingly Comfy Sneakers.

### Gunther's race

**Gunther** stands at the banner arch in West Village. Race through his ten glowing checkpoints and back to the start line: under a minute earns a **Dark Pebble**, under 35 seconds his **Gunthesizer Lichen**, which turns Wyld Boots (and 8 Shadow Crux) into **Gunther's Sneakers** (+30 Rift Time, +4 Speed).

### The hot dog contest

Ask the **Chef** to start it: for 60 seconds his stall flings hot dogs over the counter. Grab them and right-click **Joey McPizza** to hand them over. Fifty in the minute earns an **Enigma Soul**.

### The Dreadfarm's crops

**Shania** gives you a **Wand of Farming**. Hold it to farm:

| Crop | How |
| --- | --- |
| Agaricus Cap | Look at a brown mushroom until it turns red, then click it. |
| Caducous Stem | Click a rose bush's top, then its bottom. |
| Wilted Berberis | Break the dead bushes that sparkle purple; the sparkle moves to another bush. |

Twenty of a crop make a bunch. Bring Shania two bunches of each for a **Proto-Chicken**. Her task ends when you wear **Chicken Leggs**.

The **Berberis Blowgun** (60 Wilted Berberis, Wilted Berberis II) shoots arrows that only hurt chickens and Puffs. Punch a **Not Chicken** into the air and shoot it while it floats down: it drops a **Metaphoric Egg** and 50 Motes. Eggs make Chicken Leggs (32) and the **Chicken N Egg Timecharm** (8, with the Proto-Chicken and a bunch of each crop). The Blowgun also pops the balloons and presses the buttons some Enigma Souls hide behind.

### The Mirrorverse

The door in West Village's courtyard leads into **Dr. Emmett's Mirrorverse**: eight rooms, each split by a glass mirror. Your side hides something; the reflection shows it. Rift Time stands still inside and nothing can hurt you.

| Room | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Flip your levers to match the mirror's. |
| 2 | Your floor is blank; the mirror shows the lava and the iron path through it. A wrong step sends you back. |
| 3 | The mobs are invisible on your side and visible in the mirror. Their drops (written backwards) craft the **remmaH yniT**, which opens the door. The crafting table is invisible too. |
| 4 | Only the platforms in the mirror are safe; the others drop you into the lava. |
| 5 | Click the mirror to step through it; walls and lava block one side or the other. A pocket on the mirror's side, past the last lava pit, hides the **Big Brain Talisman**. |
| 6 | Dance: do what Dr. Emmett calls (Move, Sneak, Stand, Jump, Don't jump, Punch). Seven steps open the door; all twelve earn the **Tiny Dancer**. |
| 7 | Climb an invisible staircase the mirror shows. Dr. Emmett lends you his **Emmett Pointer**, whose lazer marks the invisible steps. The levers at the bottom take you back to checkpoints; climbing without them earns the **Miniaturized Tubulator**. |
| 8 | Dr. Emmett pays **60,000 Motes** once. His reflection, ttemme .rD, sells the **mrahcemiT esrevrorriM** (10,000 Motes and 10 Shadow Crux), which opens the Village Plaza with Chicken N Egg. |

A Dr. Emmett in every room takes you back to any room you've reached.

## Porhtal's eyes

**Porhtal** (at the Broken Cage in the Wyld Woods) has seven eyes scattered across the Rift: The Intruder, The Gill-Man, The Baba Yaga, The Bankster, Gooey, The Prince and The 7th Sin. Hit an eye until you've dealt **300 Rift Damage** to calm it. Each calmed eye gives Rift Time for good (+10 for the first, up to +70 for the seventh) and becomes a stop on Porhtal's fast travel (5 seconds of Rift Time a trip).

## Montezuma

Sad Jacquelle's cat Montezuma lost his soul in **nine pieces** all over the Rift: under a tree, on a rooftop, behind a cracked wall, under the snow... Right-click a piece to take it, then bring it to her. Every piece back gives **+15 Rift Time**; from six on, each also gives Mana Regen in the Rift.

## Kat's Infested House

**Kat** (West Village) lends you her **Turbomax Vacuum Cleaner**. Hold right-click at the flies, spiders and silverfish in her house to suck them up (the bag holds 8), then empty it into the **Vermin Bin** (the composter in the front room). What you've deposited unlocks her trades:

| Trade | Deposited | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disinfestor Gloves (+30 Rift Time) | 4 flies, 4 spiders, 4 silverfish | 12 Shadow Crux |
| Vermin Belt | 15 flies (and the gloves) | a Leech Belt |
| Rift Ferret pet | 20 flies, 10 spiders, 20 silverfish | 12 Riftwart Roots, 3,000 Motes |
| Key to the Infested House Soul | 40 silverfish | 500 Motes |
| Fake Neuroscience Degree | 30 flies, 40 spiders, 60 silverfish | 10 Volt Crux, 10 Shadow Crux, 5,000 Motes |

With the key, press the button by the iron door to reach the locked room and its Enigma Soul.

### Timite

Youngite ore (light blue glass) grows round the Mountaintop's Continuum and on the Time-Torn Isles. Hold a **Time Gun** (a Berberis Blowgun and 32 Youngite) on an ore for 2 seconds and it ages into **Timite** (blue); age it again for **Obsolite** (purple). Aged ore turns back after 30 seconds.

| Ore | Breaks with | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Youngite | Anti-Sentient Pickaxe (Chipper McPickaxe, 24,999 Motes), Eon or Chrono | 1 Youngite |
| Timite | Eon Pickaxe (Timite I) or Chrono Pickaxe (Timite III) | 1 Timite and +2s Rift Time |
| Obsolite | Chrono Pickaxe | 1 Obsolite |

### Shen and the Celestial Timecharm

**Madame Eleanor Q. Goldsworth X** (the summit) sells **Shen's Ringalia** for 25,000 Motes, 64 Puff Crux, 20 Youngite, 20 Timite, 20 Obsolite and the Disinfestor Gloves. **Obo** only lets its wearers into the **Cerebral Citadel**.

Inside, **Shen's Hive Mind** fills the west wall: seven lamps over seven buttons. The oak button on the left picks the sequence (one per Shen), the one on the right resets it. Each button flips its own lamp and, in every sequence but the first, some others. Light all seven and that Shen will see you: **Shen Citrinitas** gives the **Celestial Timecharm**, **Shen Magnum Opus** the **Golden Belt** (+100 Rift Time, +10 Intelligence, +4 Speed).

### Ubik's games

**Ubik Von Neumann** (the Continuum) hides an Enigma Soul in each game:

- **Ubik's Boxes:** pick one of three boxes; he opens an empty one. The prize is an IQ Point; switch, and explain why, for the soul.
- **Ubik's Averages:** pick a number from 1 to 100 each SkyBlock year. The closest tenth to half the average of everyone's picks win Free Will and the soul.
- **Tit for Tat:** 5 to 9 rounds of split or steal (both split: 3,000 Motes each; both steal: 1,000; a steal against a split: 5,000 for the stealer). Split first, then copy his last move every round, for **Ubik's Cube** and the soul.

### Bacte

Walk into the Colosseum's arena and Bacte, a giant slime with 1,000 health, oozes out. Each phase it raises tentacles (one, then two, three, four and six); it can't be hurt while any is up. Watch for spikes (stand clear of the green circles), its slice (jump) and, at the end, a floor that bounces. It drops **8 Bacte Fragments and 6,000 Motes**.

### The Vampire Slayer

**Maddox**, in Stillgore Château's great hall, starts the **Riftstalker Bloodfiend** for Motes:

| Tier | Motes | Health | Hearts a second | Slayer XP | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 2,000 | 625 | 0.5 | 10 | - |
| II | 4,000 | 1,100 | 1 | 25 | a tier I kill |
| III | 5,000 | 1,800 | 1 | 60 | a tier II kill |
| IV | 7,000 | 2,400 | 1 | 120 | a tier III kill, Combat 15 |
| V | 10,000 | 3,000 | 1.5 | 150 | a tier IV kill, Combat 25 |

Kill the Château's vampires to fill the quest, then the Bloodfiend rises beside you. It drains your Hearts every second. From tier II it also slashes with **Twinclaws** (don't stand in front of it) and goes into **Mania** (it can't be hurt for 8 seconds). Below a fifth of its health only a **Steak Stake** (Maddox sells them) finishes it. Rewards are Coven Seals, rare drops (Bubba Blister, Guardian Lucky Block, McGrubber's Burger, Unfanged Vampire Part) and Motes. Slayer levels (20, 75, 240, 840 and 2,400 XP) give Rift Time and SkyBlock XP.
