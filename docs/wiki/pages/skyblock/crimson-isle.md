The **Crimson Isle** is a nether island floating in a sea of lava, Hypixel SkyBlock's late-game combat island. Two factions split it: the **Mages** of **Scarleton** in the east and the **Barbarians** of **Dragontail** in the west, with the **Stronghold**, the **Crimson Fields** and the **Blazing Volcano** between them.

## Getting there

A launch pad on the [Spider's Den](the-islands#spider-s-den)'s east edge, next to its pads to the Hub and The End. It needs **Combat XXII**. You land at the Crimson Isle's south coast, where a pad goes back to the Spider's Den. Once you've been to them, the warps take you straight there:

| Warp | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `/warp crimson` | the arrival, at the Crimson Fields |
| `/warp dragontail` | the Barbarians' town |
| `/warp scarleton` | the Mages' town |
| `/warp smoldering` | the Smoldering Tomb |
| `/warp kuudra` | the Forgotten Skull, Kuudra's lair |

Lava rivers run between the island's three parts; four nether-brick bridges cross them. Fall into the lava sea and it throws you back out at the nearest warp.

## Trophy Fishing

Fish the Isle's lava with a **lava rod** (Starter Lava Rod and up, Fishing X). A bite that isn't a sea creature can be a **Trophy Fish**: every fish whose conditions you meet gets a try, rarest first, at its chance (more with **Trophy Fish Chance**, from Odger's teeth and lava rods). Each comes in four tiers: **Bronze** 73.353%, **Silver** 24.451%, **Gold** 1.996%, **Diamond** 0.2% (the **Charm** enchantment raises the better ones). Hypixel's pity: catch the same fish 100 times without a Gold and the next is Gold; 600 without a Diamond and the next is Diamond.

## Elle and the factions

**Elle** waits just inside the Stronghold's south gate. Before any faction takes you in:

1. Elle wants **4 Magma Chunks** (Magma Cubes), **3 Blaze Ashes** (Blazes) and **2 Spectre Dust** (Wither Spectres).
2. Then meet both leaders: **Queen Nyx** in Scarleton and **Chief Scorn** in Dragontail.
3. Talk to the one you choose and join.

You can switch sides any time at the other leader. Your **reputation** is kept with each faction, so switching doesn't lose it.

| Rank | Reputation | SkyBlock XP on reaching it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral | 0 | |
| Friendly | 1,000 | 5 |
| Trusted | 3,000 | 10 |
| Honored | 7,000 | 20 |
| Hero | 12,000 | 40 |

## Minibosses

Each has a spot of its own and comes back **two minutes** after it dies. When one appears, everyone on the island is told. Anyone who did at least half a percent of its health in damage gets its reward.

| Miniboss | Where | Level | Health | Crimson Essence | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bladesoul | the Stronghold's courtyard | 477 | 50,000,000 | 25 | 1,500 |
| Mage Outlaw | the Courtyard | 515 | 70,000,000 | 25 | 1,500 |
| Barbarian Duke X | the Dukedom | 486 | 70,000,000 | 25 | 1,500 |
| Ashfang | the Ruins of Ashfang | 508 | 50,000,000 | 50 | 1,300 |
| Magma Boss | the Magma Chamber | 523 | 200,000,000 | 50 | 1,307 |

They also give Combat XP (2,500 to 3,922) and count for your faction quests. The **Magma Boss** gives 20-35 Magma Cream, a 50% chance at Enchanted Magma Cream, Compact Ooze and a Rekindled Ember Fragment. Their moves:

- **Bladesoul** and **Barbarian Duke X** sweep everything around them (5 blocks).
- The **Mage Outlaw** and **Ashfang** burst into flame, burning everyone within 7 blocks.
- The **Magma Boss** leaps and slams down (9 blocks).

## Kuudra

**Kuudra**, a colossal magma cube, sleeps under the **Forgotten Skull**. Talk to the **Kuudra Gatekeeper** there to fight it with your party (up to 4). You must be in a faction, and each tier needs the one before it beaten.

| Tier | Kuudra's health | Mob waves | Free chest | Reputation | First clear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | 100,000 | every 35 s | 60 Crimson Essence | +10 | 20 SkyBlock XP |
| Hot | 500,000 | every 30 s | 120 | +20 | 40 |
| Burning | 3,000,000 | every 25 s | 250 | +30 | 60 |
| Fiery | 15,000,000 | every 20 s | 500 | +40 | 80 |
| Infernal | 240,000,000 | every 15 s | 1,000 | +50 | 100 |

The fight is in **Kuudra's Hollow**, a crater of lava with a fortified platform and a ballista in the middle and six islets around it on bridges. Fall in the lava and it throws you back onto the platform.

1. **Supplies.** Six supply crates sit on the islets. Right-click one to pick it up (you're slow while carrying it) and bring it to the ballista.
2. **Build.** Stand by the ballista to build it: the more of you, the faster.
3. **Kuudra.** Kuudra rises in the east. Fuel cells wash up on the islets two at a time; carry them to the ballista. Each one is a shot: right-click the ballista's back to fire. A shot takes a tenth of Kuudra's health and **stuns** it beside the platform for 10 seconds, and only then can you hurt it.

All through, Kuudra's followers climb out of the lava in waves (Kuudra Followers, Knockers, Magma Followers, Wandering Blazes, and Berserkers from Hot up), and every few seconds Kuudra slams down where someone stands (a ring of fire warns first). From **Burning**, two tentacles grab the platform after each stun and the ballista won't fire until they're dead. From **Fiery**, Kuudra heals while it isn't stunned. In **Infernal**, below a quarter of its health, it slams twice as often. Going down isn't the end: you're back on the platform after 10 seconds, but if everyone is down at once, Kuudra wins.

After the kill, two chests (`/kuudra chests` reopens them):

- **Free chest:** Crimson Essence.
- **Paid chest:** opened with that tier's **Kuudra Key**. Twice the essence (plus 2,000 in Infernal), a piece of Crimson, Aurora, Terror, Hollow or Fervor armour of the tier, Kuudra Teeth in Fiery and Infernal, sometimes a Kuudra Chunk or a Kuudra Core.

You're sent back to the Forgotten Skull a minute later (`/kuudra leave` goes at once).

## The Dojo

**Master Tao** at the Dojo (south-east, past the Mystic Marsh) tests you in his walled arena, one player at a time. Each test scores points and grades them: **S** (1,000+), **A** (800), **B** (600), **C** (400), **D** (200) or **F**. Tests last a minute (Mastery two). Leaving the arena ends a test early.

| Test | What to do | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Force | knock the helmeted zombies into the walls | iron 10, gold 20, diamond 30; leather zombies -15 |
| Stamina | walls of fire sweep across: stand in their gap | 17 a second; -50 when one catches you |
| Mastery | shoot the targets on the walls before they go dark (you get a bow) | green 8, yellow 16, red 32 |
| Discipline | hit each zombie with the sword matching its helmet (you get four swords) | +25 right, -10 wrong |
| Swiftness | stand on the green tiles, never the red | +10 or -10 every half second |
| Control | keep your eyes on the circling skeleton | 1 every tick you look within 10 degrees |
| Tenacity | fire rains on marked rings, faster and faster | 17 a second; -100 a hit |

Your best score in each test adds up to your **belt**, which Master Tao gives you with its Combat XP:

| Belt | Total | Combat XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | take any test | |
| Yellow | 1,000 | 80,000 |
| Green | 2,000 | 100,000 |
| Blue | 4,000 | 120,000 |
| Brown | 6,000 | 140,000 |
| Black | 7,000 | 160,000 |

Your first S in each test also gives 5 SkyBlock XP. Belts are worn as equipment and take Crimson stars at Vulcan.

## Vulcan

**Vulcan**, at the Forgotten Skull, adds **Crimson stars** to Kuudra armour (and the Dojo belts) for Crimson Essence: hold the piece and talk to him. Ten stars on Basic to Fiery pieces, fifteen on Infernal ones, at Hypixel's costs (30 to 90 essence a star on Basic armour, up to 265,000 on Infernal; coins, Heavy Pearls and Kuudra Teeth from the fourth star). Each star adds 2% of the piece's stats. Stars past five show as a circled number after the name.
