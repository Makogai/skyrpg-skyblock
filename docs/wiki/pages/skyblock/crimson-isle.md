The **Crimson Isle** is a nether island floating in a sea of lava, Hypixel SkyBlock's late-game combat island. Two factions split it: the **Mages** of **Scarleton** in the east and the **Barbarians** of **Dragontail** in the west, with the **Stronghold**, the **Crimson Fields** and the **Blazing Volcano** between them.

## Getting there

A launch pad on the [Spider's Den](the-islands#spider-s-den)'s east edge, next to its pads to the Hub and The End. It needs **Combat XXII**. You land at the Crimson Isle's south coast, where a pad goes back to the Spider's Den. Once you've been to them, the warps take you straight there:

| Warp | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `/warp crimson` | the arrival, at the Crimson Fields |
| `/warp dragontail` | the Barbarians' town |
| `/warp scarleton` | the Mages' town |
| `/warp smoldering` | the Smoldering Tomb |
| `/warp kuudra` | the Forgotten Skull, Kuudra's lair |

Lava rivers run between the island's three parts; four nether-brick bridges cross them. Fall into the lava sea and it throws you back out at the nearest warp.

## Trophy Fishing

Fish the Isle's lava with a **lava rod** (Starter Lava Rod and up, Fishing X). A bite that isn't a sea creature can be a **Trophy Fish**: every fish whose conditions you meet gets a try, rarest first, at its chance (more with **Trophy Fish Chance**, from Odger's teeth and lava rods). Each comes in four tiers: **Bronze** 73.353%, **Silver** 24.451%, **Gold** 1.996%, **Diamond** 0.2% (the **Charm** enchantment raises the better ones). Hypixel's pity: catch the same fish 100 times without a Gold and the next is Gold; 600 without a Diamond and the next is Diamond.

**Odger**, at his hut by the lava on the south coast, shows every fish you've caught in each tier, **fillets** the trophy fish you carry into **Magmafish** (4 to 1,300 each, by fish and tier), and ranks you:

## Elle and the factions

**Elle** waits just inside the Stronghold's south gate. Before any faction takes you in:

1. Elle wants **4 Magma Chunks** (Magma Cubes), **3 Blaze Ashes** (Blazes) and **2 Spectre Dust** (Wither Spectres).
2. Then meet both leaders: **Queen Nyx** in Scarleton and **Chief Scorn** in Dragontail.
3. Talk to the one you choose and join.

You can switch sides any time at the other leader. Your **reputation** is kept with each faction, so switching doesn't lose it.

| Rank | Reputation | SkyBlock XP on reaching it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral | 0 | |
| Friendly | 1,000 | 5 |
| Trusted | 3,000 | 10 |
| Honored | 7,000 | 20 |
| Hero | 12,000 | 40 |

### Daily faction quests

Talk to your faction's leader to see the day's **five quests** (new ones at midnight UTC) and your rank:

| Quest | Reputation |
| --- | --- |
| Two hunts: slay 10 to 30 of one of the Isle's mobs | 80-90 each |
| Defeat one of the minibosses | 120 (the Magma Boss 150) |
| Two errands for your faction's people | 100 each |

| Faction | Errands |
| --- | --- |
| Mages | Pablo: 32 Red Mushrooms · Avorius: a Cup of Blood · Mollim: 32 Mycelium |
| Barbarians | Pomtair: an Enchanted Bone Meal · Sirih: 16 Sulphur · Suus: 16 Magma Cream |

Talk to the person with the items in your inventory to hand them over. Every miniboss you help kill also gives **+20 reputation**.

## Minibosses

Each has a spot of its own and comes back **two minutes** after it dies. When one appears, everyone on the island is told. Anyone who did at least half a percent of its health in damage gets its reward.

| Miniboss | Where | Level | Health | Crimson Essence | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bladesoul | the Stronghold's courtyard | 477 | 50,000,000 | 25 | 1,500 |
| Mage Outlaw | the Courtyard | 515 | 70,000,000 | 25 | 1,500 |
| Barbarian Duke X | the Dukedom | 486 | 70,000,000 | 25 | 1,500 |
| Ashfang | the Ruins of Ashfang | 508 | 50,000,000 | 50 | 1,300 |
| Magma Boss | the Magma Chamber | 523 | 200,000,000 | 50 | 1,307 |

They also give Combat XP (2,500 to 3,922) and count for your faction quests. The **Magma Boss** gives 20-35 Magma Cream, a 50% chance at Enchanted Magma Cream, Compact Ooze and a Rekindled Ember Fragment. Their moves:

- **Bladesoul** and **Barbarian Duke X** sweep everything around them (5 blocks).
- The **Mage Outlaw** and **Ashfang** burst into flame, burning everyone within 7 blocks.
- The **Magma Boss** leaps and slams down (9 blocks).
