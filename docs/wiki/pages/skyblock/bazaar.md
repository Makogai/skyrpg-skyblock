The **Bazaar** is the market for SkyBlock's materials: over **1,000 products**, from Wheat to Enchanted Diamond Blocks. It opens at **SkyBlock Level 7** (see [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling)). Open it from the SkyBlock Menu's **Bazaar** tile, or type `/bz` (or `/bazaar`).

## Finding a product

Products are sorted into Hypixel's five tabs: **Farming**, **Mining**, **Combat**, **Woods & Fishes** and **Oddities**. Each tab has a group per collection (Cobblestone, Wheat, Ender Pearl...), and the products in a group go from the raw item to its enchanted forms. Every row shows what buying one instantly costs and what selling one instantly pays. Type in the search box to find a product in any tab.

Click a product to trade it.

## Trading

| | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy instantly** | Buy 1, a stack, or as many as your purse allows, at the cheapest sell offer. |
| **Sell instantly** | Sell all of it in your inventory to the best buy order. |
| **Buy Order** | Type an amount and a price each (or take the best order and add 0.1). The coins are paid now; the items arrive as people sell to you. |
| **Sell Offer** | Type an amount (all of yours if empty) and a price each (or one tenth under the cheapest offer). The items are handed in now; the coins arrive as people buy from you. |

Orders never trade with each other: they fill when someone else buys or sells instantly, as on Hypixel. The **order book** under the trading panels shows the best offers and orders, with how much and how many orders sit at each price.

Selling costs **1.25% tax**: on instant sales, and on the coins a sell offer earns.

## The market

So the Bazaar always trades, even with few players online, a **market maker** quotes every product at Hypixel's own prices from a snapshot: it always sells at the Hypixel buy price and buys at the Hypixel sell price. It's the line marked **Market** in the order book. Player orders that beat it trade first; nobody trades past it.

## Manage Orders

**Manage Orders** (top right) lists your orders: what they are, how much has filled, and what's ready to **Claim**. Claim one, or **Claim all**. **Cancel** an order to get back what hasn't traded yet: the coins of a buy order, or the items of a sell offer. You get a message when one of your orders fills completely. Orders belong to your [profile](profiles-and-islands).
