**The Garden** is your profile's own farm, a separate island shared by your co-op. It opens at **SkyBlock Level 5**: `/warp garden` (or the Fast Travel menu) takes you there.

## The island

The Garden is a 5 by 5 grid of 96 by 96 **plots**. The middle one holds the **Barn**: the **Desk** inside it, the **Composter** beside it, the **SkyMart** stall, **Pesthunter Phillip**'s shed and the **visitor yard**. The other 24 plots start fenced off. You can't leave the Garden's square; walking off the edge puts you back by the Barn.

You build and farm only on plots you've unlocked (anyone else visiting can look, not touch). The sidebar shows which plot you're on.

## Plots

Unlock plots at the Desk, in Hypixel's order (the first four around the Barn, then the corners of that ring, then the outer ring). Each costs Compost, later Compost Bundles, and needs a Garden level:

| Plots | Cost | Garden level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-4 | 1, 2, 4, 8 Compost | I |
| 5-8 | 16, 24, 32, 48 Compost | III |
| 9-11 | 64, 96, 128 Compost | V |
| 12 | 1 Compost Bundle | V |
| 13-24 | 1 to 15 Compost Bundles | VII |

Every plot gives **+3 Farming Fortune in the Garden** and 5 SkyBlock XP. A fresh plot is overgrown with grass, flowers, rocks and the odd tree: clear it to farm it. Compost and other costs come out of your inventory or your sacks.

## Garden levels

Garden XP raises the Garden level, I to XV (Hypixel's table: 70 XP for level II up to 10,000 a level from XI on, 60,120 in all). Each level gives 10 SkyBlock XP and lets visitors ask for more crops. Garden XP comes from **crop milestones** and **visitors**.

## Crop milestones

Harvesting the ten Garden crops on your plots counts toward their milestones: Wheat, Carrot, Potato, Pumpkin, Melon, Sugar Cane, Cactus, Cocoa Beans, Mushrooms and Nether Wart. Each broken crop counts as Hypixel's base drop (Carrots and Potatoes 3, Melons 5, Cocoa 3, Nether Wart 2.5, the rest 1) times your Farming Fortune. Milestones go up to level 46, on Hypixel's thresholds (Wheat 30 for the first level, Carrots 100, Melons 150...). Each milestone level gives **10 Garden XP times the level** and 1 SkyBlock XP. The Desk's Milestones tab shows them all.

## Visitors

A visitor arrives every **15 minutes** while you're online, up to five waiting in the yard by the Barn. Right-click one to see their offer: one or two of your Garden crops (raw, or enchanted for bigger orders), the more the higher your Garden level. Accept to hand them over for **Copper**, Garden XP and Farming XP; decline and they leave. Rarer visitors pay more:

| Rarity | Shows up | Copper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uncommon | most often | 2 and up |
| Rare | 30% | 2 and up |
| Legendary | 8% | five times as much |
| Mythic | 2%, from Garden XIV | five times as much |

Copper runs from 2 to 100 an offer. Every visitor you serve for the first time gives 1 SkyBlock XP.

## The Composter

Right-click the Composter by the Barn. It turns **4,000 Organic Matter and 2,000 Fuel into one Compost every 10 minutes**, also while you're away.

- **Organic Matter:** your crops (Wheat 1 each, Pumpkins and Mushrooms 1, Sugar Cane and Cactus 0.5, Potatoes, Cocoa and Nether Wart 0.33, Carrots 0.29, Melon Slices 0.2; enchanted crops 160 times that). **Add crops** puts in every crop you carry.
- **Fuel:** Biofuel (3,000), Oil Barrels and Volta (10,000). **Add fuel** puts in every fuel you carry.
- **Collect** takes the Compost.

Five upgrades, 25 tiers each, for Copper (100 at tier 1 up to 4,000 at tier 25) and enchanted crops: **Speed** (+20% a tier), **Multi Drop** (+3% chance of an extra Compost), **Fuel Cap** (100,000 + 30,000 a tier), **Organic Matter Cap** (40,000 + 30,000 a tier) and **Cost Reduction** (-1% Organic Matter and Fuel a tier).

## SkyMart

The SkyMart NPC (and the Desk's SkyMart tab) sells Hypixel's Garden gear for Copper (and a few things for coins): the Garden Scythe, gardening hoes and axes, the Lotus accessories, Copper Talisman and Ring, the Mk. I crop tools for each crop, vacuums, the Sprayonator and Pest Repellent, Biofuel and more.
