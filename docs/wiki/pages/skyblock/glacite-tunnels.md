The **Glacite Tunnels** are a maze of icy tunnels below the [Dwarven Mines](dwarven-mines), for miners at the top of the [Heart of the Mountain](heart-of-the-mountain). At their north end is the **Dwarven Base Camp**, a warm outpost around a campfire.

## Getting there

Talk to the **Ticket Master** at the minecart station in the Dwarven Mines (beside Gwendolyn). You need **Heart of the Mountain 7**, and the first time a **Secret Railroad Pass**, which you forge at [the Forge](dwarven-mines) (a Flawless Ruby, 2 Refined Mithril and 8 Corleonite). After that the ride is free, and **/warp base** takes you there once you've been. The Ticket Master at the Base Camp takes you back up.

At the Base Camp:

| NPC | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Emissary Lissandra | The Tunnels' [commissions](dwarven-mines#commissions) |
| Ticket Master | Back to the Dwarven Mines |
| Cold Enjoyer, Dulin, Scout Plinius | Tips about the Cold and the Tunnels |
| Dr. Stone, Researcher Beryl, Fossil Excavator | The [Fossil Research Center](#fossil-research-center) |

## The Cold

Out in the Tunnels you get colder: **1 Cold every 5 seconds**, slower with **Cold Resistance** (5 x (1 + Cold Resistance / 100) seconds) and four times as fast in water. The sidebar shows it.

| Cold | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| over your Cold Resistance | each point takes 0.5% off your Mining Speed |
| 25, 50 | "BRRR!" warnings |
| 75 | you slow down |
| 90 | frostbite: you slow down more |
| 100 | you freeze and wake up at the Base Camp |

The Base Camp warms you up at once, and so does leaving the Tunnels. Cold Resistance comes from Cold Resistance potions ([Alchemy](alchemy)), pets (the Glacite Golem, Mammoth, Penguin...) and gear.

## Mining

Everything here needs **Breaking Power 9** (Hard Stone needs 4). Blocks become bedrock and grow back after 20 seconds.

| Resource | Blocks | Strength | Each block gives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glacite | Packed Ice | 6,000 | 1 Glacite, 2 Glacite Powder |
| Tungsten | Cobblestone (and its slabs and stairs), Clay | 5,600 | 1 Tungsten (Clay: 3), 1 Glacite Powder |
| Umber | Terracotta, Brown Terracotta, Red Sandstone | 5,600 | 1 / 2 / 3 Umber, 1 Glacite Powder |
| Aquamarine, Onyx, Citrine, Peridot | blue, black, brown and green stained glass | 5,200 | 4 Rough gems, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Mithril, Titanium | as in the Dwarven Mines | | as there |

Mining Fortune and Dwarven Metal Fortune give more Glacite, Tungsten and Umber; Gemstone Fortune more gems. **Glacite Powder** is the Heart of the Mountain's third powder.

## Commissions

Emissary Lissandra's board: collect 1,500 Glacite, Tungsten, Umber, Aquamarine, Citrine, Onyx or Peridot, slay 50 Glacite Walkers (in the Dwarven Mines), enter a Glacite Mineshaft, loot 2 Frozen Corpses, find a Suspicious Scrap, or slay 10 Glacite Maniacs. Each pays **400 HotM XP (750 at HotM 10), 1,500 Glacite Powder (3,000 at HotM 10) and 25,000 Mining XP**.

## Glacite Mineshafts

Every block you mine in the Tunnels has a 1 in 2,000 chance to open a **Mineshaft portal** beside you. It can't take forever: each block also counts toward a sure one (2,000 points: Mithril 2, Glacite, Tungsten and Umber 4, gemstones and Titanium 8). The portal stays for **30 seconds**; right-click it to go in.

A mineshaft is yours alone: a long tunnel with side rooms, its walls full of one kind of ore. There are 16 kinds: Titanium, Tungsten, Umber, each of the twelve gemstones (Opal only comes from here) and the Fairy mineshaft with every gemstone at once. The kind you mined last comes up more often. Ore you mine in a mineshaft doesn't grow back.

| What's inside | |
| --- | --- |
| **Frozen Corpses** (1 to 3) | Right-click to loot: the Lapis Corpse is free, the Umber Corpse needs an Umber Key, the Tungsten Corpse a Tungsten Key, and the Fairy mineshaft's Vanguard Corpses a Skeleton Key. Each gives 3 to 8 picks from Hypixel's loot table (gems, Glacite and keys, enchanted books, Suspicious Scrap...). |
| **Glacite Maniacs** (3) | The Glacite Mutt, Bowman, Caver and Mage, level 173 to 177. Only a mining tool hurts them: every hit takes 8% of their health. They drop 50 Glacite Powder, Enchanted Glacite, sometimes a Fine gemstone (5%), a Glacite Jewel (4%) or a Glacite Shard (0.1%). |
| **Suspicious Scrap** | 1 in 200 for each block you mine, half as likely after each one you find. |

The Cold keeps building in there, and faster the longer you stay (+0.25 vulnerability every 150 seconds). The **ladder** at the start takes you back to the Base Camp with a Glacite Powder bonus: **1,000, plus 500 for each Suspicious Scrap, 250 for each corpse you looted and 10 for each point of Cold**. Freezing takes you back too, without the bonus. A mineshaft nobody is in is gone after a minute.

The [Heart of the Mountain](heart-of-the-mountain)'s Glacite Powder perks help here: Surveyor, No Stone Unturned, Gifts from the Departed, Dead Man's Chest, Vanguard Seeker, Mineshaft Mayhem, Rags to Riches and Eager Adventurer.

## Fossil Research Center

The hut at the west of the Base Camp, with the **Fossil Excavator** by its door.

**The Fossil Excavator** (HotM 7) digs for treasure and fossils. You need a **chisel** (kept) and a **Suspicious Scrap** (used up). The dig is a 9 x 6 bed of dirt: each click uses one of your chisel's charges and uncovers a tile.

| Chisel | Charges | Highlight chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chisel | 16 | 25% |
| Reinforced Chisel | 18 | 60% |
| Glacite-Plated Chisel | 20 | 60% |
| Perfect Chisel | 22 | 60% |

Uncovering plain dirt can **highlight** a tile (a gold star) with something under it: a treasure, or, once you've found a fossil, more of the fossil. 8 to 11 treasures are buried in each dig: Fossil Essence (10-40), Glacite Powder (1,000-10,000), Enchanted Glacite, Umber or Tungsten, Flawed or Fine Tunnels gemstones, Paleontologist books, and rarely Fossil the Fish. One dig in ten also hides a **fossil** (Clubbed, Footprint, Spine, Tusk, Ugly or Webbed): uncover all of it to get it; a part of it gives Fossil Essence (half a point for each percent). When the charges run out (or you press Finish) you get everything you uncovered.

**Dr. Stone** takes one of each kind of fossil (the six above and the Claw Fossil): each gives 25,000 Mining XP, 25,000 Glacite Powder, 5,000 HotM XP and 10 SkyBlock XP, and all seven another 50,000 Glacite Powder and 10,000 HotM XP.

**Researcher Beryl** sells Suspicious Scrap for 100 Fossil Essence, and salvages a scrap for 5 or a fossil Dr. Stone already has for 50 (the Claw 25). `/essence` shows your essence.
