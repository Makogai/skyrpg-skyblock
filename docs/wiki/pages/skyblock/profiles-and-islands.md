Like on Hypixel, you play SkyBlock on a **profile**. Each profile is a separate playthrough: its own **private island** and its own inventory, ender chest, skills, collections, pets and coins. You can have up to **five** profiles and switch between them whenever you like.

## Your first profile

Every profile gets a fruit for a name, picked at random: Apple, Banana, Blueberry and so on. The first time you join, everything you already had becomes your first profile, so nothing is lost.

## Your private island

Each profile has a small floating island of its own: grass over dirt and stone, an oak tree, and a **portal to the Hub** on the north side.

- **Building.** Only the profile's members can break and place blocks on an island. Each island has a build area of 160 by 160 blocks around its centre.
- **The portal.** Walk through the portal to warp to the Hub. The Hub's big portal brings you back to your island.
- **`/is`** (also `/island` and `/warp home`) warps you to your island from anywhere, after a short warm-up. You have to stand still, and you can't warp while you're in combat.
- **Dying** on an island puts you back on your own island.

## Visiting

`/visit <player>` warps you to the island of the profile that player is playing. As a guest you can look around but you can't break, place or open anything.

## Switching and new profiles

Open the K menu and choose **Profile Management**. It lists your profiles, shows which one you're playing, and has a **Switch** button on each of the others. There's also a **Create** button while you have fewer than five. Both ask for a second click, because they reconnect you to the server for a moment.

There are commands for the same things:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/profiles` | Lists your profiles. Click **[Switch]** next to one to play it, or **[Create a new profile]**. |
| `/profiles switch <name>` | Switches to that profile. |
| `/profiles create` | Starts a new profile on a new island. |

A new profile starts with nothing, on its own island. Your other profiles stay exactly as you left them.

## Co-ops

A **co-op** is a profile shared by several players. They share the island, and each member keeps their own inventory and skills on it.

1. While playing the profile you want to share, type `/coopadd <player>`.
2. The other player clicks **[Accept]** in chat, or types `/coop accept <you>`, within 5 minutes.
3. They get a new profile that's a member of your co-op, and they start on your island.

Every member can build on a co-op island.

## Dying

Like on Hypixel, you **keep your items** when you die, but you lose **half the coins in your purse**. Coins in the bank are safe.
