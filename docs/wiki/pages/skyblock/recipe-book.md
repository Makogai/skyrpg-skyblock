SkyBlock has **2,468** crafting recipes, all from Hypixel SkyBlock, with the same grids and amounts: an Enchanted Cobblestone takes 160 Cobblestone, a Cobblestone Minion I takes 80 Cobblestone around a Wooden Pickaxe. Most recipes are unlocked by a [collection](collections) tier. Some need a slayer level, and a few have no requirement at all.

## The Recipe Book

Open the SkyBlock Menu and choose **Recipe Book**. Its tile shows how many recipes you have unlocked.

- Every item that has a recipe is in the grid. Type to search by name or SkyBlock id.
- **Show:** switches between **All**, **Unlocked** (what you can make once you have the materials) and **Craftable** (what you can make right now).
- Locked items are dimmed. Hover one to see what unlocks it ("Requires Coal Collection II").
- Items you can craft right now glow green at the bottom.

Click an item to see its recipe on the right:

- The **3 by 3 grid** with how many of each ingredient a cell takes. A green line under a cell means you have enough of that ingredient; red means you don't. Hover a cell for the ingredient's tooltip and how many you have.
- **What unlocks it**, and your tier in that collection while it's still locked.
- Items with more than one recipe show **Recipe 1 of 2**: click the arrows to switch.
- **Craft** makes it from the materials in your inventory (Hypixel's Supercraft). **Shift-click** to craft as many as you can, up to 64 at once.

## The crafting table

Right-clicking a **crafting table** opens SkyBlock's crafting screen (sneak to place blocks on it instead). You can also type `/craft`.

Put the ingredients in the 3 by 3 grid as on Hypixel. A smaller pattern can sit anywhere in the grid, and mirrored. As soon as the grid makes something, the result shows on the right with its rarity's glow:

- **Craft** makes one. **Craft all** makes as many as the grid holds (shift-click does the same).
- A locked recipe shows what unlocks it.
- The **Recipe Book** link at the top opens the Recipe Book.

What you make goes into your inventory. Whatever is left in the grid comes back to you when you close the screen.

Crafted items don't count for collections. Crafting a minion for the first time counts as a new **unique minion** (see [Minions](minions)).
