**Galatea** is the foraging archipelago from Hypixel's 2025 Foraging update. Its first island is **Moonglade Marsh**: woods and fig groves in the north, and the great **Murkwater Loch** with its mangroves in the south. Galatea also brings a new skill, **Hunting**: catch the critters that roam the island for their **shards**, then syphon the shards into **attributes** that give you stats.

## Getting there

You need **Foraging XII**. The launch pad on **The Park's Jungle Island**, just east of Molbert and Juliette's campfire, takes you to **Tangleburg**, the island's town. A pad in Tangleburg takes you back. Once you've been, `/warp galatea` works too.

## Trees

Galatea's trees are chopped differently from the rest of SkyBlock. Hit a log with an axe and you take it, plus extra logs if your **Sweep** is high enough for the tree's **toughness**:

| Tree | Toughness | Order to cut | Foraging XP a log | Tree Gift extra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fig | 10 | trunk, then branches | 15 | Tender Wood |
| Mangrove | 50 | branches, then trunk, then roots | 20 | Vinesap |

Cut out of order and your Sweep only counts for half. Vines on a log don't get in the way: hitting them chops the log behind. Fig Fortune and Mangrove Fortune (on top of Foraging Fortune) give extra logs.

When the last log falls, everyone who cut at least a tenth of the tree gets a **Tree Gift**: Forest Essence, Foraging XP and a chance of Tender Wood or Vinesap. If you cut a fifth of the tree the gift is 1.5 times bigger, and if you cut a third it's doubled, so chopping together pays. The tree grows back a minute later. A tree left half-cut for two minutes grows back whole.

## Hunting

Critters wander Galatea's woods and swim its water. Each one is named after the **shard** it carries, and the name's colour shows how rare it is. Every shard you catch goes into your **Hunting Box** and gives Hunting XP (75 for a Common shard, up to 1,000 for a Legendary one).

### The lasso (forest critters)

**Auryon**, outside the Hunting Lodge, gives you your first **Abysmal Lasso**.

1. Right-click a critter within 14 blocks. The lasso catches it and its stamina bar starts draining.
2. Keep looking at it. If you look away, or it gets more than 16 blocks from you, it slips free.
3. When **REEL!** shows on your screen, right-click again within two seconds.

If you reel too early or too late, the critter escapes. Rarer critters have more stamina, and better lassos (Vinerip, Entangler, Everstretch) and **Pull** wear them down faster.

### The fishing net (water critters)

**Jaeger** gives you your first **Basic Fishing Net**. Look at a critter in the water and right-click it. Better nets reach further (8, 12, 16 or 20 blocks) and catch more often. Quick critters (Lumisquids, Salamanders, Bullfrogs, Joydives, Sepialots and Water Snakes) slip through a Basic net: you need a Medium net or better.

### Charm Chance (mobs)

Galatea's mobs carry shards too. Kill a Bogged, a Chillblade, a Phanpyre or a Lumisquid, and your **Charm Chance** is the percent chance that its shard lands in your box.

### More shards

**Hunting Fortune** gives extra shards on every catch. Each 100 is one more shard for certain, and anything left over is a chance of one more.

### Huntraps

**Dax** at the Hunting Lodge sells the **Small Huntrap** (25,000 coins). Better traps are crafted with Tender Wood. Right-click with a trap on Galatea to set it on the ground in the woods or on the water of a lake. You can have five set at once.

A trap catches the shard of a critter that lives there, but it takes real time: 8 to 12 hours for a Common shard, up to 16 to 24 hours for a Legendary one. Better traps are faster:

| Trap | Faster by |
| --- | --- |
| Small Huntrap | 0% |
| Medium Huntrap | 10% |
| Large Huntrap | 20% |
| Greater Huntrap | 35% |
| Astral Huntrap | 50% |

Rosemary's Trapped perk makes them faster still. When a trap has caught something, you get a message. Click the trap, or collect it from Dax. You keep the trap.

### Pocket Black Holes

Right-click with a **Pocket Black Hole** to set it where you're looking, for 180 seconds. It pulls in Galatea's monsters that are under half health, and any that reach it under a tenth of their health are captured: their shard goes into your Hunting Box.

### Fusion

**Kysha** in the Fusion House fuses shards. Pick two kinds of shard from your Hunting Box and she shows you what comes out before you fuse. A fusion uses five of each shard, or two of a Reptile, Amphibian or Elemental shard. Two shards of the same family make **two** shards of that family, one rarity higher. Fusing gives Hunting XP.

## Lushlilac and Sea Lumies

**Lushlilac** (lilacs in the woods and wetlands) and **Sea Lumies** (sea pickles in the Murkwater Shallows) can be picked: hit them. Each gives 15 Foraging XP, and they grow back after a minute. A Lushlilac scores 9 Starlyn Contest points.

Eat a Lushlilac or a Lushlilac Bonbon (left-click at the ground or any block; right-click puts it in your sack) for Charm Chance:

| Lushlilac | +0.5% | 8 hours |
| Lushlilac Bonbon | +0.62% | 12 hours |
| Prime Lushlilac Bonbon | +0.75% | 18 hours |
| Exalted Lushlilac Bonbon | +1% | 24 hours |

## The Jade Dragon

The **Jade Dragon** in the Dragon's Lair sells its own kind as a pet: an egg that hatches at level 100. It costs 25,000,000 coins, and the dragon only deals with Heart of the Forest tier 3 and up.

## The Hunting Box and attributes

Open the box with `/huntingbox`, with the barrel in the Hunting Lodge, or from **David Hunterborough**'s menu. Every shard has an **attribute**, for example Grove's *Nature Elemental* (+2 Health a level). **Syphon** shards into an attribute to level it up. Each level costs more shards, following Hypixel's levelling. Rarer shards need a higher Hunting level before you can syphon them: Uncommon needs Hunting 5, Rare 10, Epic 15 and Legendary 20.

Attributes that give a plain stat (Health, Strength, Speed, Fortune, Wisdom...) work right away. The others are listed with their effect and will be wired in later parts.

## The Hunting skill

Each Hunting level gives **+1 Hunting Fortune** and **+0.04% Charm Chance**.

## The Heart of the Forest

Galatea has its own perk tree, like the Heart of the Mountain. Open it with `/hotf` or from Hina in Tangleburg.

- **Heart of the Forest XP** comes from Tree Gifts (10 for a Fig, 20 for a Mangrove) and from Starlyn Contests. It raises your tier, from 1 to 8. Each tier opens the next row of perks and gives **Tokens of the Forest**.
- **A token unlocks a perk** next to one you already have.
- **Perks level up with Forest Whispers.** Every Tree Gift gives 20 to 40, and contests give thousands. Some perks cost **Desert Whispers**, which come with Torrhus later.
- **The perks** cover Sweep, Foraging Fortune, more Tree Gift loot, Hunting Fortune, combat stats on Galatea, daily bonuses, and more.
- **Right-click a perk** to switch it off or on.

**Axe abilities** unlock at tier 2. Pick one in the tree, then sneak and right-click with an axe on Galatea or in The Park to use it:

| Ability | What it does | Lasts | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage Boost | double damage to Galatea's creatures | 10 s | 210 s |
| Axe Toss | thrown axes keep all their Sweep | 10 s | 120 s |
| Maniac Slicer | uses all your mana: +1 Sweep per 100 mana | 20 s | 60 s |

### Throwing your axe

Right-click with any axe on Galatea to throw it. It flies through leaves and vines to the first log up to 20 blocks away and chops it, but with only half your Sweep. You can throw once a second.

## Starlyn Contests

**Agatha** runs a Starlyn Contest every SkyBlock day (20 minutes), so there's always one on. Everything you do on the Moonglade Marsh scores points:

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| A Tree Gift | 10 |
| 100 Fig Logs | 3 |
| 100 Mangrove Logs | 6 |
| A critter | 3 (Common) to 45 (Legendary) |

Your points put you in a bracket: Common 25, Uncommon 100, Rare 400, Epic 1,000, Legendary 2,500, Mythic 5,000, Divine 8,000, Special 12,000.

When the day ends, go to Agatha on the Murkwater Loch pier to claim your rewards: **Agatha's Coupons**, Forest Whispers, Forest Essence, Foraging XP and Heart of the Forest XP. Your first three contests each day give 1,200 extra HotF XP. Epic and higher brackets have a 10% chance of **Agatha's Prize**. Spend coupons in her prize shop.
