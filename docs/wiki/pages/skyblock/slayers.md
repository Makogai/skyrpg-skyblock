Slayers are SkyBlock's boss quests. **Maddox the Slayer** stands in the Hub. Pay him for a quest, earn Combat XP from the right mobs, and the boss rises next to you. Beat it for slayer XP, Combat XP and its drops. Slayer levels give stats and SkyBlock XP, and unlock the crafting recipes for slayer gear. Everything here (bosses, costs, health, drops, odds and levels) comes from Hypixel SkyBlock.

Talking to Maddox opens the quest screen. The **Slayers** page in the menu shows each boss, your level, every tier and what each level unlocks.

## How a quest works

1. Pick a boss and a tier at Maddox and pay the cost.
2. Kill that boss's mobs. Each kill adds its **Combat XP** to the quest bar: a zombie gives 6, a wolf 10, an enderman 12.
3. When the bar is full, the boss rises next to you, with a boss bar and a clock.
4. Kill it before the clock runs out: 3 minutes for tiers I and II, 4 minutes above.
5. If the clock runs out or you die, the quest fails and the coins are gone.

Bosses only rise where there are mobs to fight: in a Hub area with mobs, or out in the world, but never on your island. The boss follows you around that area. If you leave it, log off or change dimension, the boss goes away and comes back with the time that was left.

### Unlocking the bosses

Each tier needs the tier below it beaten, and some need a Combat level. The next boss opens once you have beaten a tier of the previous one:

- **Tarantula Broodfather** opens when you've slain a Revenant Horror II.
- **Sven Packmaster** opens when you've slain a Tarantula Broodfather II.
- **Voidgloom Seraph** opens when you've slain a Sven Packmaster IV.
- **Inferno Demonlord** opens when you've slain a Voidgloom Seraph III.

The Slayers page shows what a locked tier still needs.

## Rewards

A slain boss sends one **SLAYER QUEST COMPLETE** message that lists everything it dropped, the Combat XP and the slayer XP with your progress to the next level.

Every drop the tier has rolls its own chance:

- **Guaranteed** drops always come.
- Drops under 5% get more likely with your **Magic Find**.
- A drop that needs a slayer level only rolls once you have it. The level counts this kill's slayer XP, so a first Tier I kill (5 XP, level 1) already gets the Revenant Flesh that needs level 1.

Rare drops are announced with Hypixel's banners: **RARE DROP!**, **VERY RARE DROP!** (Extraordinary) and **CRAZY RARE DROP!** (Pray RNGesus and RNGesus Incarnate). An RNGesus Incarnate drop is announced to everyone online.

### The daggers and the Hellion Shield

From tier II the Demonlord wears a **Hellion Shield** attuned **Ashen**, **Spirit**, **Auric** or **Crystal** (the boss bar names it, and a ring of its colour circles the boss). Only a melee hit from a dagger attuned the same way does full damage; anything else does 1%. Right-click a dagger to swap its attunement (the tooltip shows it): the Firedust, Kindlebane and Pyrochaos Daggers swap between Ashen and Auric, the Twilight, Mawdredge and Deathripper Daggers between Spirit and Crystal. Carry one of each. Every 8 hits that get through, the shield picks a new attunement.

## How the bosses fight

Each boss hits for its tier's damage per second, about once a second, and every tier keeps the abilities of the tiers below. The Slayers page and Maddox's screen list each tier's abilities.

All of them:

- **Regeneration:** a boss that hasn't hurt anyone for 5 seconds heals 2% of its health a second, until it hits someone again.
- **Slam-dunk:** stand in one block for 6 seconds without the boss reaching you, and the Revenant, Tarantula or Sven pulls you in for True Damage (twice its damage per second). Keep moving.

### Revenant Horror

- **Undead:** arrows heal it instead of hurting it. It gets a burst of speed when it hits you.
- **Life Drain** (I): every 5 seconds, within 12 blocks, it drains one and a half times its damage per second from you and heals 1%.
- **Pestilence** (II): everyone within 5 blocks takes a quarter of its damage per second every second, and loses 25% of their Defense for 3 seconds.
- **Enrage** (III): after 40 seconds it gets **MAD**, and 5 seconds later **ENRAGED** for 12: 30% faster and three times as hard-hitting. Then it starts over.
- **The Atoned Horror** (V): a different boss in netherite. **Redemption:** it heals a thousandth of its health a second, and 5% whenever its TNT hits you. **Explosive Assault:** every second a circle marks the ground under you, and a moment later TNT lands there, taking 15% to 60% of your health as True Damage (more near the middle); twice as often below a third of its health. **Thermonuclear:** every 30 seconds it stops for 5 ("Boom!") while a bedrock ring grows around it, then blasts everything within 7 blocks dead.

### Tarantula Broodfather

A cave spider rides it. Hits on the rider go to the Broodfather.

- **Backstab** (I): every 7 seconds it leaps behind you. Its hits from behind deal +50% damage (+75%, +100%, +125%, +150% at II to V).
- **Noxious Paralysis** (II): while it lives, everyone fighting it heals 25% less (50%, 75%, 99% at III to V), and every fifth hit lowers your Speed a little more (0.5% of the 400 cap a stack, 1% at III and IV, 2% at V).
- **Web of Lies** (III): at 66% and 33% health it leaps away, webs the ground around it and lays egg sacs above itself: 2 sacs of 3 hits at III, 3 of 6 at IV, 4 of 9 at V. Smash them within 12 seconds or each hatches two cave spiders.
- **Bat Barrage** (IV): from 66% health, magic can't hurt it and it releases 5 bats every 20 seconds. Each bat alive takes 1% of your health a second as True Damage (2% at V).
- **Till Death Do Us Part** (V): the Primordial Broodfather doesn't stay dead. It fuses into the **Conjoined Brood**, at full health with twice the toughness and twice the damage, keeping only Backstab and Noxious Paralysis. Kill that to finish the quest.

### Sven Packmaster

- **Agile** (I): a small, fast wolf.
- **True Damage** (II): its bites also deal 10 True Damage (50 at III, 200 at IV).
- **Call the pups!** (III): at half health it slows to a crawl and calls a pup every second for 5 seconds. Each pup has a tenth of its health and a quarter of its damage. While any pup lives it is **Protected** and takes no damage, and it doesn't regenerate.

### Voidgloom Seraph

- **Malevolent Hitshield** (I): when it spawns, and at two thirds and a third of its health, it raises a shield of 15 hits (30 at II, 60 at III, 100 at IV). Every hit takes one off, whatever it deals; until it breaks, nothing gets through. From II the shield also makes it hit 3,000 harder every 3 seconds it stays up.
- **Dissonance** (I): it teleports behind or beside you every 8 seconds, every 4 if you stand still. From II it also deals half its damage per second to everyone within 6.5 blocks, every second.
- **Yang Glyphs** (II): from half its health (two thirds at III and IV), and never while its shield is up, it holds a beacon for 2 seconds and throws it nearby. Stand on or right next to it within 5 seconds to break it, or it kills everyone fighting (a thousand times their health as True Damage).
- **Nukekubi Fixations** (III): from a third of its health, a floating head appears near you every 6 seconds (up to 5). Look straight at one for half a second to destroy it. Together they deal `heads x 800 x 2^(heads - 1)` damage a second (2,000 a head at IV), and if that outdoes the boss's own damage, the boss itself stops hitting.
- **Broken Heart Radiation** (IV): at 5/6, 1/2 and 1/6 of its health it sits still for 8 seconds, can't be hurt, and sweeps four beams around it. A beam takes 25% of your health as True Damage and cuts your healing by 12% for 90 seconds.

### Inferno Demonlord

- **Immolate** (I): every second, everyone within 8 blocks takes 100 + a share of their max health as True Damage: 5% (8% below 66% of its health, 12% below 33%) at I; 5% / 10% / 15% at II and III; 15% / 25% / 35% at IV.
- **Demonsplit** (I): at half health (at 66% and 33% from III) it vanishes and two demons rise, **Quazii** and **Typhoeus** (500,000 health and 2,500 damage a second each at I; 1,750,000 / 8,000 at II; 5,000,000 / 15,000 at III; 10,000,000 / 30,000 at IV). Immolate burns from them meanwhile. When both are dead, the Demonlord comes back.
- **Hellion Shield** (II): see the daggers above.
- **Fire Pillars** (II): below 66% health a pillar rises next to you every 20 seconds. Hit it 8 times within 7 seconds, or it blows up for more than your whole health.
- **Apocalypse** (III): below 33% health, fire pits open around it every 7 seconds (one under you): smoke first, then fire. Standing in one burns 950 True Damage a second (2,865 at IV).

## The Slayer RNG Meter

Luck insurance for the rare drops. Every slayer has its own **RNG Meter**, on its page under the Slayers page (click a slayer, the pink card at the top). It opens once you've slain a **Tier III** or higher boss of that slayer.

- Each Tier III or higher kill adds its slayer XP to the meter.
- Click one of the drops on the card to **aim** the meter at it (click it again to let go). Only the rare ones can be aimed at (under 10%), once your slayer level has unlocked them and you've slain a tier that drops them.
- As the meter fills, your aimed drop gets likelier: its chance goes from 1x up to 3x. Once the meter holds the drop's price, **the drop is guaranteed** on the next kill.
- Getting the drop takes its price out of the meter (all of it, if the meter was short). Other drops leave the meter alone, and so does changing which drop you aim at.
- Magic Find still counts for a boosted chance under 5%. A full meter's drop is always yours.

The price in slayer XP is `500 x 100 / the drop's best chance (in percent)`, using the best of the tiers you've slain. A drop with a 0.5% chance costs 100,000 slayer XP; a 0.1% one costs 500,000. After each kill the quest panel shows how full the meter is.

The Catacombs RNG meters and the Vampire slayer's aren't here yet.

## Maddox's Batphone

Reach Wolf Slayer 3 and you can craft the **Maddox Batphone** (Enchanted Cobblestone around an Enchanted Redstone and an Enchanted Iron). Right-click it, **Whassup?**, to call Maddox from anywhere:

- The phone rings three times, then he picks up and his slayer screen opens.
- After a call he's busy for 20 seconds. Ring him too soon and nobody answers; try three times in a row and he shouts.
- There's no reception in the Catacombs or the Rift.

## Runes, books and dyes

Higher tiers also drop **runes** (level I), **enchanted books** and one **dye** each. They're in the drop tables above: Pestilence and Snake Runes with Smite VI and the Matcha Dye from the Revenant Horror, the Bite and Darkness Within Runes with Bane of Arthropods VI and the Brick Red Dye from the Tarantula Broodfather, the Spirit and Couture Runes with Critical VI and the Celeste Dye from Sven, the Endersnake, End and Enchant Runes with Mana Steal I, Smarty Pants I and the Byzantium Dye from the Voidgloom Seraph, and the Lavatears and Fiery Burst Runes with Fire Aspect III and the Flame Dye from the Inferno Demonlord. Dyes are 1 in 10 million on Tier I and 1 in 500,000 at the top tier, and Magic Find doesn't help. Runes and dyes are cosmetics: you can hold and trade them; applying them comes later.

> [!NOTE]
> Not here yet: the slayer shards, minibosses, and the Wisp and Mosquito pets.

## First kills

The first time you slay each tier of a boss, you get SkyBlock XP for the **Defeat Slayers** task: 25 for tiers I and II, 50 for III and IV, 75 for V (see [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling)).
