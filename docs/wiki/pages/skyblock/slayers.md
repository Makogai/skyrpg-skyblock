Slayers are SkyBlock's boss quests. **Maddox the Slayer** stands in the Hub. Pay him for a quest, earn Combat XP from the right mobs, and the boss rises next to you. Beat it for slayer XP, Combat XP and its drops. Slayer levels give stats and SkyBlock XP, and unlock the crafting recipes for slayer gear. Everything here (bosses, costs, health, drops, odds and levels) comes from Hypixel SkyBlock.

Talking to Maddox opens the quest screen. The **Slayers** page in the menu shows each boss, your level, every tier and what each level unlocks.

## How a quest works

1. Pick a boss and a tier at Maddox and pay the cost.
2. Kill that boss's mobs. Each kill adds its **Combat XP** to the quest bar: a zombie gives 6, a wolf 10, an enderman 12.
3. When the bar is full, the boss rises next to you, with a boss bar and a clock.
4. Kill it before the clock runs out: 3 minutes for tiers I and II, 4 minutes above.
5. If the clock runs out or you die, the quest fails and the coins are gone.

Bosses only rise where there are mobs to fight: in a Hub area with mobs, or out in the world, but never on your island. The boss follows you around that area. If you leave it, log off or change dimension, the boss goes away and comes back with the time that was left.

| Boss | Mobs that fill the bar (Combat XP each) |
| --- | --- |
| Revenant Horror | zombies, husks, drowned, zombie villagers (6), zombified piglins (8) |
| Tarantula Broodfather | spiders and cave spiders (5) |
| Sven Packmaster | wolves (10) |
| Voidgloom Seraph | endermen (12), endermites (4) |

### Unlocking the bosses

Each tier needs the tier below it beaten, and some need a Combat level. The next boss opens once you have beaten a tier of the previous one:

- **Tarantula Broodfather** opens when you've slain a Revenant Horror II.
- **Sven Packmaster** opens when you've slain a Tarantula Broodfather II.
- **Voidgloom Seraph** opens when you've slain a Sven Packmaster IV.

The Slayers page shows what a locked tier still needs.

## Rewards

A slain boss sends one **SLAYER QUEST COMPLETE** message that lists everything it dropped, the Combat XP and the slayer XP with your progress to the next level.

Every drop the tier has rolls its own chance:

- **Guaranteed** drops always come.
- Drops under 5% get more likely with your **Magic Find**.
- A drop that needs a slayer level only rolls once you have it. The level counts this kill's slayer XP, so a first Tier I kill (5 XP, level 1) already gets the Revenant Flesh that needs level 1.

Rare drops are announced with Hypixel's banners: **RARE DROP!**, **VERY RARE DROP!** (Extraordinary) and **CRAZY RARE DROP!** (Pray RNGesus and RNGesus Incarnate). An RNGesus Incarnate drop is announced to everyone online.

## Slayer levels

Each boss has its own slayer level, 1 to 9. A level gives a stat for good and SkyBlock XP (see [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling)), and unlocks recipes. A recipe that needs one shows **Requires Zombie Slayer 3** (Spider, Wolf or Enderman for the others) in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book). It crafts once you reach that level.
