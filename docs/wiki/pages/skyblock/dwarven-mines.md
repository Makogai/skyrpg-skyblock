The **Dwarven Mines** are a huge cavern kingdom of dwarves under the [Deep Caverns](the-islands#deep-caverns), Hypixel SkyBlock's second mining island. It's where you mine **Mithril** and **Titanium**, do **Commissions** for the King, level up your [Heart of the Mountain](heart-of-the-mountain) and forge gear at **the Forge**.

**Getting there:** the launch pad on the Obsidian Sanctuary floor, at the bottom of the Deep Caverns (**Mining XII**). You arrive at **The Lift**, on the east side of the Dwarven Village; a pad there takes you back. After your first visit, `/warp mines` takes you to The Lift and `/warp forge` to the Forge.

## Mining here

Everything in the Mines uses Hypixel's mining rules (see [Mining](mining#block-strength-and-breaking-power)): **Mithril** needs a pickaxe with **Breaking Power 4**, **Titanium** Breaking Power 5. Mined blocks turn to bedrock and come back after a few seconds.

| Block | Looks like | Strength | Breaking Power | Gives | Mithril Powder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mithril | gray wool, cyan terracotta | 500 | 4 | 1 Mithril | 1 |
| Mithril | prismarine | 800 | 4 | 2 Mithril | 2 |
| Mithril | light blue wool | 1,500 | 4 | 5 Mithril | 5 |
| Titanium | polished diorite | 2,000 | 5 | 2 Titanium | - |
| Pure Ores | ore blocks (Royal Mines) | 600 | 3 | the ore's item | - |

Mithril and Titanium count Mining Fortune and pay 45 and 100 Mining XP. Every Mithril block you mine has a **0.5% chance** to turn into Titanium instead of bedrock (more with the Titanium Insanium perk). **Mithril Powder** only drops here; it's what [Heart of the Mountain](heart-of-the-mountain) perks cost.

## Commissions

Talk to the **King** in the Royal Palace or any **Emissary** for the Commissions board. Each commission is a task:

| Commission | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Mithril Miner | Mine 350 Mithril anywhere in the Mines |
| *Area* Mithril | Mine 250 Mithril in Lava Springs, Cliffside Veins, Rampart's Quarry, the Upper Mines or the Royal Mines |
| Titanium Miner | Mine 15 Titanium |
| *Area* Titanium | Mine 10 Titanium in one of those areas |
| Goblin Slayer | Slay 100 Goblins (Knifethrowers and Fireslingers count) |
| Glacite Walker Slayer | Slay 50 Glacite Walkers |
| Treasure Hoarder Puncher | Damage Treasure Hoarders 10 times |

When one is done, go back to the King or an Emissary and click it for its rewards: **HotM XP** (100 at HotM 1, 200 at HotM 2, 400 from HotM 3), **500 Mithril Powder** and **5,000 Mining XP**. Your first **4** commissions each day also give **+900 HotM XP**. A new commission takes the finished one's place.

You have **2** slots; a third opens after **100** commissions, and Core of the Mountain level 3 adds a fourth.

## The Forge

The **Forger** in the Forge (`/warp forge`) forges Hypixel's **123 Forge recipes**: Refined Mithril, Mithril and Titanium pickaxes and drills, Golden Plates, Gemstone gear, Travel Scrolls, pets and more. The Forge opens at **Heart of the Mountain 2**.

- The top row shows your **slots**: 2 at HotM 2, one more each tier up to HotM 6, and one from Core of the Mountain level 2.
- Below are the recipes, each with its materials (green when you have enough), coins and time. Click one to start it in a free slot; its materials and coins are taken.
- Forging takes the recipe's time in **real time**, even while you're offline. You get a message when it's ready; click the slot to take the item.
- The **Quick Forge** perk takes 10.5% to 30% off every forge time.

Some recipes need a higher HotM tier, shown on the recipe.
