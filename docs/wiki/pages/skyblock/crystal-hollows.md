The **Crystal Hollows** are a huge cave world under the [Dwarven Mines](dwarven-mines), 512 by 512 blocks of **Hard Stone** with caverns, a lava sea and veins of **gemstones**. Unlike everywhere else, what you mine here is gone for good: you dig your own tunnels, as on Hypixel. The whole world is made anew once a week (Hypixel's lobbies close too), with fresh gemstones.

## Getting in

Talk to **Gwendolyn** at The Lift in the Dwarven Mines. You need **Mining XII** and **Heart of the Mountain IV**. She sells a **Crystal Hollows Pass**: **10,000 coins for 6 hours** (real time). With a pass, talking to her sends you down to the **Crystal Nucleus**. When the pass runs out you're taken back to the Dwarven Mines; the pad in the Nucleus goes back any time.

## Mining here

| Block | Strength | Breaking Power | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hard Stone (all the stone) | 50 | 4 | Hard Stone |
| Ruby | 2,300 | 6 | Rough Ruby, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Amethyst, Jade, Amber, Sapphire | 3,000 | 7 | its Rough gem, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Topaz | 3,800 | 8 | Rough Topaz, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Jasper | 4,800 | 9 | Rough Jasper, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Mithril, Titanium | as in the Dwarven Mines | 4, 5 | Mithril, Titanium, Mithril Powder |

Gemstones use **Gemstone Fortune**, Mithril and Titanium Mining Fortune and Dwarven Metal Fortune. **Gemstone Powder** only comes from the Hollows (gemstones, treasure chests and Commissions); it pays for the higher [Heart of the Mountain](heart-of-the-mountain) perks. Gems go in items' slots: see [Gemstones](gemstones).

### Treasure chests

Every Hard Stone you mine has a **1 in 500** chance (more with the **Great Explorer** perk) to uncover a **treasure chest** right where it was. Only you can open it, within a minute: right-click it for 3 to 6 rolls of **Gemstone Powder** (200-500 each), Mithril Powder, the zone's **Rough** and **Flawed** gems, **Treasurite**, and now and then an Oil Barrel, Sludge Juice, Ascension Rope, Wishing Compass, Jungle Key, Goblin Egg or, very rarely, a **Pickonimbus 2000**.

## The Crystal Nucleus

The great cavern in the middle, where you arrive: the five crystal pedestals (Jade, Amber, Topaz, Sapphire, Amethyst), **Emissary Sisko** with the Hollows' Commissions, **Gemma** with the [Gemstone Grinder](gemstones#the-gemstone-grinder), and the pad back to the Dwarven Mines.

## Commissions

Emissary Sisko has the Hollows' own board (the [Dwarven Mines'](dwarven-mines#commissions) is separate): **Hard Stone Miner** (1,000), a **Gemstone Collector** for each gem (100), **Chest Looter** (3 treasure chests), and slayers for Goblins (13), Automatons (13), Sludges (25), Yogs (13), Thysts (5) and Team Treasurite (13). Each pays **400 HotM XP**, **1,500 Gemstone Powder** and **10,000 Mining XP**; your first four of the day (on either board) pay +900 HotM XP.
