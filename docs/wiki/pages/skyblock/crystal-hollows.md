The **Crystal Hollows** are a huge cave world under the [Dwarven Mines](dwarven-mines), 512 by 512 blocks of **Hard Stone** with caverns, a lava sea and veins of **gemstones**. Unlike everywhere else, what you mine here is gone for good: you dig your own tunnels, as on Hypixel. The whole world is made anew once a week (Hypixel's lobbies close too), with fresh gemstones.

## Getting in

Talk to **Gwendolyn** at The Lift in the Dwarven Mines. You need **Mining XII** and **Heart of the Mountain IV**. She sells a **Crystal Hollows Pass**: **10,000 coins for 6 hours** (real time). With a pass, talking to her sends you down to the **Crystal Nucleus**. When the pass runs out you're taken back to the Dwarven Mines; the pad in the Nucleus goes back any time.

## Mining here

| Block | Strength | Breaking Power | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hard Stone (all the stone) | 50 | 4 | Hard Stone |
| Ruby | 2,300 | 6 | Rough Ruby, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Amethyst, Jade, Amber, Sapphire | 3,000 | 7 | its Rough gem, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Topaz | 3,800 | 8 | Rough Topaz, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Jasper | 4,800 | 9 | Rough Jasper, 2 Gemstone Powder |
| Mithril, Titanium | as in the Dwarven Mines | 4, 5 | Mithril, Titanium, Mithril Powder |

Gemstones use **Gemstone Fortune**, Mithril and Titanium Mining Fortune and Dwarven Metal Fortune. **Gemstone Powder** only comes from the Hollows (gemstones, treasure chests and Commissions); it pays for the higher [Heart of the Mountain](heart-of-the-mountain) perks. Gems go in items' slots: see [Gemstones](gemstones).

### Treasure chests

Every Hard Stone you mine has a **1 in 500** chance (more with the **Great Explorer** perk) to uncover a **treasure chest** right where it was. Only you can open it, within a minute: **pick its locks** first (aim at the sparkles on the chest and right-click them 5 times in a row; a miss starts over, and **Great Explorer** takes a lock away every 4 levels) for 3 to 6 rolls of **Gemstone Powder** (200-500 each), Mithril Powder, the zone's **Rough** and **Flawed** gems, **Treasurite**, and now and then an Oil Barrel, Sludge Juice, Ascension Rope, Wishing Compass, Jungle Key, Goblin Egg or, very rarely, a **Pickonimbus 2000**.

### Worms

Mining Hard Stone has a **1 in 600** chance to bring "something approaching" down your tunnel (30 seconds between worms): a **Stoneworm** (4 in 5, 5 segments, level 119) or a **Scatha** (1 in 5, 10 segments, level 181). They slither along your tunnel and bite when you're close (400 and 2,500 damage). Every hit with anything does exactly one hit and costs the worm a segment.

## The Crystal Nucleus

The great cavern in the middle, where you arrive: the five crystal statues, **Emissary Sisko** with the Hollows' Commissions, **Gemma** with the [Gemstone Grinder](gemstones#the-gemstone-grinder), and the pad back to the Dwarven Mines.

Find the five **Crystals** in the Hollows (below), carry each to its statue and right-click the statue to place it. With all five placed you get the **Crystal Loot Bundle** and a Nucleus run (the first 50 runs give 4 SkyBlock XP each): 14 to 19 rolls on Hypixel's table (Flawless and Flawed gems, Divan's Alloy, Divan Fragments, Quick Claw, Treasurite, Wishing Compasses, fossils, Pickonimbus 2000, Bob-ombs, Gold and Diamond Essence, Fortune IV and Lapidary I books, Ruby and Jasper Crystals...), **1 of every Fine gemstone** and 800 HotM XP (2,000 to 7,000 Mithril or Gemstone Powder at Heart of the Mountain 10). Then the crystals are up for grabs again. Nothing you did in a Hollows carries over to the next one (they're made anew every week), except your run count.

| Crystal | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Jade** | Mines of Divan: any **Keeper** gives you a **Metal Detector**; hold it (it shows the distance to the treasure and beeps faster the closer you get) and right-click within 3 blocks of the spot to dig the treasure up. Four finds are Divan's lost tools (Scavenged Diamond Axe, Emerald Hammer, Golden Hammer, Lapis Sword): hand each to its Keeper, then right-click the pedestal in the middle. |
| **Amber** | Goblin Holdout: bring a **Goblin Egg** (goblins drop them) to **King Yolkar** in his tower for the **King's Scent** (20 minutes, washed off by water), then right-click the throne in the Goblin Queen's Den. Without the scent the Queen's guards come for you. |
| **Amethyst** | Jungle: trade Sludge Juice at **Odawa** for a **Jungle Key** (or find one), give it to the **Kalhuiki Door Guardian**, cross the temple and right-click the altar: the crystal plus two loot chests with a better chance at a Jungle Heart. |
| **Sapphire** | Precursor Remnants: Automatons drop the six **Automaton Parts** (Electron Transmitter, FTX 3070, Robotron Reflector, Superlite Motor, Control Switch, Synthetic Heart; 2% each); give them to **Professor Robot** in the Lost Precursor City (or a Precursor Apparatus instead) and he opens the giant's storage. |
| **Topaz** | Magma Fields: kill **Bal** in Khazad-dum (everyone who did half a percent of his health gets one). |

## Bosses

**Boss Corleone** (level 195, 1,000,000 health, 4,000 damage) waits in the Mines of Divan's central chamber and returns 90 seconds after he falls. He throws a ball that becomes a **Smog** (10,000 health), which blows up after a few seconds. Drops: Rough Ruby, Rough Jade and a 25% **Corleonite**, for everyone who hurt him.

**Bal** (level 263, 10,000,000 health, 4,000 damage) rises from the lava of Khazad-dum's arena a minute after the last one, while somebody is in the hall. He hops after the nearest player and spits a fireball each hop; hitting him can summon Bald Blazes, and each hit may shake loose a Flawed Ruby or Topaz (5% each), a Yoggie (4.5%) or the **Bal Pet** (0.0126% epic, 0.0054% legendary). The Topaz Crystal is his final drop.

## Fairy Grottos

Up to three **Fairy Grottos** hide in each Hollows: round pink caves of moss and petals, walled with **Jasper** (magenta glass) and glowstone, with **Butterflies** (a Jasper Crystal one time in fifty). Dig, or listen for the falling cherry petals.

## Commissions

Emissary Sisko has the Hollows' own board (the [Dwarven Mines'](dwarven-mines#commissions) is separate): **Hard Stone Miner** (1,000), a **Gemstone Collector** for each gem (100), **Chest Looter** (3 treasure chests), and slayers for Goblins (13), Automatons (13), Sludges (25), Yogs (13), Thysts (5) and Team Treasurite (13). Each pays **400 HotM XP**, **1,500 Gemstone Powder** and **10,000 Mining XP**; your first four of the day (on either board) pay +900 HotM XP.
