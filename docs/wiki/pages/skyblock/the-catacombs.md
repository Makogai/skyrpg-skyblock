**The Catacombs** are Hypixel SkyBlock's Dungeons: you and your party (up to 5) go down into a crypt that's laid out anew every run, clear its rooms, open the **Blood room**, beat its **boss**, and open a **reward chest**.

## The Dungeon Hub

**/warp dungeon_hub** (it unlocks the first time you're there), or walk into the fountain portal by the Hub's village square. It's **Hypixel's own Dungeon Hub map**: you arrive in the courtyard, and **Mort** stands at the great gate in front of you, the way into the Catacombs.

| NPC | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Mort | Pick your class and a floor, and in you go |
| Ophelia | Dungeon gear and supplies |
| Malik | Adds stars to dungeon items for essence |
| Croesus | Keeps reward chests you left behind (later) |
| Adventurer Saul | Tips |

**Party Finder** (the button on Mort's screen): a party's leader lists the party for a floor (◀ ▶ to pick it); anyone not in a party can join a listing with room, and the leader is told their class. A listing goes after 30 minutes, when it's full, or when the leader leaves.

Talk to Mort: click a **class** (Healer, Mage, Berserk, Archer, Tank), then a **floor**. Your party's leader picks the floor and takes everyone in. Each floor needs a **Catacombs level** (and a Combat level):

| Floor I | Bonzo | 1 | 1 | 4 × 5 |
| Floor II | Scarf | 3 | 3 | 5 × 5 |
| Floor III | The Professor | 5 | 5 | 5 × 5 |
| Floor IV | Thorn | 9 | 9 | 6 × 5 |

## A run

Every run is a new map: a grid of rooms (some long, square or L-shaped) joined by doors. You start in the **Entrance** (green); the timer starts after 5 seconds. Where the server has Hypixel's dungeon maps installed, the rooms are Hypixel's own (their basements and hidden corners too, with secret chests where Hypixel's chests are), and so are the boss arenas.

- **Rooms**: their mobs appear when you first walk in. Mobs with a **✯** before their name are **star mobs**: kill them all and the room is cleared.
- **Keys and doors**: some doors are black **Wither doors**. Clearing the right room drops a **Wither Key**; walk into it, then right-click the door. The **Blood room**'s red door needs the **Blood Key** the same way.
- **Secrets**: hidden chests, items lying on ledges, and bats. Each holds essence or a dungeon item (Spirit Leaps, Healing Tissue, traps...). They count toward your score.
- **The Fairy room** (pink) is a quiet room; it counts as cleared when you walk in.
- **Blessings**: solving a puzzle, beating a miniboss and one secret chest in five bless the whole party for the rest of the run. Their levels add up (I on Floor I, up to IV on Floor VII, one more in Master Mode). Per level: **Life** +3% Health, **Power** +2% Strength and Crit Damage, **Stone** +2% Defense, **Wisdom** +2% Intelligence and Speed, **Time** +2% Health, Defense, Strength and Intelligence.
- **Minibosses** (from Floor II): a room's strongest mob is sometimes a miniboss: the Lost Adventurer, Angry Archaeologist (Floor III+), Shadow Assassin (V+), Frozen Adventurer (VI+) or King Midas (VII). Beating one blesses the party.
- **Crypts**: some rooms hide a **Crypt Undead**. Each one killed is +1 Bonus score, up to 5. The sidebar counts them.
- **The Mimic** (Floor VI and up): one secret chest is alive. Kill it for +2 Bonus score ("Mimic Killed!").
- **Puzzles** (purple rooms; Floor I has one, later floors more). Solve one and its room is cleared; get it wrong (or leave it) and it costs 14 Skill score:
  - **Three Weirdos**: three people before three chests. Talk to each; exactly one of them tells the truth. Open the chest with the reward.
  - **Higher Or Lower**: ten blazes float with their health over their heads. Kill them in the order the sign says (lowest to highest, or highest to lowest).
  - **Tic Tac Toe**: right-click a white tile on the floor to play. The AI never loses, so a draw is the way to solve it; if it wins, you fail.
- **The Blood room**: opening it wakes **the Watcher**, who sends his undead in waves. Kill them all and he opens the way to the **boss**.
- **The fairy** in the Fairy room revives everyone who's dead, once a run, when someone alive walks up to it.
- **Dying** makes you a **ghost**: you float, unseen, and can't fight or open anything, until the boss room (everyone comes back for the boss). If the whole party is dead, the run fails.

The sidebar shows the run: time, rooms cleared, secrets found, your keys, and your team with their class and health.

### The bosses

Each floor ends with its boss (a health bar shows at the top):

| Floor | Boss | Health | How the fight goes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Bonzo | 250,000 | Throws balloons that burst around you and calls undead. When he falls he gets back up once ("Oh, I'm not done yet!"), faster. |
| II | Scarf | 1,000,000 | Shielded while his Undead Warrior, Archer, Priest and Mage live. Then he fights, blinking around, burning his target and calling archers. |
| III | The Professor | 3,000,000 | Shielded behind four Guardians. At half health he calls two more and speeds up. |
| IV | Thorn | 4 hits | A floating spirit only the **Spirit Bow** can hurt. Spirit Wolves and a **Spirit Bear** come; whoever kills the bear gets a Spirit Bow with one arrow. Four hits and he's done. |
| V | Livid | 7,000,000 | Nine Livids in nine colours. The real one's colour is the wool block in the middle; hitting a fake hurts you. |
| VI | Sadan | 40,000,000 | Survive his Terracotta army for a minute, then beat his four Giants (Diamond Giant, Bigfoot, L.A.S.R., Jolly Pink Giant, 25,000,000 each), then Sadan himself. Giants stomp the ground around them. |
| VII | Maxor, Storm, Goldor, Necron | 100M, 400M, 750M, 1B | **Maxor** is shielded until both **Energy Crystals** are carried to their pads (walk into a crystal, right-click a pad). **Storm** only takes damage for 10 seconds after someone pulls down a **Pillar**. **Goldor** hurts everyone every few seconds until all four **Terminals** are done. **Necron** can always be hurt, and blasts everything around him at 80% and 5%. |

## Master Mode

Beat **Floor VII** and Mort's screen gets a **Master Mode** switch: the same seven floors, much harder.

- Each Master floor needs a higher Catacombs (and Combat) level: **24 for M1, then 2 more a floor, up to 36 for M7**.
- Mobs are past Floor VII's, and stronger each floor. The bosses have Hypixel's Master Mode health: Bonzo 200,000,000, Scarf 375,000,000, The Professor 600,000,000, Thorn 6 Spirit Bow hits, Livid 600,000,000, Sadan 800,000,000 (his Giants 250,000,000), Maxor, Storm, Goldor and Necron 800,000,000 to 1,400,000,000. Their minions are much stronger too.
- The Speed score allows **8 minutes**, and every secret counts toward the Explore score.
- Chests cost twice as much and drop **Master Skulls**, the **Master Stars** (First on M3 ... Fifth on M7) and, on M7, the **Dark Claymore**. Runs give three times the Catacombs XP.
- **Master Stars**: Malik puts them on an item that already has all five stars, in order (First to Fifth). Each adds **5% stats in Master Mode**. They show as ➊ to ➎ after the stars.

## Classes

Pick one at Mort before the run (hover a class there for what it does). In a run, the **drop key** (Q) uses your class **ability** and **sneak + drop key** your **ultimate**; nothing is dropped. The class's bonuses grow with its level.

| Class | Passive | Ability (drop key) | Ultimate (sneak + drop key) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healer | Allies within 8 blocks heal 1% of their health a second | Healing Circle: allies nearby heal 25% (10 s) | Wish: everyone to full health, +20% absorption (120 s) |
| Mage | +100 Intelligence (+3 a level), abilities 25% faster | Guided Sheep: a blast 10 blocks ahead (30 s) | Thunderstorm: lightning on mobs around you for 10 s (100 s) |
| Berserk | +20 Strength (+1 a level) | Throwing Axe: hits the first mob in 15 blocks (10 s) | Ragnarok: +100 Strength and Speed for 15 s (60 s) |
| Archer | +10 Crit Damage (+1 a level) | Explosive Shot: three blasts where you aim (40 s) | Rapid Fire: five shots a second at your target for 4 s (100 s) |
| Tank | +100 Health and +50 Defense (more each level) | Seismic Wave: mobs within 7 blocks, more with Defense (15 s) | Castle of Stone: 70% less damage for 20 s (150 s) |

## Score

| Part | Out of | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill | 100 | 20 + 80 for the share of rooms cleared, -2 for each death, -14 for each puzzle failed or left |
| Explore | 100 | 60 for the share of rooms cleared, 40 for secrets (Floor I counts 30% of them as all of them, Floor VII 100%) |
| Speed | 100 | full within the floor's time (10 minutes on Floor I), then a little less every few seconds |
| Bonus | up to 7 | +1 for each Crypt Undead killed (up to 5), +2 for the Mimic |

**S+** at 300, **S** 270, **A** 230, **B** 160, **C** 100, else **D**.

## Rewards

At the end you get **Catacombs XP** and **class XP** (the class you played gets it all, the others a quarter), more for a better score. Then the **reward chests**: right-click the chest in the boss room and pick **one**.

| Chest | Cost | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | free | |
| Gold | from 25,000 coins | |
| Diamond | from 50,000 coins | |
| Emerald | from 100,000 coins | rank A, Floor II+ |
| Obsidian | from 250,000 coins | rank S, Floor III+ |
| Bedrock | 2,000,000 coins | rank S+, Floor V+ |

Every chest holds Undead Essence (Wither Essence too from Floor III), and each floor has its own drops: Floor I Bonzo Fragments, the Red Nose, Balloon Snake, Bonzo's Mask and Staff; Floor II Scarf Fragments, Scarf's Studies and Thesis, the Adaptive Belt, Stone Blade and Red Scarf; Floor III the Adaptive armour, Suspicious Vial and Scarf's Grimoire; Floor IV Thorn Fragments, Spirit Wing, Bone and Decoy, the Spirit Bow and Sword, Thorn's Boots and Ultimate Rend; Floor V Livid Fragments, the Dark Orb, Shadow Assassin armour, Lethality VI, the Livid Dagger, AOTE Stone, Shadow Fury and Last Breath; Floor VI Giant Fragments, Sadan's Brooch, the Fel Skull, Soulweaver Gloves, Necromancer Sword and Lord Chestplate, Summoning Ring, Giant's Sword and Precursor Eye; Floor VII Wither armour, the Wither Cloak, Blood and Catalyst, Precursor Gear, Auto Recombobulator, Ultimate One For All, the three scrolls, Necron's Handle and the Lords' fish. Any floor can also give a Hot Potato or Fuming Potato Book, a Recombobulator 3000, a Kismet Feather or a Dungeon Chest Key. Better chests and a better rank make the rare ones likelier. Essence goes to `/essence`.

## Dungeon items and stars

**Dungeon items** (most gear from the Catacombs, and others marked as dungeon items) get stronger inside the Catacombs: **+1% stats for each Catacombs level**, and **+10% for each star**.

**Stars** (✪, up to 5) are added by **Malik** in the Dungeon Hub: hold the item and talk to him. Each star costs essence (Wither Essence for most gear), as Hypixel's item says; some also cost coins or items. A starred item shows its stars after its name.

### Malik's essence shop

The other tab of Malik's screen spends essence on perks that work in the Catacombs (click one for its next level):

| Perk | Essence | Levels | Each level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Health / Defense / Strength / Intelligence Essence | Undead | 5 | +2 Health / +1 Defense / +1 Strength / +2 Intelligence |
| Critical Essence | Undead | 5 | +1 Crit Damage |
| Forbidden Health / Defense / Strength / Intelligence | Wither | 5 | +1% of that stat |
| Forbidden Speed | Wither | 2 | +1 Speed |
| Forbidden Blessing | Wither | 10 | blessings 1% stronger |

Undead perks cost 1,000 Undead Essence and up (to 50,000); Wither perks 100 Wither Essence and up.

## The Catacombs level

Catacombs XP levels you up to 50 on Hypixel's table (50 XP for level 1 ... 569,809,640 in all); each level gives 20 SkyBlock XP (40 from level 40). Classes level the same way.
