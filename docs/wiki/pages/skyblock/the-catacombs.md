**The Catacombs** are Hypixel SkyBlock's Dungeons: you and your party (up to 5) go down into a crypt that's laid out anew every run, clear its rooms, open the **Blood room**, beat its **boss**, and open a **reward chest**.

## The Dungeon Hub

**/warp dungeon_hub** (it unlocks the first time you're there). The crypt at the north end is the way into the Catacombs, and **Mort** stands in front of it.

| NPC | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Mort | Pick your class and a floor, and in you go |
| Ophelia | Dungeon gear and supplies |
| Malik | Essence (his shop comes later) |
| Croesus | Keeps reward chests you left behind (later) |
| Adventurer Saul | Tips |

Talk to Mort: click a **class** (Healer, Mage, Berserk, Archer, Tank), then a **floor**. Your party's leader picks the floor and takes everyone in. Each floor needs a **Catacombs level** (and a Combat level):

| Floor II | Scarf | 3 | 3 | 5 × 5 |
| Floor III | The Professor | 5 | 5 | 5 × 5 |
| Floor IV | Thorn | 9 | 9 | 6 × 5 |

## A run

Every run is a new map: a grid of rooms (some long, square or L-shaped) joined by doors. You start in the **Entrance** (green); the timer starts after 5 seconds.

- **Rooms**: their mobs appear when you first walk in. Mobs with a **✯** before their name are **star mobs**: kill them all and the room is cleared.
- **Keys and doors**: some doors are black **Wither doors**. Clearing the right room drops a **Wither Key**; walk into it, then right-click the door. The **Blood room**'s red door needs the **Blood Key** the same way.
- **Secrets**: hidden chests, items lying on ledges, and bats. Each holds essence or a dungeon item (Spirit Leaps, Healing Tissue, traps...). They count toward your score.
- **The Fairy room** (pink) is a quiet room; it counts as cleared when you walk in.
- **The Blood room**: opening it wakes **the Watcher**, who sends his undead in waves. Kill them all and he opens the way to the **boss**.
- **Dying** makes you a **ghost**: you float, unseen, and can't fight or open anything, until the boss room (everyone comes back for the boss). If the whole party is dead, the run fails.

The sidebar shows the run: time, rooms cleared, secrets found, your keys, and your team with their class and health.

### The bosses

**Bonzo** (Floor I, 250,000 health) throws balloons that burst around you and calls his undead. When he falls he gets back up once ("Oh, I'm not done yet!") with all his health, faster. Beat him again to win. Floors II to VII have their real bosses' health but Bonzo's fight for now.

## Score

| Part | Out of | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill | 100 | 20 + 80 for the share of rooms cleared, -2 for each death |
| Explore | 100 | 60 for the share of rooms cleared, 40 for secrets (Floor I counts 30% of them as all of them, Floor VII 100%) |
| Speed | 100 | full within the floor's time (10 minutes on Floor I), then a little less every few seconds |
| Bonus | - | crypts and the Mimic (later) |

**S+** at 300, **S** 270, **A** 230, **B** 160, **C** 100, else **D**.

## Rewards

At the end you get **Catacombs XP** and **class XP** (the class you played gets it all, the others a quarter), more for a better score. Then the **reward chests**: right-click the chest in the boss room and pick **one**.

| Chest | Cost | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | free | |
| Gold | from 25,000 coins | |
| Diamond | from 50,000 coins | |
| Emerald | from 100,000 coins | rank A, Floor II+ |
| Obsidian | from 250,000 coins | rank S, Floor III+ |
| Bedrock | 2,000,000 coins | rank S+, Floor V+ |

Floor I's chests hold Undead Essence and Bonzo Fragments, sometimes a Hot Potato Book, Red Nose, Balloon Snake, Bonzo's Mask, Recombobulator 3000, Fuming Potato Book, Ultimate Bank I, Bonzo's Staff, Kismet Feather or Dungeon Chest Key. A better rank makes the good ones likelier. Essence goes to `/essence`.

## The Catacombs level

Catacombs XP levels you up to 50 on Hypixel's table (50 XP for level 1 ... 569,809,640 in all); each level gives 20 SkyBlock XP (40 from level 40). Classes level the same way.
