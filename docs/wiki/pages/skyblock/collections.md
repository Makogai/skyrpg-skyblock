Collections count how much of each item you have gathered. There are **83** of them, the same as on Hypixel SkyBlock: 25 Mining, 20 Farming, 15 Foraging, 12 Fishing and 11 Combat. Each has its own tiers (from 5 up to 12) with Hypixel's amounts, and each tier unlocks what it does on Hypixel.

Open the SkyBlock Menu and choose **Collections** to see your tiers.

## How collections count

A collection counts items you **gather**:

- Items you pick up from the ground, mine, harvest, chop or fish.
- What you collect from your **minions** (Collect All, taking items out of their storage, or picking the minion up).

Items you buy, craft or get from another player don't count. A collection counts its own item only: an Enchanted Cobblestone is not 160 Cobblestone for your collection.

The Mushroom collection counts both Red and Brown Mushrooms.

## What a tier gives

Every tier lists its rewards, as on Hypixel:

- **Recipes.** Most tiers unlock something in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book): the collection's minion, its enchanted forms, minion upgrades, gear. Until you reach the tier, the recipe is locked.
- **SkyBlock XP**: 4 per tier.
- Some tiers list skill experience ("+1,000 Mining Experience").

When you reach a tier you get a notice, a sound and the list of rewards in chat.

### Example: Cobblestone

| Tier | Cobblestone gathered | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 50 | Cobblestone Minion recipes |
| II | 100 | Stone Platform |
| III | 250 | Silverfish pet, Auto Smelter |
| IV | 1,000 | Enchanted Cobblestone |
| V | 2,500 | Compactor |
| VI | 5,000 | +1,000 Mining Experience |
| VII | 10,000 | Haste Ring |
| VIII | 25,000 | Hyper Furnace |
| IX | 40,000 | Haste Artifact |
| X | 70,000 | Super Compactor 3000 |

Collections are kept per [profile](profiles-and-islands). The Rift's collections come with the Rift.
