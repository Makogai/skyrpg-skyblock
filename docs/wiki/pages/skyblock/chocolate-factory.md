**The Chocolate Factory** makes chocolate every second, online or not. Open it with `/cf`. Chocolate pays for the factory's upgrades and the Chocolate Shop; the more you make, the more rabbits you collect, and the rabbits make more chocolate.

## Making chocolate

Your chocolate a second is **(employees + rabbits) x (multiplier)**. The factory screen's top cards show your chocolate (click it to bake by hand) and your production. Every card is its own button: what a click does is written at its top right.

- **Employees**: Rabbit Bro makes +1 a second for each level, Cousin +2, Sis +3, Daddy +4, Granny +5, Uncle +6 and Dog +7. Each one is hired once the one before reaches level 20. Levels get dearer as you go; the most is 120 at Factory I, 20 more for each Factory level.
- **Hand-Baked Chocolate**: clicking the chocolate gives 1, up to 10 with upgrades.
- **Rabbit Barn**: room for 20 rabbits, 2 more with each upgrade. Rabbits that don't fit are paid out as chocolate instead.
- **Time Tower** (from Factory II): three charges, one back every 8 hours. A charge adds +0.1x a level to your multiplier for an hour.
- **Rabbit Shrine** (Factory III): rarer rabbits from eggs. **Coach Jackrabbit** (Factory IV): +0.01x a level.
- **Stray rabbits**: while the factory is open, a rabbit sometimes runs through for 15 seconds. Catch it for 5 to 50 seconds' worth of chocolate (double during Hoppity's Hunt).

## Rabbits

Hypixel's 577 rabbits, each counting once you have it. The **Rabbits** card shows your collection by rarity.

| Rarity | Gives | A duplicate pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | +1 a second, +0.002x | 150 seconds of chocolate |
| Uncommon | +2 a second, +0.003x | 300 seconds |
| Rare | +4 a second, +0.004x | 750 seconds |
| Epic | +10 a second, +0.005x | 1,500 seconds |
| Legendary | +0.02x | 4,000 seconds |
| Mythic | its own perk | 8,000 seconds |
| Divine | +0.025x | 15,000 seconds |

Rabbits come from Chocolate Eggs and Hoppity (below), and from **milestones**: 26 for all-time chocolate (1k to 700B, Almond Amaretto to Zest Zephyr), 24 for chocolate spent in the shop, 100 to 2,000 duplicates, rabbit collections, Factory III, and finding 15 egg spots on an island (its own resident rabbit). Each duplicate of a rabbit pays 10% more than the one before, up to ten.

## Prestige

Make 150M chocolate (then 1B, 4B, 10B, 30B) since your last prestige to raise the factory to the next level, up to VI: a bigger multiplier (x1.1 up to x2.5), a bigger chocolate cap, rarer rabbits in eggs and new shop items. Everything but the Rabbit Barn and your rabbits starts over.

## The Chocolate Shop

Spend chocolate on Supreme Chocolate Bars, the **Egglocator** (it points the way to the nearest egg you haven't found), the chocolate Rift-time items, Dark Cacao Truffles, the Chocolate Syringe pet item and more. Each item needs a Factory level, and the shop restocks every SkyBlock year.

## Hoppity's Hunt

All spring on the [SkyBlock calendar](calendar-and-events), **Chocolate Eggs** turn up on every island at 7am, 2pm and 9pm (Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner, or Brunch, Déjeuner and Supper on alternate days). The six newest are out at once. Each player can find each egg once; right-click it to crack it open for a rabbit. Each island has 15 egg spots.

**Hoppity** stands in the Hub. Each SkyBlock year he has seven rabbits for you, for coins, during the Hunt: the first two Common, later ones likelier to be rarer (250k coins for a Common up to 5M for a Legendary).
