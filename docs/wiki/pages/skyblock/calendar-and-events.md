SkyBlock has its own calendar, and it runs on **Hypixel's clock**: the date in SkyRPG is the same as the date on Hypixel at that moment.

## How the calendar works

| Unit | How long it lasts |
| --- | --- |
| A SkyBlock hour | 50 seconds |
| A SkyBlock day | 20 minutes (one Minecraft day) |
| A month | 31 days (about 10 hours 20 minutes) |
| A year | 12 months (124 hours, about 5 days) |

The months are **Early Spring, Spring, Late Spring, Early Summer, Summer, Late Summer, Early Autumn, Autumn, Late Autumn, Early Winter, Winter** and **Late Winter**. Year 1 began on June 11, 2019.

The sky follows the calendar: at 6am on the SkyBlock clock the sun comes up, and at 7pm night falls.

## The sidebar

The **SkyBlock sidebar** on the right of your screen shows, from the top:

- the real date;
- the SkyBlock date ("Late Summer 12th");
- the time, with ☀ by day or ☽ by night;
- where you are (⏣ Your Island, ⏣ Hub, or someone else's island);
- your **purse**.

Move it, resize it or hide it in the HUD editor ([K, Settings, HUD, Edit HUD layout](hud-and-minimap#the-hud-editor)).

## Calendar and Events page

Open the K menu and choose **Calendar and Events**. It shows:

- today's SkyBlock date and time, and how long until the next day;
- all twelve months, with their events (hover a month for details); the current month has a bar showing how far through it you are;
- **Coming up**: the next events, with a countdown to each, or how long one that's running has left.
