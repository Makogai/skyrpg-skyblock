The **Hub** is where everyone meets. It's **Hypixel's own Hub map**, as it was in autumn 2022: the **Village** in the middle, with the shops, the Bank, the Auction House and the elections hall, and the areas all around it. The sidebar shows the area you're in.

| Area | Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Village](#village) | the middle | Shops, the Bank, the Auction House, the elections, the Dungeon Hub fountain |
| [Coal Mine](#coal-mine) | north | Mining: Coal and Stone; the way to the Gold Mine |
| [Farm](#farm) | north-east | Farming: Wheat; the way to The Barn |
| [Graveyard](#graveyard) | north-west | Combat: Graveyard Zombies, Zombie Villagers at night; the Hub Crypts below |
| [Forest](#forest) | west | Foraging: Oak trees; the way to The Park |
| [Ruins](#ruins) | far west | Combat: Wolves and Old Wolves |
| [Mountain](#mountain) | south | Climbing, Carrots at its foot |
| [Wizard Tower](#wizard-tower) | south-east of the Mountain | The way into the Rift |
| [Fisherman's Hut](#fisherman-s-hut) | east | Fishing on the lake |
| [Wilderness](#wilderness) | south and south-east | The Fairy Pond, the Dark Auction |
| [Colosseum](#colosseum) | far south-east | Lucius, Shifty |

Breaking blocks is only allowed where an area lets you: the Coal Mine's Coal Ore and Stone, the Farm's Wheat, the Forest's Oak logs and leaves, the Mountain's Carrots. What you break grows back after a few seconds, so there's always more. Everything else in the Hub is protected.

## The mobs

The Hub's mobs have Hypixel's names and stats. Killing one pays its **Combat XP** and **coins** straight into your purse, and counts for [slayer quests](slayers) (a Crypt Ghoul adds its 36 Combat XP to a Revenant Horror quest, not a plain zombie's 6). None of them burn in daylight.

| Mob | Where | Level | Health | Damage | Combat XP | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Graveyard Zombie | Graveyard | 1 | 100 | 20 | 6 | 1 |
| Zombie Villager | Graveyard, at night | 1 | 120 | 24 | 7 | 1 |
| Crypt Ghoul | Hub Crypts | 30 | 2,000 | 350 | 36 | 13 |
| Golden Ghoul | Hub Crypts (one at a time) | 50 | 45,000 | 800 | 50 | 100 |
| Wolf | Ruins | 15 | 250 | 90 | 15 | 1 |
| Old Wolf | Ruins (two at a time) | 50 | 15,000 | 720 | 40 | 40 |

The Ruins' wolves attack anyone who comes near. Night mobs leave at sunrise. During the [Spooky Festival](calendar-and-events#the-spooky-festival), Spooky mobs come out at night in the Graveyard and the Wilderness.

## Village

Where you arrive (`/warp hub`): the square, with the **Bank** (the gold rotunda, with the **Banker**), the **Auction House** to the west, the **Library** (the **Librarian**, and **Seraphine Vael** by the enchanting table), the **Blacksmith** and his street (the **Weaponsmith**, the **Mine Merchant**, **Rosetta**) to the north, and the elections hall (**Clerk Seraphine**, and the Mayor) north of the square. The fountain by the square is the **portal to the Dungeon Hub**: walk in.

Event visitors stand on the square too: the **Fear Mongerer** during the Spooky Festival, **Oringo** with the Traveling Zoo, the **Baker** at the New Year.

## Coal Mine

North of the village: open veins of **Coal Ore** and **Stone** (they become bedrock for a few seconds when mined). At the far end, a launch pad takes you to the [Gold Mine](the-islands#gold-mine) (needs Mining I).

## Farm

North-east of the village: fields of **Wheat** that grow back, the **Farmer**, **Arthur** (minions) and the **Farm Merchant**. A launch pad at the north end takes you to [The Barn](the-islands#the-barn) (needs Farming I).

## Graveyard

North-west of the village, dark under its dead trees: **Pat**'s house, the **Tavern** (the **Bartender**, **Maddox the Slayer**), and the graves. A launch pad at the back takes you to the [Spider's Den](the-islands#spider-s-den) (needs Combat I).

Below it run the **Hub Crypts**, where **Crypt Ghouls** roam and a **Golden Ghoul** waits. `/warp crypt` takes you there once you've been. Revenant Horror quests are best done here.

## Forest

West of the village: **Oak** trees whose logs and leaves grow back, the **Lumber Jack** and the **Lumber Merchant**. At its west edge a launch pad takes you to [The Park](the-islands#the-park) (needs Foraging I).

## Ruins

Far west, past the Park's launch pad: the old castle, where **Wolves** and **Old Wolves** roam, so Sven Packmaster quests are done here. The **Lonely Philosopher** stands on a tower. `/warp castle` takes you here once you've visited.

## Mountain

The snowy mountain south of the village. **Scoop** lives near the top, and **Carrots** grow at its foot.

## Wizard Tower

The round tower with the purple roof, south-east of the Mountain. The **Wizard** waits on its top floor beside the portal to [the Rift](the-rift). `/warp wizard` takes you to its door.

## Fisherman's Hut

East of the village, on the lake: the **Fishing Merchant** and **Fisherman Gerald**.

## Wilderness

South and south-east: the **Fairy Pond** with **Tia the Fairy**, and the **Dark Auction**, with **Sirius** at its door. `/warp da` takes you there once you've visited.

## Colosseum

The arena in the far south-east: **Lucius** and **Shifty**.

## Fairy Souls

**79 Fairy Souls** are hidden around the Hub, where Hypixel hid them: small glowing pink crystals, on rooftops, under bridges, in the crypts and up the mountain. A soul you haven't found yet sparkles. Click one to collect it: "**SOUL!** You found a Fairy Soul!", with how many you have on the Hub and in total. Each profile finds its own.

Bring them to **Tia the Fairy** at the Fairy Pond. She exchanges them five at a time: each exchange gives **10 SkyBlock XP** (see [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling)).

> [!NOTE]
> On Hypixel some exchanges also unlock Backpack slots; that comes with backpacks. The other islands have their own souls
> (see [The Islands](the-islands#fairy-souls)); Tia takes them all.
