The **Hub** is where everyone meets: the village of **Skyhaven** in the middle, with the shops, the Bank, the Bazaar and the Auction House, and seven islands around it, joined to the village by sky bridges. Each island is one of Hypixel SkyBlock's Hub areas, with the same purpose, mobs and resources, built as a floating island. The sidebar shows the area you're in.

| Area | Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Graveyard](#graveyard) | west | Combat: Graveyard Zombies, Zombie Villagers at night |
| [Coal Mine](#coal-mine) | north | Mining: Coal and Stone; the Hub Crypts below |
| [Farm](#farm) | north-east | Farming: five Wheat fields |
| [Forest](#forest) | south-west | Foraging: Oak trees |
| [Ruins](#ruins) | far west, past the Forest | Combat: Wolves and Old Wolves |
| [Mountain](#mountain) | south | Climbing, a Carrot farm inside, High Level's Skeletons |
| [Wilderness](#wilderness) | east | Fishing, the Fairy Pond, the Tavern and the Dark Auction |

Breaking blocks is only allowed where an area lets you: the Coal Mine's Coal Ore and Stone, the Farm's Wheat, the Forest's Oak logs and leaves, the Mountain's Carrots. What you break grows back after a few seconds, so there's always more. Everything else in the Hub is protected.

## The mobs

The Hub's mobs have Hypixel's names and stats. Killing one pays its **Combat XP** and **coins** straight into your purse, and counts for [slayer quests](slayers) (a Crypt Ghoul adds its 36 Combat XP to a Revenant Horror quest, not a plain zombie's 6). None of them burn in daylight.

| Mob | Where | Level | Health | Damage | Combat XP | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Graveyard Zombie | Graveyard | 1 | 100 | 20 | 6 | 1 |
| Zombie Villager | Graveyard, at night | 1 | 120 | 24 | 7 | 1 |
| Crypt Ghoul | Hub Crypts | 30 | 2,000 | 350 | 36 | 13 |
| Golden Ghoul | Hub Crypts (one at a time) | 50 | 45,000 | 800 | 50 | 100 |
| Wolf | Ruins | 15 | 250 | 90 | 15 | 1 |
| Old Wolf | Ruins (two at a time) | 50 | 15,000 | 720 | 40 | 40 |
| Skeleton | High Level, at night | 6 | 200 | 40 | 6 | 1 |

The Ruins' wolves attack anyone who comes near. Night mobs leave at sunrise.

## Graveyard

West of the village, over the bridge from its west edge. A dark island of podzol and moss: rows of graves inside an iron fence, dead trees and cobwebs, and the chapel with the **Emerald Altar**. In the chapel's west aisle, barred stairs lead down towards the Hub Crypts. **Pat**, in his house by the gate, sells Flint and Gravel. At the back, a launch pad waits for the Spider's Den (coming soon).

Revenant Horror quests are best done here: the zombies fill the bar and the boss can rise among the graves.

## Coal Mine

North of the village, reached from the Starwatch islet. A rocky hill with a timber-framed mine entrance. Inside, tunnels and three caverns of **Stone** and **Coal Ore** (both become bedrock for a few seconds when mined), with rails, posts and lanterns. A trail climbs to the summit outside.

Stairs from the first cavern lead down to the **Hub Crypts**: deepslate corridors and burial rooms where **Crypt Ghouls** roam, and a gilded room where a **Golden Ghoul** waits. `/warp crypt` takes you there once you've been to the Coal Mine. Deep in the mine, a sealed gate leads to the **Gold Mine** (coming soon).

## Farm

North-east, over the bridge from the Bazaar islet's north end. Five fields of **Wheat** that grow back (floating crystals hang above them), scarecrows, a windmill, a well and the Farmer's shack. **Arthur** stands by a Wheat Minion on display and talks about minions. A launch pad waits for The Barn (coming soon).

## Forest

South-west, over the bridge from the village's south-west edge. A thick **Oak** forest with big old oaks; logs and leaves grow back. The **Lumber Jack** lives in the log cabin near the path. A launch pad waits for The Park (coming soon), and a second bridge leads west to the Ruins.

## Ruins

Far west, over the bridge from the Forest. A ruined castle: broken walls and towers, the great hall with its fallen throne, arches and rubble, and a wolf den in the rocks. **Wolves** and **Old Wolves** roam the courtyard, so Sven Packmaster quests are done here. The **Lonely Philosopher** stands on the north-west tower. `/warp castle` takes you here once you've visited.

## Mountain

South of the village, over the bridge from the Wildgate islet. A snowy peak with a path winding to the top, where **Scoop** has his cabin. A tunnel at the foot leads into a cave of **Carrots** that grow back. On the mountainside: the arch to the Dungeon Hub (coming soon) and a hot air balloon with **Amelia** and **Christopher**.

Behind the mountain lies **High Level**: a huge wooden door between two stone knights, where **Skeletons** come out at night.

## Wilderness

East of the village, over the bridge from the Bazaar islet's east end. Meadows and trees, a stream, the **Fairy Pond** with **Tia the Fairy** on her rock, and a fishing lake with **Fisherman Gerald**'s hut and dock. **Shifty** and **Lucius** are in the Tavern; **Sirius** stands outside the Dark Auction house next door (the auction itself is coming). `/warp da` takes you there once you've visited.

## Fairy Souls

**24 Fairy Souls** are hidden around the Hub's islands (three or four on each): small glowing pink crystals, on rooftops, under bridges, in crypts and tree tops. A soul you haven't found yet sparkles. Click one to collect it: "**SOUL!** You found a Fairy Soul!", with how many you have on the Hub and in total. Each profile finds its own.

Bring them to **Tia the Fairy** at the Fairy Pond. She exchanges them five at a time: each exchange gives **10 SkyBlock XP** (see [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling)).

> [!NOTE]
> On Hypixel some exchanges also unlock Backpack slots; that comes with backpacks. Other islands will bring their own souls.

| `/warp crypt` | the Hub Crypts | the Coal Mine |

| `/warp da` | the Dark Auction | the Wilderness |
