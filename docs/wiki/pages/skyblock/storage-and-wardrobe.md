Open the K menu and choose **Storage**, or type `/storage`. Using an ender chest anywhere in the world opens it too. It has two parts: your **Wardrobe** and your **Ender Chest pages**. Each card shows a peek at what's inside, so you can see which page holds what. Click a card to open it.

Both belong to your [profile](profiles-and-islands): each profile has its own pages and its own Wardrobe.

## Ender Chest pages

Each Ender Chest page holds **45 slots**. You start with **2 pages**, and there are 9 in all. More pages will unlock with SkyBlock Leveling once it arrives.

The Ender Chest screen has a tab for every page down its left side. Click one to switch pages without closing the screen; locked pages are greyed out. The tab under them opens the Wardrobe.

- **`/ec`** opens page 1, and **`/ec <page>`** opens any page you have.
- Shift-click an ender chest to place things against it without opening Storage.
- The first time you use Storage, whatever was in your old 27-slot ender chest moves into page 1.

## Wardrobe

The Wardrobe saves up to **9 armour sets** and lets you swap between them in one click. Open it from the Storage page, the tab in the Ender Chest screen, or type **`/wardrobe`**.

Each set is a card with a **model of you wearing it**. The model turns slowly, and towards your mouse when you point at it. Point at a model to see everything that set gives you, added up.

- Under each model are the set's four slots: helmet and chestplate, then leggings and boots. Put armour in to save a set. Each slot only takes its kind of armour, and shift-clicking armour from your inventory puts it in the first free slot.
- The button under each card shows its state:
  - **Empty**: nothing saved yet.
  - **Wear**: click to put this set on.
  - **Worn** (the card glows green): you're wearing it. Click again (it says **Take off**) to take it off.
- Wearing another set puts the one you had on back into its own card first.
- If what you're wearing didn't come from the Wardrobe, it goes into your inventory, so you need room for it.

The set you're wearing stays on its card, so you can change a piece there and it changes on you too.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/storage` | Opens the Storage page. |
| `/ec [page]` | Opens an Ender Chest page (page 1 if you leave the number out). |
| `/wardrobe` | Opens the Wardrobe. |
| `/storage unlock <players> <pages>` | Admins: sets how many Ender Chest pages players have (1 to 9). |
