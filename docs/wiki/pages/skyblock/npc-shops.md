Merchants sell what they sell on Hypixel SkyBlock, at Hypixel's prices: the Farm Merchant's 3 Wheat for 7 coins, the Mine Merchant's pickaxes, the Bartender's drinks. All **88** of Hypixel's shops are in the game's data; the Hub's merchants are open now, and the rest come with their islands.

Right-click a merchant to open their shop.

## Buying

The shop shows its offers in a grid. Hover one to see what it costs: coins, and sometimes items too, each with how many you have. What you can't afford is dimmed.

- **Click** to buy one.
- **Shift-click** to buy a stack's worth.

## Selling

Every merchant buys anything that has an NPC price. Hover an item in your inventory to see what it sells for, and **click it** to sell the whole stack.

## Buying back

Your last 9 sales sit in the **Buyback** row above your inventory. Click one to buy it back for what you got for it. Buyback is kept until you log out.
