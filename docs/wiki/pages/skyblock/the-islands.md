Past the [Hub](the-hub) lie Hypixel SkyBlock's other public islands, each one floating in its own sky. Everyone shares them, like the Hub: you can break the resources they're for, and nothing else.

| Island | Way in from the Hub | Needs | Warp | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [The Barn](#the-barn) | the Farm's launch pad | Farming I | `/warp barn` | Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Pumpkins, Melons; Cows, Pigs, Chickens |
| [Mushroom Desert](#mushroom-desert) | over the bridge from the Barn | Farming V | `/warp desert` | Cactus, Sugar Cane, Cocoa, Mushrooms, Sand; Rabbits, Sheep, Mooshrooms |
| [The Park](#the-park) | the Forest's launch pad | Foraging I | `/warp park` | Birch, Spruce, Dark Oak, Acacia and Jungle wood; the Howling Cave |
| [Gold Mine](#gold-mine) | the Coal Mine's gold gate | Mining I | `/warp gold` | Stone, Coal, Iron and Gold Ore |
| [Deep Caverns](#deep-caverns) | the lift shaft at the bottom of the Gold Mine | Mining V | `/warp deep` | Six layers of ores and their mobs |
| [Spider's Den](#spider-s-den) | the Graveyard's launch pad | Combat I | `/warp spider` | Spiders, Gravel; the Spider Mound and Arachne's Sanctuary |

**Launch pads** are 3x3 pads of slime blocks with a sign saying where they go. Step on one and you're thrown to the island, if you have the skill level it needs; if not, it pushes you back and says what you're missing. Every island has a pad back to where you came from in the Hub.

**Warps** open the first time you set foot on an island (see Fast Travel in the [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu)). After that `/warp <name>` takes you straight there. Falling off an island puts you back at its arrival point.

## The mobs

They have Hypixel's names and stats, pay Combat XP and coins on the kill and count for [slayer quests](slayers): the Spider's Den's spiders for the Tarantula Broodfather, the Howling Cave's spirits for Sven Packmaster, zombies for the Revenant Horror. Neutral mobs (the Howling Cave's wolves, the Deep Caverns' pigmen) attack anyone who comes near.

## The Farming Islands

### The Barn

The first farming island, reached by the launch pad on the Hub's Farm (Farming I). A wide green island with a big red **barn**, a **windmill** and fields of **Wheat**, **Carrots** and **Potatoes**, a **Pumpkin** patch and a **Melon** patch. Crops grow back after you harvest them. **Cows** graze the pasture, **Pigs** the mud pen and **Chickens** the coop's yard. The **Farmhand** works the fields; the **Windmill Operator** at the windmill's door sells farming goods.

At the east edge, a gate opens onto the sky bridge to the Mushroom Desert (Farming V).

### Mushroom Desert

Over the bridge from the Barn. Sand and red sand dunes with **Cactus** fields, **Sugar Cane** along water channels, an oasis whose palms bear **Cocoa Beans**, and a mycelium field with a mushroom cave full of **Red** and **Brown Mushrooms** and **Mooshrooms**. Plain **Sand** on the dunes can be dug (it comes back). A desert settlement holds **Jake**'s shop; **Trevor** the trapper has his den to the north, the **Shepherd** minds the sheep pen and **Farmer Jon** keeps the cactus fields.

## The Park

The foraging island, through the launch pad in the Hub's Forest (Foraging I). A group of forest islands, each with its own wood, all of it choppable and regrowing:

- **Birch Park** (where you arrive): a bright birch wood around a lake. **Charlie** and **Vanessa**.
- **Spruce Woods**, to the west: tall spruces and a viking camp with **Kelly**, **Gustave** and the **Melancholic Viking**.
- **The Howling Cave**, north of the Spruce Woods: a dark cavern where **Pack Spirits**, **Howling Spirits** and the **Soul of the Alpha** roam. **Old Shaman Nyko** keeps a fire at the entrance and sells his wares.
- **Dark Thicket**, east over the bridge: dense dark oak, and the Campfire Cult's clearing with **Ryan** and the **Campfire Adept**.
- **Savanna Woodland**: acacia trees and a camp with **Melody**, **Master Tactician Funk** (a shop) and **Worker Xavier**.
- **Jungle Island**: towering jungle trees and an old temple, with **Molbert** and **Juliette**.

## Gold Mine

Through the gold-trimmed gate deep in the Hub's Coal Mine (Mining I). A huge open-pit mine in terraces, ramps winding down to the bottom, with tunnels and caverns off them. **Stone**, **Coal Ore**, **Iron Ore** and **Gold Ore** turn to bedrock for a few seconds when mined, then come back. The miners' camp at the top has **Rusty**, the **Lazy Miner**, the **Iron Forger** and the **Gold Forger** (both shops).

At the bottom of the pit, a lift shaft goes down to the Deep Caverns (Mining V).

## Deep Caverns

A mountain of rock with six caverns stacked inside it, each with its own ore and mobs, joined by spiral ramps:

| Layer | Ores | Mobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gunpowder Mines | Coal, Iron, Gold | Sneaky Creepers |
| Lapis Quarry | Lapis | Lapis Zombies |
| Pigmen's Den | Redstone | Redstone Pigmen |
| Slimehill | Emerald | Emerald Slimes |
| Diamond Reserve | Diamond | Miner Zombies and Skeletons |
| Obsidian Sanctuary | Diamond, Obsidian, Diamond Blocks | stronger Miner Zombies and Skeletons |

**Walter** sells supplies at the entrance at the top. On every layer a **Lift Operator** stands in the lift cage in the middle: talk to one to ride the lift to any layer you've already walked into. The **Lapis Miner**, the **Redstone Miner** and **Rhys** (at the shrine in the Obsidian Sanctuary) live down here too. A launch pad at the top takes you back to the Gold Mine.

## Spider's Den

Through the launch pad behind the Hub's Graveyard (Combat I). A dark island of cobwebs and dead trees around the **Spider Mound**, a huge hill that a path winds up. Spiders crawl everywhere; at the top of the mound they're level 42. **Gravel** can be dug on the slopes and in the gravel pit, where **Gravel Skeletons** lurk. Under the mound, a stair goes down to **Arachne's Sanctuary**, a webbed chamber with an altar (Arachne herself is coming).

The camp by the arrival has **Grandma Wolf**, **Haymitch**, **Rick**, **Shaggy** (a shop) and the **Archaeologist**.

## Fairy Souls

Every island hides its own [Fairy Souls](the-hub#fairy-souls), four on each part: 8 on the Farming Islands, 8 in The Park, 4 in the Gold Mine, 4 in the Deep Caverns and 4 in the Spider's Den. Some sit in plain sight; others are on roofs, in tree tops, in caves or behind cobwebs. Nothing needs to be broken to reach them. **Tia the Fairy** in the Hub exchanges them all.
