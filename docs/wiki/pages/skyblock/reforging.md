Every weapon, armour piece, tool and piece of equipment can be made better than it came: a **reforge** in front of its name gives it bonus stats, a **Recombobulator 3000** raises its rarity, and **Potato Books** add flat stats. All of it stays on the item, as on Hypixel, and shows on its tooltip.

## The Blacksmith

The **Blacksmith** works at Rosetta's forge in the Hub village. Talk to him to open **Reforge Item**: put the item in the top slot and press **Reforge**.

With nothing in the second slot he gives the item a **random reforge** that fits it (never the one it already has), for coins from your purse by the item's rarity:

| Rarity | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 250 |
| Uncommon | 500 |
| Rare | 1,000 |
| Epic | 2,500 |
| Legendary | 5,000 |
| Mythic | 10,000 |
| Divine | 15,000 |
| Special | 25,000 |
| Very Special | 50,000 |

There are 50 of his reforges, each for one kind of item: swords (Epic, Fair, Fast, Gentle, Heroic, Legendary, Odd, Sharp, Spicy...), bows (Awkward, Deadly, Fine, Grand, Hasty, Neat, Rapid, Rich, Unreal...), armour (Clean, Fierce, Heavy, Light, Mythic, Pure, Smart, Titanic, Wise...), equipment, pickaxes, axes, farming tools and fishing rods. Their stats grow with the item's rarity.

## Reforge stones

Hypixel's 86 **reforge stones** each carry one special reforge (Warped for the Aspect of the End, Ambered for pickaxes, Ancient, Necrotic, Spiritual, Renowned...). Most of them are on the [Bazaar](bazaar). Put the stone in the Blacksmith's second slot: the card shows the item with its new reforge and the price, and **Reforge** applies it and uses the stone. A stone only fits the items its reforge is for; the card says so when it doesn't.

Some stone reforges have a special bonus as well as stats ("Ambered Bonus: find Goblins from mining 40% more often"); the tooltip shows it.

## Reforge abilities

The reforge of the item in your main hand and of each armour piece you wear counts; every item with the reforge adds its bonus again.

| Reforge | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient | +1 Crit Damage per Catacombs level |
| Withered | +1 Strength per Catacombs level |
| Loving | +5% Ability Damage |
| Perfect | +2% Defense (of what your held item and armour give) |
| Renowned | +1 Magic Find and +1% of your Strength, Crit Damage, Crit Chance, Defense and Health from items |
| Empowered | +10 Mending in the Catacombs |
| Fruitful | +75 Defense on the mining islands |
| Fleet | +50 Mining Spread |
| Auspicious | +1% Mining Fortune |
| Calcified | +25 Defense against sea creatures |
| Cubic | -2% damage from the mobs of Crimson Isle and Kuudra |
| Undead | -2% damage from undead |
| Fanged | Every 7th melee hit deals +100% damage |
| Headstrong, Precise | +8% and +10% damage for an arrow that hits a mob's head |
| Buzzing | x2 damage against Garden pests |
| Fabled | A crit deals up to 15% more damage |
| Gilded | A kill has a 1% chance to pay 50,000 coins |
| Bountiful | +0.2 coins for every crop you harvest in the Garden |
| Magnetic | Double the XP orbs from mining |
| Refined, Blessed | 0.25% chance for the enchanted form of a mined ore or a harvested Garden crop |
| Stiff | A sea creature you kill drops +1 Chum |

> [!NOTE]
> The other special bonuses (Heat, Cold, the Wisp Pet, Griffin burrows, the Feast, the Spooky Festival, Mining Events and so on) are shown on the tooltip but don't work yet; the stats do.

## The anvil

Any anvil (in the Hub or on your [island](profiles-and-islands)) opens **Combine Items**: the item in the top slot, the upgrade in the second, and **Combine**.

| Upgrade | What it does | Goes on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recombobulator 3000 | One rarity up (Common to Uncommon... Mythic to Divine, Special to Very Special), once per item. A reforge's stats follow the new rarity. | Weapons, armour, equipment, accessories, tools and wands |
| Hot Potato Book | **+2 Damage and +2 Strength** on weapons and fishing rods, **+4 Health and +2 Defense** on armour. Up to 10 per item. | Weapons, fishing rods, armour |
| Fuming Potato Book | The same, past the tenth book: up to 15 in all. | Weapons, fishing rods, armour |

All three are on the Bazaar.

## On the tooltip

A reforged item's name starts with its reforge ("Fierce Aspect of the End"). Each stat line shows the total, then what the Potato Books add in yellow and what the reforge adds in blue, as on Hypixel. A recombobulated item's rarity line has flickering marks at both ends. An item that can still be reforged says "This item can be reforged!" above its rarity line.
