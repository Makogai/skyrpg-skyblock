Every SkyBlock year (124 real hours) SkyBlock elects a new **Mayor**. The Mayor's perks change how the whole game plays for that year, and the runner-up becomes **Minister** with one of their perks.

## The election

- An election **opens on the 27th of Late Summer** and **closes on the 27th of Late Spring** the next year. The winner takes office as soon as it closes.
- There are **five candidates**. Each runs with between one and four of their perks, and one of those perks is marked for the Minister.
- Last term's Mayor and Minister don't run again (unless Diaz's Long Term Investment brings the Minister back).
- **Every 8 years** a special candidate runs with all of their perks: Jerry, then Scorpius, then Derpy.

**Vote** with **Clerk Seraphine** on the Hub plaza, or with `/election`. You get one vote, and you can change it any time until the election closes. `/mayor` shows the perks in effect right now.

## Diana's Mythological Ritual

While Diana is Mayor (or her perk is in effect), **Griffin burrows** appear on the Hub's grass. **Diana**, on the Hub plaza while her ritual is on (she isn't there otherwise), sells the **Griffin** pet (25,000 coins) and the **Ancestral Spade** (5,000 coins and 32 Gold Ingots).

1. **Hold a spade in the Hub** and three **start burrows** (green sparks) appear on the grass near you.
2. **Left-click a burrow** with the spade to dig it. That starts a **chain** of 4 burrows (the Archaic Spade makes it 6, the Deific Spade 8).
3. **Right-click the spade (Echo)** to see a trail of particles pointing to the next burrow. The trail is yellow when it's close, red when it's further, and grey when it's far away.
4. Each later burrow is either **treasure** (gold sparks) or a **mob** (red sparks).
   - **Treasure:** Griffin Feathers, coins (up to 1,000,000 with the Deific Spade), and with better spades Mythos Fragments and the very rare Braided Griffin Feather. The Crown of Greed doubles the coins.
   - **Mob:** beat it and the burrow counts. You need your Griffin pet out to dig a mob burrow.

The **Griffin's rarity** decides which **Mythological mobs** can come out, and how strong they are:

| Griffin | New mobs |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Minos Hunter, Siamese Lynxes |
| Uncommon | Stranded Nymph, Cretan Bull |
| Rare | Harpy, Gaia Construct |
| Epic | Minotaur, Minos Champion |
| Legendary | Sphinx, Minos Inquisitor |
| Mythic | Manticore, King Minos |

Only a Griffin at least as rare as the one that called a mob can hurt it. Every mob drops Ancient Claws and often Enchanted Gold. Each one also has a rare drop, such as the Daedalus Stick (Minotaur), the Minos Relic (Minos Champion), the Chimera book (Minos Inquisitor) or the Crown of Greed (King Minos). The four elusive mobs (Inquisitor, Sphinx, Manticore, King Minos) come more often with **Tracking**.

## Marina's Fishing Festival

While Marina is Mayor, the **first 3 days of every SkyBlock month** are a Fishing Festival. During it, 15% of the sea creatures you catch in water are **sharks** (Shark Bait adds 20%):

| Shark | Fishing level | Health | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nurse Shark | 5 | 2,500 | Shark Fins, Nurse Shark Tooth |
| Blue Shark | 10 | 25,000 | Shark Fins, Blue Shark Tooth |
| Tiger Shark | 18 | 250,000 | Shark Fins, Tiger Shark Tooth, Epic Megalodon (1%) |
| Great White Shark | 24 | 1,500,000 | Shark Fins, Great White Shark Tooth, Legendary Megalodon (1%) |

## Cole's Mining Fiesta

While Cole is Mayor, the whole term is a **Mining Fiesta**. On top of +25 Mining Wisdom and Mining Fortune, ores and Dwarven Metals can drop **Refined Minerals**, and gemstones can drop **Glossy Gemstones**. The chance is better on harder blocks: 0.02% for Coal up to 0.18% for Glacite.

## Scorpius's bribe

If **Scorpius** wins and you voted for him, claim a bribe at the Election. It's worth more the longer you've played: 50,000 coins under 50 hours, up to 1,000,000 coins from 300 hours.
