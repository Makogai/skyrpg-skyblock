Every SkyBlock year (124 real hours) SkyBlock elects a new **Mayor**. The Mayor's perks change how the whole game plays for that year, and the runner-up becomes **Minister** with one of their perks.

## The election

- An election **opens on the 27th of Late Summer** and **closes on the 27th of Late Spring** the next year. The winner takes office as soon as it closes.
- There are **five candidates**. Each runs with between one and four of their perks, and one of those perks is marked for the Minister.
- Last term's Mayor and Minister don't run again (unless Diaz's Long Term Investment brings the Minister back).
- **Every 8 years** a special candidate runs with all of their perks: Jerry, then Scorpius, then Derpy.

**Vote** with **Clerk Seraphine** on the Hub plaza, or with `/election`. You get one vote, and you can change it any time until the election closes. `/mayor` shows the perks in effect right now.

## The mayors

| Mayor | Perks |
| --- | --- |
| **Aatrox** (Slayer) | SLASHED Pricing (Slayer quests half price), Slayer XP Buff (+25% Slayer XP), Pathfinder (+20 Magic Find) |
| **Cole** (Mining) | Prospection (mining minions 25% faster), Mining XP Buff (+60 Mining Wisdom), Mining Fiesta, Molten Forge (forging 25% faster) |
| **Diana** (Pets) | Huntress' Intuition (+10 Tracking), Mythological Ritual, Pet XP Buff (+35% pet XP), Sharing is Caring |
| **Diaz** (Economist) | Long Term Investment, Volume Trading (Shen's Auctions sell twice as many), Stock Exchange, Shopping Spree |
| **Finnegan** (Farming) | Pest Eradicator (pests 20% more often), GOATed, Blooming Business, Grand Feast |
| **Foxy** (Events) | Sweet Benevolence, Extra Event, A Time for Giving, Chivalrous Carnival |
| **Marina** (Fishing) | Fishing XP Buff (+50 Fishing Wisdom), Luck of the Sea 2.0 (+15 Sea Creature Chance), Fishing Festival, Double Trouble |
| **Paul** (Dungeons) | Marauder (dungeon chests 20% cheaper), EZPZ (+10 Dungeon score), Benediction (blessings 25% stronger) |
| **Jerry** (special) | Perkpocalypse (another mayor's perks every 18 SkyBlock days), Statspocalypse (+10% combat stats), Jerrypocalypse |
| **Scorpius** (special) | Bribe, Darker Auctions |
| **Derpy** (special) | QUAD TAXES!!!, TURBO MINIONS!!!, DOUBLE MOBS HP!!!, MOAR SKILLZ!!! (+50% skill XP) |

Wisdom perks (Mining, Fishing) only count on public islands. The mayor events (Diana's Mythological Ritual, Marina's Fishing Festival, Cole's Mining Fiesta, Foxy's Carnival) and a few perks that need them are still to come.

## Scorpius's bribe

If **Scorpius** wins and you voted for him, claim a bribe at the Election. It's worth more the longer you've played: 50,000 coins under 50 hours, up to 1,000,000 coins from 300 hours.
