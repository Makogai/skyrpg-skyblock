**Accessories** (talismans, rings, artifacts and relics) give their stats just by being yours. Keep them in the **Accessory Bag**, where they also give **Magical Power**, which powers up the **Accessory Power** you pick at **Maxwell**. **Equipment** (necklaces, cloaks, belts, gloves and bracelets) is worn in its own four slots.

## The Accessory Bag

Open it from the **Accessory Bag** tile in the [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu), or with `/accessories`. It unlocks at **Redstone collection II** and grows by **6 slots** with every Redstone tier after that (see [Collections](collections)), and more from the Redstone Miner and Jacobus (see [Extra bag slots](#extra-bag-slots)). Only accessories go in.

Every accessory you have counts for its stats, in the bag or in your inventory, with two rules:

- **Each accessory once:** two Speed Rings give one Speed Ring's stats.
- **Only the highest of a line:** with a Speed Ring, the Speed Talisman stops counting; with a Speed Artifact, both do.

## Magical Power

Each accessory **in the bag** (once, and the highest of its line) gives Magical Power by its rarity. A [Recombobulator 3000](reforging) raises it.

| Rarity | Magical Power |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 3 |
| Uncommon | 5 |
| Rare | 8 |
| Epic | 12 |
| Legendary | 16 |
| Mythic | 22 |
| Special | 3 |
| Very Special | 5 |

The bag's title shows your total. Every point of Magical Power is **1 SkyBlock XP**, paid once (it only counts upwards, so taking an accessory out and putting it back gives nothing twice). Some accessories are special:

- **Hegemony Artifact:** gives **double** its own Magical Power (32 at Legendary, 44 after a Recombobulator).
- **Abicase:** **+1 Magical Power for every 2 contacts**. Your contacts are the NPCs you have talked to, up to 80. Only one Abicase counts.
- **Rift Prism:** bring one to Maxwell's **Bag Slots** tab and imbue it for 200,000 coins: it is used up for good and gives **11 Magical Power**.
- **Dungeon accessories** give **double** inside the Catacombs.

## Accessory Powers (Maxwell)

**Maxwell** stands beside Seraphine in the Hub (the bag's crystal tab opens him too). He shows every power with what it gives you at your Magical Power. Click one to make it **your power**: its stats are multiplied by your Magical Power, 29.97 × ln(0.0019 × MP + 1)^1.2 (about ×0.44 at 16 Magical Power, ×9.6 at 250 and ×26 at 750).

- **Starter powers** (Fortuitous, Pretty, Protected, Simple, Warrior) are yours from the start; the others (Commando, Disciplined, Inspired, Ominous, Prepared) need Combat XV.
- **Stone powers** (Bloody, Silky, Sweet, Shaded, Forceful, Adept, Bizarre and 14 more) are learned by giving Maxwell **9 of their Power Stone**. They also have a **unique bonus** that doesn't scale (Bloody: +10 Bonus Attack Speed). All of them are on the [Bazaar](bazaar).

### Stats Tuning

Maxwell's **Stats Tuning** tab turns Magical Power into stats of your choice: every **10 Magical Power is 1 Tuning Point**. Put points on a stat with **+** (right-click a card, or hold Shift for ten) and take them back with **-** for free. **Reset points** takes all of them back.

| Stat | Per point |
| --- | --- |
| Health | +5 |
| Defense | +1 |
| Speed | +1.5 |
| Strength | +1 |
| Crit Damage | +1 |
| Crit Chance | +0.2 |
| Bonus Attack Speed | +0.3 |
| Intelligence | +2 |

Points stay with the profile. If you lose Magical Power, the points past what it pays for stop counting. One **Tuning Template** (Hypixel's first one, which is free) saves your spread and loads it back.

## Enrichments

An **Accessory Enrichment** adds a little of one stat to an accessory that is **Legendary or better** (after a Recombobulator too). Put the accessory in the first slot of an **Anvil** and the Enrichment in the second, like a Recombobulator. The bonus shows in the tooltip in dark aqua, and a new Enrichment replaces the old one.

| Enrichment | Gives |
| --- | --- |
| Speed | +1 |
| Intelligence | +2 |
| Critical Damage | +1 |
| Critical Chance | +1 |
| Strength | +1 |
| Defense | +1 |
| Health | +3 |
| Magic Find | +0.5 |
| Ferocity | +0.3 |
| Sea Creature Chance | +0.3 |
| Attack Speed | +0.5 |

The **Accessory Enrichment Swapper** changes the enrichment of **every** enriched accessory in your Accessory Bag: Shift + right-click picks which one, right-click swaps and uses it up. Hypixel sells Enrichments for Bits, which SkyRPG does not have, so for now they come from admins (`/sbitem`).

## Extra bag slots

The Maxwell screen's **Bag Slots** tab shows where your bag's slots come from. Jacobus and the Redstone Miner open it too.

- **Redstone collection:** 6 slots a tier from II, 48 at most.
- **Redstone Miner** (Deep Caverns, needs Redstone II): **4 slots**, for 128, then 256, 512 and 1,024 Redstone.
- **Jacobus** (Hub, by Maxwell): **2 slots a purchase, 99 purchases (198 slots)**, for coins: 1.5M, then 5M for the 2nd to 5th, 8M for the 6th to 10th, 12M up to the 20th, and 20M after that. He gives you his **Jacobus Register** after the fifth.

Each upgrade is **2 SkyBlock XP**. Learning a Power at Maxwell is 15 SkyBlock XP each.

## What accessories do

Besides their stats, these work from the bag or your inventory (the highest of a line only):

| Accessory | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Zombie Talisman, Ring, Artifact | 5%, 10%, 15% less damage from Undead |
| Spider Talisman, Ring, Artifact | 5%, 10%, 15% less damage from Arthropods |
| Sea Creature Talisman, Ring, Artifact | 5%, 10%, 15% less damage from Sea Creatures |
| Wolf Talisman, Ring | 5%, 10% less damage from Animals |
| Skeleton Talisman | 5% less damage from Skeletons |
| Blaze Talisman, Nether Artifact | 5% less damage from Infernal mobs |
| Ender Artifact, Relic | 20%, 25% less damage from Ender mobs |
| Wither Artifact, Relic | 20%, 25% less damage from Wither mobs |
| Feather Talisman, Ring, Artifact | Fall 5, 7, 10 blocks further before it hurts (the Ring and Artifact also cut the rest by 5% and 15%) |
| Haste Ring, Artifact | Permanent Haste II, III |
| Night Vision Charm | Night Vision |
| Fire Talisman | No fire damage |
| Scavenger Talisman, Ring, Artifact | 0.5, 0.6, 0.75 coins per level of what you kill |

The Slayer levels some rings and artifacts need on Hypixel are not checked.

## Equipment

Necklaces, cloaks, belts, gloves and bracelets go in the four equipment slots next to your armour in the inventory: **Necklace**, **Cloak**, **Belt** and **Gloves** (gloves or a bracelet). Their stats count while worn, with their reforge and Potato Books. Equipment takes the Blacksmith's equipment reforges (Astute, Blended, Brilliant...).
