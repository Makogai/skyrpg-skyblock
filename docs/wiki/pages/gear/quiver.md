Your arrows live in your **Quiver**, not your inventory. Arrows you pick up, buy or are given go into it by themselves (45 slots). Type **`/quiver`** to see it, take arrows out or put them in.

## Shooting

A bow shoots from your quiver: **one arrow a shot**, of the type you picked (or the first type in the quiver). Hold an **Arrow Swapper** and right-click to pick the next type, sneak and right-click for the one before. The Quiver screen shows which type your bows shoot.

**Infinite Quiver** on a bow saves the arrow 3% of the time per level.

## Arrow damage

A SkyBlock bow's arrow hits for **5 + the bow's Damage + the arrow's Damage**, less if the bow wasn't fully drawn, then your Strength and crits count as for a melee hit.
