Every tool in SkyRPG SkyBlock is a Hypixel SkyBlock tool with its real name, rarity and stats. This page covers the kinds, which of their stats work today, and how to get one. The full list is in [Items: tools](items-tools).

That is 151 tools in all. Their tooltips use Hypixel's layout: name in the rarity colour, stats in Hypixel's order, lore and the bold rarity line, such as **EPIC PICKAXE**.

## What works today

A tool's stats count while it is in your main hand.

| Stat | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Mining Speed | Makes you break blocks faster: 100 Mining Speed is twice as fast. See [Mining](mining) |
| Mining Fortune, Farming Fortune, Foraging Fortune | Extra drops from ores, grown crops and logs. See [Stats](stats#fortune) |
| Fishing Speed | Fish bite sooner while fishing. See [Fishing](fishing) |
| Damage, Strength | A pickaxe, axe or rod with Damage hits like a weapon |

> [!NOTE]
> These are shown on tools but do nothing yet: **Breaking Power** (every tool breaks every block the zones let you break), Pristine, Mining Spread, **Ore, Block, Dwarven Metal and Gemstone Fortune**, Sea Creature Chance and Fishing Fortune, the crop-specific Fortunes (Wheat, Carrot, Cocoa Beans and so on), drill fuel and abilities such as Mining Speed Boost. Hypixel's item data carries no ability text.

## Never wearing out

SkyBlock tools never break and never need repairing. They are always unbreakable, as on Hypixel.

## Reforges

Pickaxes, axes, farming tools and fishing rods each have their own reforges, rolled at the Blacksmith for coins or applied from a reforge stone (the Ambered stone is for pickaxes). Fishing rods also take Hot Potato Books. See [Reforging](reforging).

## Where to get one

- **NPC shops** sell 30 tools, mostly the starter ones such as the Rookie tools. See [NPC shops](npc-shops).
- **Crafting** makes 72 tools from Hypixel's recipes. See the [Recipe Book](recipe-book).
- The **Auction House** lets players sell tools to each other. See [Auction House](auction-house).
- Admins can give any item with `/sbitem`.

In all, 91 of the 151 tools have a shop offer or a crafting recipe. Drills and the later pickaxes need the Dwarven Mines, which are not built yet.

## Where to use them

Tools work in the Hub's and the islands' working areas: the Coal Mine, Farm, Forest, The Barn, The Park, the Gold Mine, Deep Caverns and Spider's Den. See [The Hub](the-hub) and [The Islands](the-islands). On your Private Island you can break and place anything.
