All of Hypixel SkyBlock's armour is in the game: **269 helmets, 191 chestplates, 183 leggings and 191 boots**, with Hypixel's names, rarities and stats. With FurfSky Reborn installed, each piece looks the way it does on Hypixel. This page explains how armour works today. The full list is in [Items: armour](items-armour), and every piece has its own page.

## How armour works

- **Armour counts in its own slot.** A helmet held in your hand gives nothing. Wear a piece on the head, chest, legs or feet to get its stats.
- **Stats add up.** Health, Defense, Strength, Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Speed, Intelligence, Magic Find and every other stat on a worn piece goes into your totals (a few, such as Ferocity, have no effect yet), along with your skills, slayer levels and SkyBlock Level. Open **Stats** in the menu to see where each point comes from.
- **No vanilla armour points.** Defense replaces them: damage you take is multiplied by `100 / (100 + Defense)`. See [Stats](stats#defense).
- **It never wears out.** SkyBlock armour is always unbreakable, so it needs no repairs.
- **Looks.** Heads show their own skin and leather armour keeps its colour. FurfSky Reborn gives the rest their Hypixel textures. See [SkyBlock items](skyblock-items#how-an-item-looks).

The tooltip is Hypixel's: the stats in Hypixel's order, the lore, and the rarity line in bold, such as **LEGENDARY HELMET**.

## Set bonuses

> [!NOTE]
> Hypixel's set bonuses and full-set abilities (Strong Blood, Superior Blood, Protective Blood and the rest) are not in the game yet. Hypixel's item data has stats and a little lore but no set or ability text, so wearing every piece of a set gives you the pieces' stats and nothing more. They arrive as their own systems are built.

Other things some pieces list on Hypixel, such as dungeon stars, gemstone slots and the requirements for dungeon armour, are in the data but do nothing yet.

## Reforges, Recombobulators and Potato Books

Armour can be improved without changing what it is:

- The **Blacksmith** in the Hub gives a piece a random armour reforge (Clean, Fierce, Heavy, Light, Mythic, Pure, Smart, Titanic, Wise...) for coins, and **reforge stones** give a chosen one.
- At any anvil, a **Recombobulator 3000** raises a piece one rarity.
- **Hot Potato Books** add **+4 Health and +2 Defense** each, up to 10, and **Fuming Potato Books** take it to 15.

See [Reforging](reforging).

## The Wardrobe

The [Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe#wardrobe) holds 9 armour sets and swaps a whole set in one click. It opens at SkyBlock Level 5.

## Where to get armour

- **NPC shops** sell 91 pieces, the starter and mid-level sets. See [NPC shops](npc-shops).
- **Crafting** makes 397 pieces from Hypixel's recipes. See the [Recipe Book](recipe-book), which also shows what unlocks each (a collection tier or a slayer level).
- **Slayer drops** feed the slayer armour recipes. See [Slayers](slayers).
- The **Auction House** lets players sell armour to each other. See [Auction House](auction-house).
- Admins can give any item with `/sbitem`.

In all, 480 of the 834 pieces have a shop offer or a crafting recipe. Dungeon, Crimson Isle and other late sets need places that are not built yet.
