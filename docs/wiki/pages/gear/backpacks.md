Backpacks give you more room to carry things. You carry up to **three** at once, each in its own slot, and you open them on the move without putting anything down.

> [!NOTE]
> This is the survival game's backpack system, kept as a stand-in. Hypixel's backpacks (70 items, from Small to Jumbo, in many colours) are in the game as items, and the [Recipe Book](recipe-book) shows their recipes, but they do nothing yet: they will live in [Storage](storage-and-wardrobe) when that part is built. The five packs below can no longer be crafted, sold or upgraded, so only an admin can hand you one.

## The slots

Three backpack slots sit in a strip to the right of the inventory in the E screen (and on the creative inventory's Survival Inventory tab). Like the accessory strip, it hides while the recipe book is open.

- Put a backpack in a slot: click it in, or shift-click it from your inventory.
- Right-click the slot to open that backpack. Press **B** to open the first one (you can rebind the key under SkyRPG in Controls).
- Backpacks cannot go inside backpacks.
- A pack's contents are stored on the pack item, so it keeps them wherever it goes. Backpacks you carry are stored with you, so you keep them on death. The tooltip shows how full a pack is.
