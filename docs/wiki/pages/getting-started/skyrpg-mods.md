SkyRPG is not one big mod but **31 small ones** that work together, each adding one part of the game. You never install them one by one: the pack brings all of them (see [Welcome](welcome)). You'll see them in **Mod Menu** (the Mods button on the title screen) and in your launcher.

The pack also installs two client-side extras that dress SkyBlock's items: **Catharsis**, which reads each item's SkyBlock id, and the **FurfSky Reborn** resource pack, which gives the items their look. Both are always on. See [Mods and shaders](mods-and-shaders).
