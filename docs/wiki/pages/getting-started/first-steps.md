You start on your **Private Island**, a small floating island of grass with an oak tree and a portal to the Hub. This page is a route through your first hour: what to open, where to walk, what to collect and where the first coins come from. It follows the same loop as Hypixel SkyBlock: gather, unlock, craft, sell, repeat.

## Minute 0: your island

You appear on your island with a welcome in chat. Only you (and the members of a [co-op](profiles-and-islands#co-ops)) can build here. Look at the sidebar on the right: it shows the SkyBlock date and time, the area you are in and your purse. The minimap is in the top right corner, and **M** opens the world map.

Press **K** to open the **SkyBlock Menu**. It is the board for everything: Skills, Collections, Recipe Book, Storage, Fast Travel and more. See [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu). Press **K** again to close it.

`/is` brings you back to your island from anywhere.

## Into the Hub

Walk through the **portal** on the north side of your island. You arrive in the **Hub**, in the village of Skyhaven, where you walk 60% faster. Nobody can hurt you in the village, and nobody builds here. Take a minute to look at the town: the shops, the Bank, and the NPCs with their names over their heads. See [Skyhaven](skyhaven) and [NPCs](npcs).

You can buy a few things right away. Every merchant also buys anything with an NPC price: click an item in the shop's sell panel to sell the whole stack. See [NPC Shops](npc-shops).

## The Hub's areas

Sky bridges lead from the village to seven areas, each with something to gather or fight. All of them are in [The Hub](the-hub). The first four are the ones to learn the skills on:

| Area | Skill | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farm (north-east) | Farming | Harvest Wheat. It grows back |
| Forest (south-west) | Foraging | Chop Oak. Logs and leaves grow back |
| Coal Mine (north) | Mining | Mine Coal Ore and Stone. They turn to bedrock for a few seconds, then return |
| Graveyard (west) | Combat | Kill Graveyard Zombies. Each pays Combat XP and a coin |

Every break and kill gives **skill XP**. The first level of each skill is quick, and it is the key to the launch pads: Farming I, Foraging I, Mining I and Combat I each open the way to a public island (see the [Progression guide](progression-guide)).

## Collections, recipes and your first minion

Everything you gather counts toward a **collection**. Open **Collections** in the SkyBlock Menu to see them: gather enough of an item and you reach the next tier, which unlocks recipes and gives SkyBlock XP. See [Collections](collections).

Open the **Recipe Book** to see what you can craft. Crafting tables and `/craft` open a 3 by 3 grid; the Recipe Book fills it for you with **Supercraft**. See [Recipe Book](recipe-book).

Your first **minion** is a craft away: a few Cobblestone or Wheat, a few tiers of collection and the recipe from the Recipe Book. Place it on your island, give it room, and come back later to **Collect All**: it works while you are away. See [Minions](minions). Arthur, at the Farm, tells you about them.

## Your first coins

Coins sit in your **purse**, shown in the sidebar. Early on they come from:

1. **Selling.** Wheat, logs, ore and mob drops all have an NPC price. Click them in a merchant's sell panel. See [NPC Shops](npc-shops).
2. **Mobs.** Every kill in the Hub's areas pays a few coins.
3. **Skill levels.** Each level pays coins as well as stats.
4. **Minions.** What they collect can be sold, or kept for crafting.
5. **The Bazaar**, from **SkyBlock Level 7**: sell materials in bulk at live prices. See [Bazaar](bazaar).

Put coins you will not spend into the Bank, where they are safe when you die. The **Banker** stands in the middle of the village. See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).

> [!TIP]
> When you die you keep your items but lose half of your purse. Spend or bank your coins before a risky fight.

## Tia and the Fairy Souls

**24 Fairy Souls** are hidden around the Hub's islands, and more on the public islands: small glowing pink crystals, on rooftops, under bridges and in tree tops. Click one to collect it. **Tia the Fairy**, at the Fairy Pond in the Wilderness, turns every five into **10 SkyBlock XP**. See [The Hub](the-hub#fairy-souls).

## A good first hour

1. Press K and look at the SkyBlock Menu once.
2. Walk through the portal and meet the village's merchants. Find the Banker and the Bartender.
3. Cross to the Farm, the Forest and the Coal Mine and gather until each skill is at level 1 or 2.
4. Kill a few zombies in the Graveyard for Combat XP.
5. Sell what you gathered, and check the Collections page for new tiers.
6. Craft your first minion from the Recipe Book and place it on your island.
7. Go back to the Hub and hunt Fairy Souls on your way to Tia.
8. Open the Settings page once and glance at the HUD and Map options. See [Settings](settings).

> [!WARNING]
> Some fights are tougher than they look. The Ruins' wolves attack on sight, and the Hub Crypts' Golden Ghoul has 45,000 health. Stay on the Graveyard and the Farm until your Combat level is up.
