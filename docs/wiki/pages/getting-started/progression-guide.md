SkyRPG follows Hypixel SkyBlock's loop: **gather, unlock, craft, earn, go further.** There is no single route, and this page lays out one in six stages: what to gather, which gates open and what to aim for. The levels are the ones the game itself checks: the skill levels on the launch pads, the SkyBlock Level that opens the Bazaar and the Combat levels the slayer tiers need. Everything else is a rough guide.

## How the loop works

- **Gathering** gives skill XP, collection progress and coins. Each skill level gives stats, coins and SkyBlock XP. See [Skills overview](skills-overview).
- **Collections** count everything you gather. Each tier unlocks recipes, above all your minions, and gives SkyBlock XP. See [Collections](collections).
- **Crafting** turns gathered items into enchanted items, minions and gear. The [Recipe Book](recipe-book) lists every recipe and what unlocks it.
- **Minions** gather while you are away, so your island earns for you. See [Minions](minions).
- **Selling** pays for the next step: the NPC shops buy anything with an NPC price, the [Bazaar](bazaar) trades materials at live prices and the [Auction House](auction-house) trades gear. See [NPC Shops](npc-shops).
- **SkyBlock Level** rises with nearly everything you do and opens features along the way. See [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling).

## The gates

Every public island is behind a launch pad or gate that asks for a skill level. If you don't have it, you are pushed back and told what is missing.

| Island | Way in | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Barn](the-islands#the-barn) | Launch pad on the Hub's Farm | Farming I |
| [The Park](the-islands#the-park) | Launch pad in the Hub's Forest | Foraging I |
| [Gold Mine](the-islands#gold-mine) | Gold gate deep in the Hub's Coal Mine | Mining I |
| [Spider's Den](the-islands#spider-s-den) | Launch pad behind the Hub's Graveyard | Combat I |
| [Mushroom Desert](the-islands#mushroom-desert) | Bridge from the Barn | Farming V |
| [Deep Caverns](the-islands#deep-caverns) | Lift shaft at the bottom of the Gold Mine | Mining V |

Each island you visit also unlocks its `/warp`.

## The stages at a glance

| Stage | Goal | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Your island | Learn the menu, place your first minion | Private Island, Skyhaven |
| 2. The Hub | Level each skill to I, meet the merchants | The Hub's areas |
| 3. The first islands | Farm, forage, mine and fight on the public islands | Barn, Park, Gold Mine, Spider's Den |
| 4. The Bazaar | Sell in bulk, grow your minions | SkyBlock Level 7 and up |
| 5. The deep | Mine and farm for rarer goods | Mushroom Desert, Deep Caverns |
| 6. Slayers | Kill the four slayer bosses | Maddox in the Hub |

## Stage 1: your island

**Goal:** know where everything is, and have a minion working.

- Press **K** and look through the [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu).
- Walk through your island's portal to the Hub, meet Skyhaven's merchants and come back with `/is`.
- Gather the materials for a first minion and craft it from the Recipe Book. Place it on your island.

See [First steps](first-steps) and [Profiles and islands](profiles-and-islands).

## Stage 2: the Hub

**Goal:** reach level I in Farming, Foraging, Mining and Combat, which opens the four first launch pads.

- **Farm:** Wheat. **Forest:** Oak. **Coal Mine:** Coal and Stone. **Graveyard:** zombies. See [The Hub](the-hub).
- Sell what you don't need, and keep the rest for your collections and recipes.
- Find the **Fairy Souls** on the Hub's islands and bring five at a time to Tia. See [The Hub](the-hub#fairy-souls).
- Put coins in the Bank. See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).
- The Ruins' wolves and the Hub Crypts' ghouls are tough. Come back to them later, for Combat XP and for [Sven](slayers#sven-packmaster).

## Stage 3: the first islands

**Goal:** work each skill where it pays best, and fill your first collections.

| Skill | Island | What for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farming | [The Barn](the-islands#the-barn) | Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Pumpkins and Melons |
| Foraging | [The Park](the-islands#the-park) | Birch, Spruce, Dark Oak, Acacia and Jungle wood |
| Mining | [Gold Mine](the-islands#gold-mine) | Stone, Coal, Iron and Gold Ore |
| Combat | [Spider's Den](the-islands#spider-s-den) | Spiders, level 2 to 10 around the camp and level 42 on the Mound |

The Park's Howling Cave is a good place to fight for wolf drops. The merchants on these islands (the Windmill Operator, Old Shaman Nyko, the Iron and Gold Forgers and Shaggy) sell their own goods. See [NPC Shops](npc-shops). New minion recipes unlock as your collections grow.

## Stage 4: the Bazaar

**Goal:** SkyBlock Level 7, and a way of making coins that doesn't need you.

- SkyBlock Level 7 opens the [Bazaar](bazaar). Sell your materials in bulk, and buy what your recipes need.
- Level 5 opens the [Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe#wardrobe).
- Upgrade your minions: higher tiers work faster and hold more, and fuel and upgrades improve them again. See [Minions](minions).
- Visit the **Blacksmith** at Rosetta's forge to reforge your gear. See [Reforging](reforging).
- List gear on the [Auction House](auction-house), or buy it there.
- Check the [calendar](calendar-and-events) for the day's events.

> [!TIP]
> Minion slots grow with the number of different minions you craft, so craft a new kind before you upgrade an old one.

## Stage 5: the deep

**Goal:** Farming V and Mining V, and the rarer ores.

- **Farming V** opens the bridge from the Barn to the [Mushroom Desert](the-islands#mushroom-desert): Cactus, Sugar Cane, Cocoa Beans and Mushrooms.
- **Mining V** opens the lift from the Gold Mine to the [Deep Caverns](the-islands#deep-caverns): six layers, from Coal and Iron down to Diamond and Obsidian. Lift Operators on every layer take you to any layer you have already walked into.
- Craft the enchanted versions of what you mine. Collection tiers unlock the recipes for better gear and minions. See [Collections](collections).

## Stage 6: slayers

**Goal:** beat Maddox's four bosses.

Talk to Maddox the Slayer in the Hub to start. Each boss is a chain of tiers; a tier needs the one below it beaten, and some also need a Combat level. See [Slayers](slayers).

| Boss | Where to fight | Opens when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenant Horror | Graveyard, Hub Crypts | From the start (2,000 coins) |
| Tarantula Broodfather | Spider's Den | Revenant Horror II beaten |
| Sven Packmaster | Ruins, the Park's Howling Cave | Tarantula Broodfather II beaten |
| Voidgloom Seraph | Any area with mobs | Sven Packmaster IV beaten |

Slayer levels give stats and SkyBlock XP, and unlock the slayer gear's recipes in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book).

## Tips for the whole climb

- Keep **every** skill moving. Skill levels give stats and coins, and each adds SkyBlock XP.
- Fill your collections: they unlock almost everything.
- Wear the best gear you have, and reforge it at the Blacksmith. See [Weapons](weapons), [Armour sets](armour-sets) and [Accessories](accessories).
- Get a pet for the skill you are levelling. See [Pets](pets).
- Keep coins in the Bank, because dying costs you half of your purse.
- Use the Wardrobe and the Ender Chest to keep your sets and spares. See [Storage and Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe).
