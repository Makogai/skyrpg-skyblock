The **Bestiary** counts every mob you kill, by **family**: Hypixel's 255 families on 19 islands (Hub, The Farming Islands, Spider's Den, The End, Deep Caverns, Dwarven Mines, Crystal Hollows, The Park, your Private Island, and islands still to come). Open it with `/bestiary` (or `/be`).

## Families and tiers

A kill counts for the family named like the mob: a **Crypt Ghoul** for Crypt Ghoul, a **Zealot Bruiser** for Zealot, a **Knifethrower** for Goblin, any of the dragons for Dragon. Vanilla mobs with no name of their own (on your island) count for the Private Island's families: Zombie, Skeleton, Spider and the rest.

Each family has Hypixel's **tiers**: kill counts that get bigger each tier, up to the family's top tier (5 to 25). A common mob needs thousands of kills for its last tier, a boss only a handful.

## Rewards

- Every new family tier: **+1 SkyBlock XP**.
- Every **10** family tiers (all families added up): a **Bestiary Milestone**, **+2 Health** each, for good.

A panel in chat shows each tier up ("BESTIARY Goblin II → III").

## The menu

The islands are down the left with how many of their families you've found; click one to see its families. Each card has the family's head, its tier (out of its top tier) and a bar to the next tier; families you've never killed show as **???**. Hover a card for its kills and the kills its next tier needs. The header shows your Milestone and your tiers in all.
