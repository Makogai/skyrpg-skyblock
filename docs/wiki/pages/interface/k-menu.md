Press **K** and one window opens with everything the pack adds: the SkyBlock Menu, your character, skills, slayers, storage, the Bazaar and Auction House, collections and settings. It has a sidebar of pages on the left and a dark custom look instead of vanilla screens. This page explains how to use the menu and lists every page, in the order they appear.

## Using the menu

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **K** | Open the menu, on the page you last used (the SkyBlock Menu the first time). Press again to close. |
| **Esc** | Step back inside a page, for example clear a search. Closes the menu when there is nowhere left to go back. |
| Up and Down arrows | Previous or next page |
| **1** to **9** | Jump to one of the first nine pages |
| Click a page in the sidebar | Go there |
| Scroll wheel over the page | Scroll the page |
| Scroll wheel over the sidebar | Scroll the list of pages, when they don't all fit |

A page with a text field, such as the Auction House, Bazaar, Party or Settings, takes digits and letters first.

The window fits itself to your screen. **Settings, Interface, Menu size** lets you pick Auto, Compact or Large. See [Settings](settings).

On a small screen nothing is out of reach:

- When the pages don't all fit down the sidebar, the list scrolls. A thin scrollbar and a fade show where it is cut off, and picking a page with the keys scrolls it into view.
- A page taller than the window scrolls inside it, with a scrollbar on the right. Tiles and cards keep a readable size instead of being squashed.

## The pages

The sidebar lists the pages in this order:

| Page | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| SkyBlock Menu | The first page: everything on one board. See [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu) |
| Profile | Your character and a tile for each stat |
| Stats | The main stats, grouped, with where each bonus comes from |
| Skills | A card for each skill |
| Slayers | Maddox's four slayer lines |
| Calendar and Events | The SkyBlock date and what is on. See [Calendar and events](calendar-and-events) |
| Storage | Ender Chest pages and the Wardrobe. See [Storage and Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe) |
| Fast Travel | Warps to your island, the Hub and the islands you have visited. See [SkyBlock Menu](skyblock-menu#fast-travel) |
| Pets | Your pet and your pet storage (admin-given only for now) |
| Party | Your party: members, health, where they are, invites |
| Collections | Gathered items and their tiers |
| Brainrots | Your brainrot album (also on **J**) |
| Wiki | This wiki, inside the game |
| Auction House | Buy and sell items. See [Auction House](auction-house) |
| Bazaar | Instant trades and orders. See [Bazaar](bazaar) |
| SkyBlock Leveling | Your SkyBlock Level and what it unlocks. See [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling) |
| Recipe Book | Every crafting recipe. See [Recipe Book](recipe-book) |
| SkyBlock Items | Every SkyBlock item. See [SkyBlock items](skyblock-items#browsing-every-item) |
| Profile Management | Your profiles and co-ops. See [Profiles and islands](profiles-and-islands) |
| Settings | Every option |

The survival game's pages for Talents, Contracts, the Journal, Mounts, Titles and Pay are switched off in SkyBlock and do not show.

### Profile

Your character is on the left. On the right is a tile for each thing the mods register:

- **Purse**, the coins you carry.
- **SkyBlock Level**, shown in aqua, which is also the badge in chat.
- **Skill Average**.
- One tile for every stat: Health, Defense, Strength, Crit Chance and so on.

Tiles fill one to three columns, depending on the window, and scroll when there are more than fit. A number too long for its tile is shortened, such as `1.23B`. Hover to see it in full. See [SkyBlock Leveling](skyblock-leveling) and [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).

### Stats

The main stats as tiles, in four groups:

| Group | Stats |
| --- | --- |
| Combat | Health, Defense, Strength, Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Speed, Intelligence |
| Gathering | Mining Speed, Mining Fortune, Farming Fortune, Foraging Fortune, Fishing Speed, Sea Creature Chance, Fishing Fortune |
| Wisdom | Farming, Mining, Foraging, Combat and Fishing Wisdom |
| Other | Magic Find |

Hover a tile to see what the stat does and where your bonuses come from: skills, gear, slayer levels, pets and your SkyBlock Level. Hypixel's other stats are on item tooltips but have no tile yet. See [Stats](stats).

### Skills

A card for every skill with your level, an animated progress bar and your XP. Hover for your perk, what you have earned and what the next level gives. See [Skills overview](skills-overview).

### Slayers

The overview has a card for each slayer line with your level, an XP bar, your boss kills per tier and any quest you are running. Click a line to see its level track (what each level gives and unlocks), the tiers (the boss, cost, rewards, drops and odds) and your own stats. See [Slayers](slayers).

### Pets

A live 3D preview of the selected pet, with its rarity, name, level and XP bar, its stats and perks, and your **pet storage**. Buttons: **Equip**, **Unequip** and **Take out**. Pets are only given out by admins at the moment. See [Pets](pets).

### Party

Your party's members, each on a card with their face, a crown for the leader, online or offline, health and where they are. Click a member to set a waypoint to them. The leader can **Promote** and **Kick**. There are buttons for **Party chat**, **Leave** and **Disband**, a name field to **Invite** a player, and your pending invites with **Accept** and **Deny**. See [Party](party).

### Collections

Tabs for each collection group. Each row shows an item's icon, your count, your tier, a bar to the next tier and what that tier unlocks. Hover a row for all its tiers. See [Collections](collections).

### Brainrots

Your brainrot album: a card for each of the fifteen, with the 3D model of the ones you have caught and a **?** for the rest. Click a card to see it up close and hear it say its name. **J** opens this page straight away. Brainrots cannot be caught in SkyBlock today. See [Brainrots](brainrots).

### Settings

Every option, grouped by category, with a search box. See [Settings](settings).

## Other screens

These open from NPCs and blocks rather than the menu, but use the same look:

- **NPC shops**, by right-clicking a merchant. See [NPC shops](npc-shops).
- **The crafting table**, by right-clicking one or typing `/craft`. See [Recipe Book](recipe-book).
- **The Blacksmith and the anvil**, for reforges and potato books. See [Reforging](reforging).
- **Ender chests and the Wardrobe**, by `/ec` and `/wardrobe`. See [Storage and Wardrobe](storage-and-wardrobe).
- **Minions**, by right-clicking one. See [Minions](minions).
- **Slayer quests**, by right-clicking Maddox. See [Slayers](slayers).

## The Wiki

This wiki is also inside the game. Look for **Wiki** in the menu, or type `/wiki`. Hover an item in any inventory and press **H** to jump to its entry. See [Controls and keys](controls-and-keys).

> [!TIP]
> The menu remembers the last page you used. Press K twice to go back to where you were.
