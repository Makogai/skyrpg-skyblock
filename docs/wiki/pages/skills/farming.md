Farming levels by harvesting crops. The Farming skill is Hypixel SkyBlock's: 60 levels, the **Farmhand** perk (+4 Farming Fortune a level, 240 at level 60) and bonus Health every level. See [Skills overview](skills-overview) for the XP curve and what each level pays.

## Where to farm

Crops only break in the farming areas, and every crop you harvest grows back after a few seconds.

| Area | Where | Crops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Farm](the-hub#farm) | Hub, north-east | Wheat |
| [The Barn](the-islands#the-barn) | the Farm's launch pad (Farming I) | Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Pumpkins, Melons |
| [Mushroom Desert](the-islands#mushroom-desert) | over the Barn's bridge (Farming V) | Cactus, Sugar Cane, Cocoa Beans, Red and Brown Mushrooms, Sand |
| [Mountain](the-hub#mountain) | Hub, south | Carrots, in the cave at its foot |

Crops pay XP only when fully grown. The Barn and the Mushroom Desert also hold farm animals (Cows, Pigs, Chickens, Rabbits, Sheep, Mooshrooms); killing them pays Combat XP, not Farming.

## Farming XP

| Crop | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Melon, Sugar Cane, Cactus | 4 |
| Cocoa Beans (fully grown) | 4 |
| Pumpkin | 4.5 |
| Red or Brown Mushroom | 6 |

Blocks you placed yourself never pay XP, and neither do minions: only what you harvest with your own hands counts.

## Collections

What you harvest fills the Farming [collections](collections): Wheat, Seeds, Carrot, Potato, Pumpkin, Melon Slice, Sugar Cane, Cactus, Cocoa Beans, Mushroom and the rest of the 20. Each tier unlocks recipes in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book): Wheat I opens the Wheat Minion, and higher tiers open the enchanted forms. Items you collect from your minions count too.

## Stats

| Stat | What it does here |
| --- | --- |
| Farming Fortune | Each full 100 drops the crop's items one more time, and the rest is the chance of one more. It works on fully grown crops, Melons, Pumpkins, Sugar Cane, Cactus and Cocoa, not on Mushrooms. The Farmhand perk gives +4 a level. |
| Wheat Fortune and the other crop fortunes | Listed on gear, but have no effect yet. |

## Merchants and minions

The **Farm Merchant** in Skyhaven and the **Windmill Operator** at the Barn sell the same goods: Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Melons, Sugar Cane, Pumpkins, Cocoa Beans, Mushrooms, Sand, Cactus, Enchanted Bone Meal and the Rookie Hoe. Click any crop in your inventory to sell it (see [NPC shops](npc-shops)). **Jake**, in the Mushroom Desert, trades animal axes.

Farming minions work on your island: Wheat, Carrot, Potato, Pumpkin, Melon, Mushroom, Cactus, Cocoa Beans, Sugar Cane, Nether Wart, Flower and the animal minions. See [Minions](minions).

A reforge and Potato Books make a farming tool better: see [Reforging](reforging).
