Mining levels by breaking stone and ore. The Mining skill is Hypixel SkyBlock's: 60 levels, the **Spelunker** perk (+4 Mining Fortune a level) and +1 Defense a level (+2 from level 15). See [Skills overview](skills-overview) for the XP curve and what each level pays.

## Where to mine

Stone and ore only break in the mining areas. A broken block turns to bedrock for a few seconds, then comes back, so the ore never runs out.

| Area | Where | Blocks that break |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Coal Mine](the-hub#coal-mine) | Hub, north | Stone, Coal Ore |
| [Gold Mine](the-islands#gold-mine) | through the Coal Mine's gold gate (Mining I) | Stone, Coal Ore, Iron Ore, Gold Ore |
| [Deep Caverns](the-islands#deep-caverns) | the Gold Mine's lift shaft (Mining V) | Stone and the ore of each layer |

The Deep Caverns' six layers each have their own ore:

| Layer | Ore |
| --- | --- |
| Gunpowder Mines | Coal, Iron, Gold |
| Lapis Quarry | Lapis |
| Pigmen's Den | Redstone |
| Slimehill | Emerald |
| Diamond Reserve | Diamond |
| Obsidian Sanctuary | Diamond, Obsidian, Diamond Blocks |

The rarer the block, the longer it takes to grow back: Stone and Coal Ore 4 to 6 seconds, Gold Ore and Diamond Ore 8, Obsidian 20, Diamond Blocks 30. Your own [island](profiles-and-islands) has no ore to mine, but a [minion](minions) can mine for you there.

## Mining XP

| Block | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Stone, Cobblestone | 1 |
| Coal Ore, Iron Ore | 5 |
| Gold Ore | 6 |
| Lapis Ore, Redstone Ore | 7 |
| Emerald Ore | 9 |
| Diamond Ore | 10 |
| Obsidian | 20 |

Deepslate versions pay the same. Sand, Gravel and Diamond Blocks pay nothing. Blocks you placed yourself never pay XP, and neither do minions: only what you break with your own hands counts.

## Collections

What you mine fills the Mining [collections](collections): Cobblestone, Coal, Gold Ingot, Iron Ingot, Redstone, Diamond, Emerald, Obsidian, Gravel, Sand and the rest of the 25. Each tier unlocks recipes in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book): Cobblestone I opens the Cobblestone Minion, Cobblestone IV the Enchanted Cobblestone. Items you collect from your minions count too.

## Stats

| Stat | What it does here |
| --- | --- |
| Mining Speed | Breaks blocks faster. Pickaxes and some armour carry it. |
| Mining Fortune | Each full 100 drops the ore's items one more time, and the rest is the chance of one more. It counts for ores, not for Stone or Obsidian. The Spelunker perk gives +4 a level. |
| Breaking Power | Shown on pickaxes, but nothing checks it yet. |

## Merchants and minions

The **Mine Merchant** in Skyhaven sells Coal, Iron and Gold Ingots, Torches, Cobblestone, Stone and Gravel, the Rookie and Promising Pickaxes, and a Gold Pickaxe for 3 Gold Ingots. The **Iron Forger** and the **Gold Forger** in the Gold Mine's camp sell ingots, armour and pickaxes. Right-click a merchant to buy, and click any item in your inventory to sell it (see [NPC shops](npc-shops)).

Mining minions work on your island: Cobblestone, Coal, Iron, Gold, Lapis, Redstone, Emerald, Diamond, Obsidian, Gravel, Sand, Quartz, Glowstone and more. See [Minions](minions).

A reforge and Potato Books make a pickaxe better: see [Reforging](reforging).
