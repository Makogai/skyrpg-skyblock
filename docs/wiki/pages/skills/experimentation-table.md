The **Experimentation Table** runs Hypixel's three enchanting experiments. They pay **Enchanting XP**, and Superpairs also gives enchanted books, Experience Bottles, the Guardian pet and more.

## Getting a table

Its recipe unlocks at **Enchanting X**: 8 logs around an Experience Bottle (see the [Recipe Book](recipe-book)). It's furniture: **place it on your [island](profiles-and-islands)** and right-click it. Break it to take it back.

Every experiment comes in **stakes**. A stake needs an Enchanting level and costs **XP levels** (the green number above your hotbar) to start.

## Superpairs

A board of face-down tiles. Each click turns one over; turn two of the same prize in a row and you win it. A wrong pair turns back after a moment, so remember where things are. When your clicks run out (or the board is cleared), the board is shown and you get everything you won.

Two tiles are power-ups: the **Feather** gives 3 more clicks, and the **Diamond** makes the next tile you turn find its pair by itself.

| Stake | Enchanting | XP levels | Board | Clicks | Prizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | 10 | 25 | 7 x 2 | 10 | Enchanting XP, Experience Bottles, books, Common Guardian |
| High | 20 | 50 | 5 x 4 | 12 | + Grand and Titanic Experience Bottles, Uncommon Guardian |
| Grand | 25 | 75 | 5 x 4 | 12 | + Rare Guardian, Metaphysical Serum |
| Supreme | 30 | 100 | 7 x 4 | 14 | + ultra-rare books, Epic Guardian |
| Transcendent | 40 | 200 | 7 x 4 | 16 | + Legendary Guardian |
| Metaphysical | 50 | 350 | 7 x 4 | 12 (add-on clicks count twice) | + Experiment the Fish |

Books are a random table enchantment at the table's top level (Sharpness V, Growth V...). An **ultra-rare book** is a level above that (Sharpness VI).

**Charges:** each Superpairs uses one of your **3 charges**. A charge comes back 24 hours after you used it. You can buy one back early for 50, then 100, then 200 XP levels (three a day).

## Chronomatron and Ultrasequencer (add-ons)

Do these first: how far you get adds **bonus clicks to your next Superpairs** (your best result counts, and they're used up when Superpairs starts). They need a charge to be there but don't use it.

**Chronomatron:** coloured pads play a chain of notes. Click them in the same order; the chain then plays again with one note more. A wrong pad ends it.

**Ultrasequencer:** numbers show on a grid for a moment. Click their squares from 1 up; the next round has one number more (it starts at 3). A wrong square ends it.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chronomatron | High | 20 | 5 | 3 colours | 1,500 | +1 at a chain of 5, +2 at 9 |
| Chronomatron | Grand | 25 | 10 | 5 colours | 2,500 | +1 at 5, +2 at 9 |
| Chronomatron | Supreme | 30 | 15 | 7 colours | 3,500 | +1 at 5, +2 at 9 |
| Chronomatron | Transcendent | 35 | 20 | 8 colours | 4,500 | +1 at 5, +2 at 9 |
| Chronomatron | Metaphysical | 40 | 40 | 10 colours | 6,000 | +1 at 5, +2 at 9, +3 at 12 |
| Ultrasequencer | Supreme | 25 | 15 | 7 x 3 | 3,500 | +1 at a sequence of 5, +2 at 7 |
| Ultrasequencer | Transcendent | 30 | 20 | 7 x 4 | 5,000 | +1 at 5, +2 at 7 |
| Ultrasequencer | Metaphysical | 40 | 40 | 9 x 4 | 7,000 | +1 at 5, +2 at 7, +3 at 9 |

Enchanting XP is your longest chain or sequence times the step's XP, up to 15 steps for Chronomatron and 20 for Ultrasequencer.

## Metaphysical Serum

Right-click to drink it: each dose makes every add-on milestone **one step shorter** (a chain of 4 gives what 5 did). Up to 3 doses.
