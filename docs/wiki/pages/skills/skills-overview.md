Skills are how your character grows. You gain XP in a skill by doing what it is about, and every level gives permanent stats, coins and SkyBlock XP. The skills, their XP curve and their rewards are Hypixel SkyBlock's. Open the SkyBlock Menu and choose **Skills**, or type `/skills`. Hover a skill to see your perk, everything it gives you so far, and what the next level adds.

## The skills

| Skill | Max level | Perk (every level) | Stats every level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Farming | 60 | Farmhand: +4 Farming Fortune | Health: +2 (levels 1 to 14), +3 (15 to 19), +4 (20 to 25), +5 (26 on) |
| Mining | 60 | Spelunker: +4 Mining Fortune | Defense: +1 (1 to 14), +2 (15 on) |
| Combat | 60 | Warrior: +4% damage to mobs (+1% a level after 50) | +0.5 Crit Chance |
| Foraging | 50 | Logger: +4 Foraging Fortune | Strength: +1 (1 to 14), +2 (15 on) |
| Fishing | 50 | Treasure Hunter: +0.1 Treasure Chance | Health: +2 (1 to 14), +3 (15 to 19), +4 (20 to 25), +5 (26 on) |
| Enchanting | 60 | Conjurer: +4% experience orbs | Intelligence: +1 (1 to 14), +2 (15 on); +0.5 Ability Damage |
| Alchemy | 50 | Brewer: +1% potion duration | Intelligence: +1 (1 to 14), +2 (15 on) |
| Taming | 60 | Zoologist: +1% pet XP | +1 Pet Luck |
| Carpentry | 50 | | +1 Health |

Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing and Enchanting earn XP by doing them. Two more earn XP alongside:

- **Carpentry**: crafting (the [Recipe Book](recipe-book) or a crafting table) gives 3% of what the ingredients sell for to an NPC, as on Hypixel. 160 Cobblestone into an Enchanted Cobblestone is 4.8 Carpentry XP.
- **Taming**: whatever XP your active pet earns, you earn in Taming too.

Alchemy earns XP from brewing potions (see [Alchemy](alchemy)).

## How XP and levels work

Every skill uses Hypixel's curve. The table shows the XP needed to reach each level in total.

| Level | Total XP | Level | Total XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 50 | 30 | 8,022,425 |
| 5 | 1,175 | 35 | 15,522,425 |
| 10 | 9,925 | 40 | 25,522,425 |
| 15 | 67,425 | 45 | 38,072,425 |
| 20 | 522,425 | 50 | 55,172,425 |
| 25 | 3,022,425 | 60 | 111,672,425 |

Every XP gain shows above the hotbar ("+4 Mining (54/125)").

## What every level gives

Every level of every skill gives:

1. **The perk's next tier** and the skill's **stats** (above). They are permanent and stack with your gear.
2. **Coins**: 100 at level 1, 250, 500, 750, 1,000 at level 5, then more each level, up to 1,000,000 for each level from 45 on. A skill to 50 pays 17,259,100 coins; to 60, 27,259,100.
3. **SkyBlock XP**: 5 a level up to 10, 10 a level from 11 to 25, 20 from 26 to 50, and 30 from 51 to 60.

A level up shows a notice and the list of rewards in chat:

> [!NOTE]
> **LEVEL UP** Mining ➜ IV
> **Rewards**
> Spelunker IV +16 Mining Fortune
> +4 Defense
> +1,600 Coins
> +20 SkyBlock XP

(Levelling several times at once adds up the rewards of every level in between.)

Some [collection](collections) tiers list skill XP ("+1,000 Mining Experience"); those lines grant nothing yet.

## Where each skill's XP comes from

| Skill | Source | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farming | Fully grown crops on the Farm, the Barn and the Mushroom Desert | [Farming](farming) |
| Mining | Stone and ore in the Coal Mine, Gold Mine and Deep Caverns | [Mining](mining) |
| Foraging | Logs in the Forest and The Park | [Foraging](foraging) |
| Fishing | Every catch, in any water | [Fishing](fishing) |
| Combat | Killing the Hub's and the islands' mobs (their Combat XP) | [Slayers](slayers) for the bosses |
| Enchanting | The stand-in enchanting screen (Hypixel enchanting is coming) | [Enchanting](enchanting) |
| Carpentry | Crafting | [Crafting](crafting) |

Minions give no skill XP, as on Hypixel. Runecrafting, Social and Hunting aren't built yet.
