Skills are how your character grows. You gain XP in a skill by doing what it is about, and every level gives permanent stats, coins and SkyBlock XP. The skills, their XP curve and their rewards are Hypixel SkyBlock's. Open the SkyBlock Menu and choose **Skills**, or type `/skills`. Hover a skill to see your perk, everything it gives you so far, and what the next level adds.

Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing and Enchanting earn XP today. Alchemy, Taming and Carpentry are there with their rewards, and earn XP once brewing, pet levelling and crafting XP come.

## How XP and levels work

Every skill uses Hypixel's curve. The table shows the XP needed to reach each level in total.

| Level | Total XP | Level | Total XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 50 | 30 | 8,022,425 |
| 5 | 1,175 | 35 | 15,522,425 |
| 10 | 9,925 | 40 | 25,522,425 |
| 15 | 67,425 | 45 | 38,072,425 |
| 20 | 522,425 | 50 | 55,172,425 |
| 25 | 3,022,425 | 60 | 111,672,425 |

Every XP gain shows above the hotbar ("+4 Mining (54/125)").

## What every level gives

Every level of every skill gives:

1. **The perk's next tier** and the skill's **stats** (above). They are permanent and stack with your gear.
2. **Coins**: 100 at level 1, 250, 500, 750, 1,000 at level 5, then more each level, up to 1,000,000 for each level from 45 on. A skill to 50 pays 17,259,100 coins; to 60, 27,259,100.
3. **SkyBlock XP**: 5 a level up to 10, 10 a level from 11 to 25, 20 from 26 to 50, and 30 from 51 to 60.

A level up shows a notice and the list of rewards in chat:

> [!NOTE]
> **LEVEL UP** Mining ➜ IV
> **Rewards**
> Spelunker IV +16 Mining Fortune
> +4 Defense
> +1,600 Coins
> +20 SkyBlock XP

(Levelling several times at once adds up the rewards of every level in between.)

Some [collection](collections) tiers also give skill XP ("+1,000 Mining Experience").
