Fishing levels by catching things with a rod. The Fishing skill is Hypixel SkyBlock's: 50 levels, the **Treasure Hunter** perk (+0.1 Treasure Chance a level) and bonus Health every level. See [Skills overview](skills-overview) for the XP curve and what each level pays.

## Where to fish

Any water you can reach works: the Hub's **Wilderness** has a lake with Fisherman Gerald's hut and dock, and **Birch Park** in [The Park](the-islands#the-park) has one too. Cast, wait for the bite, and reel in. Fishing needs no zone.

## Fishing XP

Every catch pays **8 Fishing XP**, whatever it is. What comes up is vanilla's fishing loot: fish, Lily Pads, Ink Sacs and the usual junk and treasure.

## Collections

What you fish up fills the Fishing [collections](collections): Raw Cod, Raw Salmon, Pufferfish, Tropical Fish, Lily Pad, Ink Sac, Clay Ball, Sponge, Prismarine Shard and Crystals, and the rest of the 12. Each tier unlocks recipes in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book), the Fishing Minion among them. Items you collect from your minions count too.

## Stats

| Stat | What it does here |
| --- | --- |
| Fishing Speed | Shortens the wait for a bite. Every point takes 0.2 seconds off, so 25 points is 5 seconds less. |
| Treasure Chance, Sea Creature Chance, Fishing Fortune | Listed on rods, armour and the Treasure Hunter perk, but have no effect yet. |

## Merchants and minions

The **Fishing Merchant** in the Hub sells a Fishing Rod (50 coins), Minnow Bait (15), a Diamond Sword and the Angler armour set with the Angler Necklace, Cloak, Bracelet and Belt (100 coins each). Click any catch in your inventory to sell it (see [NPC shops](npc-shops)). Bait has no effect yet.

The **Fishing Minion** fishes for you on your island. See [Minions](minions).

A reforge and Potato Books make a rod better: see [Reforging](reforging).
