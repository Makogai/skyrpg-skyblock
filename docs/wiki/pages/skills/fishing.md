Fishing levels by catching things with a rod and killing the sea creatures that come up. The Fishing skill is Hypixel SkyBlock's: 50 levels, the **Treasure Hunter** perk (+0.1 Treasure Chance a level) and bonus Health every level. See [Skills overview](skills-overview) for the XP curve and what each level pays.

## Where to fish

Any water you can reach works: the Hub's **Wilderness** has a lake with Fisherman Gerald's hut and dock, and **Birch Park** in [The Park](the-islands#the-park) has one too. Cast, wait for the bobber to dip, and reel in.

**Lava:** in the [Crystal Hollows](crystal-hollows), any rod's bobber floats on the Magma Fields' lava sea, for the Hollows' lava sea creatures and lava catches. On the [Crimson Isle](crimson-isle) you need a **lava rod** (Starter Lava Rod, Polished Topaz Rod, Magma Rod, Inferno Rod, Hellfire Rod, Elle's Lava Rod), and its lava brings up **Trophy Fish** too.

## What a bite brings up

Each time you reel in a bite, in this order:

1. **A sea creature**, at your **Sea Creature Chance** (20% to start; rods, armour and baits add to it). On your private island it's a quarter of that.
2. On the Crimson Isle's lava, otherwise maybe **a Trophy Fish** (see [Crimson Isle](crimson-isle#trophy-fishing)).
3. Otherwise **treasure** (in water), at your **Treasure Chance** (your Fishing level gives it). Treasure is a **Good Catch** (89%), a **Great Catch** (10%) or an **Outstanding Catch** (1%), each with its own message.
4. Otherwise **a fish**: Raw Cod, Raw Salmon, Tropical Fish or Pufferfish (in lava: Netherrack, Magma Cream, Blaze Powder, Glowstone Dust, Quartz or Nether Wart).

Everything goes straight into your inventory.

| Treasure | What it can be |
| --- | --- |
| Good Catch | 16 Raw Cod or Raw Salmon, 12 Tropical Fish or Pufferfish, 32 Clay, 16 Sponges, 8 Prismarine Shards or Crystals, 4 Grand Experience Bottles, 25,000 to 50,000 coins |
| Great Catch | 3 to 4 enchanted fish, an Enchanted Sponge, Clay or Ink Sac, a Titanic Experience Bottle, a Common Squid pet, 100,000 to 250,000 coins |
| Outstanding Catch | 24 to 32 enchanted fish, 8 Enchanted Sponges or Prismarine Crystals, an Epic or Legendary Squid pet, 500,000 to 1,000,000 coins |

## Sea creatures

A sea creature surfaces at your bobber and comes for you, with Hypixel's name, level, health and catch message ("You caught a Sea Walker."). **Fishing rods hit sea creatures twice as hard.** Whoever kills one gets its coins, its drops (rare ones are announced with their odds) and its **Fishing XP**, which is where most Fishing XP comes from. Sea creatures killed on a private island give no Fishing XP.

## Bait

Bait in your inventory counts for each cast, and **each catch uses one up** (the first bait in your inventory).

| Bait | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Minnow Bait, Fish Bait | +25, +45 Fishing Speed |
| Spiked Bait | +6 Sea Creature Chance |
| Ice Bait, Glowy Chum Bait, Frozen Bait | Fishing Speed and +4, +3, +8 Sea Creature Chance |
| Light Bait, Dark Bait | Rare sea creatures 25% likelier by day, by night (Dark Bait also brings Night Squids) |
| Whale Bait | +30 Fishing Speed, rare sea creatures 25% likelier |
| Carrot Bait | 5% of sea creatures are the Carrot King |
| Blessed Bait | Great and Outstanding Catches 50% likelier |
| Golden Bait, Treasure Bait | +4, +2 Treasure Chance |
| Worm Bait | +60 Fishing Speed in the Crystal Hollows |

## Fishing XP

- **A fish or treasure:** what Hypixel gives for that item (Raw Cod 25, Raw Salmon 35, Pufferfish 50, Tropical Fish 100; treasure at least 50, 200 or 800 by tier).
- **A sea creature:** its Fishing XP when you kill it (25 for a Squid up to 2,380 for a Water Hydra).

Fishing Wisdom adds its percentage to all of it.

## Collections

What you fish up fills the Fishing [collections](collections): Raw Cod, Raw Salmon, Pufferfish, Tropical Fish, Lily Pad, Ink Sac, Clay Ball, Sponge, Prismarine Shard and Crystals, and the rest of the 12. Each tier unlocks recipes in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book), the Fishing Minion among them. Items you collect from your minions count too.

## Stats

| Stat | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Fishing Speed | Shortens the wait for a bite (10 to 20 seconds with none). 300 is the cap: fish bite straight away. |
| Sea Creature Chance | The chance a bite is a sea creature. 20% to start. |
| Treasure Chance | The chance a bite that isn't a sea creature is treasure. |
| Double Hook Chance | The chance a sea creature comes up with a second one. |
| Fishing Wisdom | More Fishing XP. |

## Merchants and minions

The **Fishing Merchant** in the Hub sells a Fishing Rod (50 coins), Minnow Bait (15), a Diamond Sword and the Angler armour set with the Angler Necklace, Cloak, Bracelet and Belt (100 coins each). Click any catch in your inventory to sell it (see [NPC shops](npc-shops)).

The **Fishing Minion** fishes for you on your island. See [Minions](minions).

A reforge and Potato Books make a rod better: see [Reforging](reforging).
