**Heart of the Mountain** (HotM) is Hypixel SkyBlock's second mining progression, next to the [Mining](mining) skill. It grows with **HotM XP**, mostly from [Commissions](dwarven-mines#commissions) in the [Dwarven Mines](dwarven-mines), and opens a tree of **perks** that you buy with **Tokens of the Mountain** and level up with **powder**. Open it with `/hotm`. It's per profile.

Each tier opens the next row of the tree. HotM 2 opens [the Forge](dwarven-mines#the-forge), and every tier up to 6 gives it one more slot.

## The tree

The tree is Hypixel's: **46 perks** on a grid, tier 1 at the bottom, tier 10 at the top. In the menu:

- **Coal**: locked. **Emerald**: unlocked. **Diamond**: maxed. Blocks are **Pickaxe Abilities**; the red block in the middle is **Core of the Mountain**; **redstone** is a perk you switched off.
- A perk can be **unlocked** with a Token of the Mountain once its tier is open and it's next to a perk you already have (Mining Speed, at the bottom, is the first).
- **Click** an unlocked perk to level it up with powder (**shift-click**: ten levels). Each level costs more: Mining Speed's level 2 costs 27 Mithril Powder, level 50 over 130,000.
- **Right-click** a perk to switch it off (it keeps its level) or back on.
- Hover any perk for what it gives at its level and the next, and what the next level costs.

Lower perks are paid with **Mithril Powder** (from the Dwarven Mines), higher ones with **Gemstone** and **Glacite Powder** (from the Crystal Hollows and Glacite Tunnels, which aren't here yet).

What works now: **Mining Speed** (+20 Mining Speed a level), **Mining Fortune** (+2 a level), **Titanium Insanium** (a bigger Titanium chance), **Powder Buff** (more Mithril Powder), **Quick Forge** (shorter forging), **Core of the Mountain** (below) and the **Mining Speed Boost** ability. The other perks show what they do but don't do it yet.

## Core of the Mountain

The perk in the middle of the tree, levelled with powder (50,000 Mithril Powder for level 2 up to 1,250,000 Glacite Powder for 10). Its levels give a Forge slot (2), a Commission slot (3), +1 Mithril Powder per Mithril block (4), Tokens of the Mountain (+1 at 5 and 7, +2 at 10), and make the Pickaxe Abilities stronger from level 2.

## Pickaxe Abilities

Unlocking a Pickaxe Ability perk selects it; click another unlocked one to switch. **Right-click** with a pickaxe, drill or gauntlet in a SkyBlock world to use it (2 minute cooldown):

- **Mining Speed Boost**: +200% Mining Speed for 10 seconds (+250% for 15 seconds with Core of the Mountain 2).

## Powder
