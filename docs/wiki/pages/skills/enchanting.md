Enchanting makes your weapons, armour and tools stronger with **Hypixel's 145 enchantments**: Sharpness, Critical, Protection, Growth, Efficiency, Life Steal, the ultimate enchantments and the rest. Enchantments are paid for with **experience levels** (the green number above your hotbar), and every level you spend levels up the **Enchanting** skill.

## The Enchantment Table

Use any **enchanting table** (there's one in the Hub, and you can place your own on your [island](profiles-and-islands)), or talk to **Seraphine Vael** in the Hub. Put the item in the slot: the list shows every enchantment it can take. Pick one to see what it does and a button for each level the table can give (up to V for most, less for some), each with its XP cost.

- Click a level to put it on. Most enchants cost 10 to 50 levels at the top table level; some cost more.
- An enchantment that **conflicts** comes off: Protection replaces Blast, Fire and Projectile Protection (and the other way round), Life Steal replaces Syphon and Mana Steal, First Strike and Triple Strike replace each other, a tool can have only one Turbo enchant, and **One For All** replaces every other damage enchant. Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods work together, as on Hypixel.
- Click the level the item already has to **take it off** (it costs the same).
- Levels above what the table gives (Sharpness VI and VII, Growth VI...) only come from books.

## Enchanted books and the anvil

Enchanted books carry an enchantment, often at a level the table can't give. Most of them are on the [Bazaar](bazaar), in the **Oddities** tab, one group per enchantment.

Use any **anvil** to open **Combine Items** (see [Reforging](reforging)):

- **Book on an item:** the enchantment goes on (if it fits the item) for the XP levels of that level.
- **Book on a book:** two books of the same enchantment and level make one of the next level, for free. They combine up to the table's top level (two Sharpness IV make V, but two V don't make VI); enchantments the table doesn't have combine up to their highest book.
- The higher level always stays: a Sharpness III book does nothing on a Sharpness V sword.

## Ultimate enchantments

**Ultimate enchantments** (One For All, Ultimate Wise, Soul Eater, Swarm, Legion...) only come as books and show in bold pink. An item can hold **one**: putting on another replaces it.

## What they do

Each enchantment's tooltip says what it does at its level. They work as on Hypixel:

| Kind | Enchantments |
| --- | --- |
| Damage (added to the Combat skill's bonus) | Sharpness, Power (bows), Snipe (bows, per 10 blocks), Smite (undead), Bane of Arthropods, Ender Slayer, Cubism (creepers and slimes), Impaling (sea mobs), Smoldering, Magmarizer, Gravity, Dragon Hunter, First Strike (first hit), Triple Strike (first three hits), Execute, Prosecute, Giant Killer, One For All, Swarm, Combo, Habanero Tactics (slayer bosses) |
| Stats while worn | Protection (Defense), Growth (Health), True Protection, Big Brain, Smarty Pants (Intelligence), Small Brain, Sugar Rush (Speed), Rejuvenate and Respite (Health Regen; Respite only out of combat), Ice Cold, Respiration, Pesterminator |
| Stats on equipment | Prosperity, Cayenne (per digit of your Magical Power), Green Thumb (per different visitor your Garden has served), The One (per maxed collection) |
| Stats while held | Critical (Crit Damage), Overload, Vicious (Ferocity), Divine Gift (Magic Find), Efficiency (Mining Speed), Fortune, Lapidary, Pristine, Harvesting and Cultivating (Farming Fortune), the Turbo enchants (their crop's Fortune), Angler (Sea Creature Chance), Luck of the Sea, Expertise, Piscary (Fishing Speed), Petalfall, Absorb, Compact |
| On a hit | Life Steal (heals 0.5% of your health a level), Syphon (heals on crits), Mana Steal, Fire Aspect, Knockback, Flame and Punch (arrows), Thunderlord and Thunderbolt (every third hit), Venomous (slows and poisons), Cleave (hits mobs around) |
| On a kill | Scavenger (coins by the mob's level), Vampirism (heals your missing health) |
| Drops and XP | **Looting** (swords) and **Chance** (bows): the mob's loot is rolled a second time at their percent. **Luck**: mobs you kill drop the armour and weapon they carry more often. **Experience**: mobs and ores give double XP orbs at its chance |
| Farming | **Dedication** (Farming Fortune times the crop's Garden milestone), **Delicate** (won't break stems or crops that haven't grown), **Replenish** (plants the crop again from seeds in your inventory) |
| Fishing | **Lure** (shorter wait), **Magnet** (XP orbs with every catch), **Blessing** (better treasure), **Frail** (sea creatures start with less health), **Caster** (saves bait), Charm (better trophy fish) |
| Abilities | Ultimate Wise (less mana for the item's ability) |

## The Enchanting skill

Spending experience levels at the table or the anvil gives **Enchanting XP**: 3.5 × levels^1.5 (spending 30 levels gives 575). See [Skills](skills-overview) for what each level gives.
