Foraging levels by chopping trees. The Foraging skill is Hypixel SkyBlock's: 50 levels, the **Logger** perk (+4 Foraging Fortune a level) and +1 Strength a level (+2 from level 15). See [Skills overview](skills-overview) for the XP curve and what each level pays.

## Where to chop

Logs and leaves only break in the forests. What you chop grows back: logs after 20 seconds, leaves after 15.

| Area | Where | Wood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Forest](the-hub#forest) | Hub, south-west | Oak |
| Birch Park | [The Park](the-islands#the-park), where you arrive (Foraging I) | Birch |
| Spruce Woods | The Park, west | Spruce |
| Dark Thicket | The Park, east | Dark Oak |
| Savanna Woodland | The Park | Acacia |
| Jungle Island | The Park | Jungle |

The Howling Cave in the Park is for fighting, not chopping.

## Foraging XP

Every log pays **6 XP**, whatever the tree. Leaves pay nothing. Blocks you placed yourself never pay XP, and neither do minions: only what you chop with your own hands counts.

## Collections

What you chop fills the Foraging [collections](collections): Oak, Birch, Spruce, Dark Oak, Acacia and Jungle Log. Each tier unlocks recipes in the [Recipe Book](recipe-book): the wood's minion, then its enchanted form. Items you collect from your minions count too.

## Stats

| Stat | What it does here |
| --- | --- |
| Foraging Fortune | Each full 100 drops the log one more time, and the rest is the chance of one more. The Logger perk gives +4 a level. |

## Merchants and minions

The **Lumber Merchant** in Skyhaven sells logs (25 coins each), the Rookie Axe (12 coins), the Promising Axe (35), the Sweet Axe and Efficient Axe (100) and basic wooden tools. Click any log in your inventory to sell it (see [NPC shops](npc-shops)). **Master Tactician Funk** in the Savanna Woodland has a shop too.

Foraging minions work on your island: Oak, Birch, Spruce, Jungle, Dark Oak, Acacia and Flower. See [Minions](minions).

A reforge and Potato Books make an axe better: see [Reforging](reforging).
