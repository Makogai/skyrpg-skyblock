Crafting turns what you gather into better things: enchanted materials, minions, tools, armour. SkyBlock has **2,468** recipes, all from Hypixel, and two ways to use them: the **crafting table** and the **Recipe Book**. The Carpentry skill levels as you craft. The [Recipe Book and Crafting](recipe-book) page has the screens in full; this page is how it all fits together.

## Two ways to craft

| Way | How | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crafting table | Right-click any crafting table (sneak to place blocks on it instead), or type `/craft` | Opens SkyBlock's 3 by 3 grid. Lay the ingredients out as on Hypixel; the result shows on the right. **Craft** makes one, **Craft all** as many as the grid holds. |
| Recipe Book | SkyBlock Menu, **Recipe Book** | Every recipe in a grid with search and filters. **Craft** makes the item straight from your inventory, with no table. Shift-click makes up to 64. |

Both check the same rules. A locked recipe says what unlocks it, and crafting it is refused. Vanilla's crafting recipes are not used: only SkyBlock's.

## What unlocks a recipe

| Needs | Recipes | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing | 965 | the basics you start with |
| A collection tier | 1,348 | Cobblestone I: Cobblestone Minion. Cobblestone IV: Enchanted Cobblestone |
| A slayer level | 140 | gear from the Revenant Horror, Tarantula Broodfather, Sven Packmaster and Voidgloom Seraph |
| A boss from the dungeons | 15 | stays locked for now |

Collections fill as you gather: see [Mining](mining), [Farming](farming), [Foraging](foraging) and [Fishing](fishing), or [Collections](collections). Slayer levels come from [Slayers](slayers). A recipe that needs Inferno Demonlord or Riftstalker levels can't be unlocked yet, since those slayers aren't built.

## Carpentry

Every craft pays **Carpentry XP**: 3% of what the ingredients sell for to an NPC, as on Hypixel. 160 Cobblestone into an Enchanted Cobblestone is 4.8 XP. It is quiet XP: no line above the hotbar, but your level-ups show as usual.

Carpentry has 50 levels on Hypixel's curve and gives +1 Health a level (see [Skills overview](skills-overview)). It has no perk.

## Minions and collections

The first time you craft a minion tier it counts as a new **unique minion**, which brings you closer to another minion slot (see [Minions](minions)). Crafted items never count for [collections](collections): only what you gather does.

## Making gear better

Crafting makes the item; [reforging](reforging) improves it afterwards with the Blacksmith's reforges, the Recombobulator and Potato Books. Merchants sell the basic tools and ingredients (see [NPC shops](npc-shops)), and the [Bazaar](bazaar) sells the rest.
