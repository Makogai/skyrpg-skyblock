**Alchemy** is brewing potions at a **brewing stand**. Potions give you stats and other effects for a while, and brewing them levels the Alchemy skill (**Brewer**: every level makes your potions last 1% longer).

## Brewing

Place a **Brewing Stand** on your [island](profiles-and-islands) and right-click it: three bottle slots at the bottom, the ingredient on top. Every brew takes **20 seconds** and needs no fuel, and it keeps going with the stand closed.

1. **Water Bottles** + **Nether Wart** make **Awkward Potions**.
2. Awkward Potions + a **Brewing Ingredient** make that ingredient's potion, at level I, III or V.
3. Then **modifiers**, each its own brew:

| Modifier | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Glowstone Dust | +1 level |
| Enchanted Glowstone Dust | +2 levels |
| Enchanted Glowstone | +3 levels |
| Redstone | +8 minutes |
| Enchanted Redstone | +16 minutes |
| Enchanted Redstone Block | +40 minutes |
| Enchanted Redstone Lamp | +3 levels and +16 minutes (it counts as both) |
| Gunpowder | Splash potion, half the time |
| Enchanted Gunpowder | Splash potion, full time |

A potion takes one level modifier and one time modifier, up to its top level. Short potions (Healing, Stamina, Blindness, Damage) take no time modifier.

## Drinking

**Right-click** a potion to drink it. A **splash potion** gives its effect to every player within 8 blocks, you too. You get a "BUFF!" message, and **`/effects`** lists your effects with their time left.

- An effect lasts the potion's time, plus 1% per Alchemy level, plus 10%, 25% or 50% with a Potion Affinity Talisman, Ring or Artifact.
- A new potion of the same effect replaces it, unless it's a lower level.
- Effects count down while you're online and stay through death.

## Alchemy XP

Each brew adds its ingredient's **Alchemy XP** to the potion (Hypixel's numbers: Sugar 5, Enchanted Sugar 300, Enchanted Blaze Rod 23,000...), and you get it **when you take the potion out** of the stand.
